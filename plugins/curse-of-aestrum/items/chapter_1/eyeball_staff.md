## Description

A long, polished black wooden staff. The head is carved into a stone eyeball — white, faintly bloodshot from quartz-like veins, with a sheen around the iris suggesting moisture. A non-magical duplicate also exists. At campaign start the staff rests in the Trambeathen tomb in the keeping of the Runic Skeleton.

## Properties

- The eye's gaze is unsettling.
- The eye moves of its own accord, looking around its environment.
- The staff seemingly vibrates in the presence of the Runic Skeleton.
- Inert inside magical dead zones; active outside them.
- Once attuned, the wielder can summon bones within 100 feet (excluding bones inside living bodies or active undead) into a large Behemoth creature.
- **Effect on its wielder:** it always feels as though the staff is watching whoever holds it; looked at directly, the eye is gazing elsewhere. Only the attuned wielder feels watched — others nearby do not. The staff also feels as though it is slowly, persistently drawing something out of the one attuned to it — not painfully, but without letup.

## DM Notes

The stone eyeball is Lisandre Trambeathen's own eye, voluntarily removed and transmuted into the staff as a permanent scrying conduit. He watches through it at will.

Lisandre scrys through the eye at will and checks on it each morning, expecting the crypt. Should the staff ever be moved from the tomb, his morning scrying instead shows him whatever the eye now faces — and outside a dead zone he will study whoever holds it, flitting between them and any newcomer who approaches, working to understand who has the staff and how to return it to the Runic Skeleton. Whether he connects a given holder to the defeat of the Runic Skeleton or grasps what has become of his crypt guardianship is a matter for play, tracked on the relevant `saved/` overlay.
