## Description

A brass-cased instrument, small enough to sit in one palm. The right-side panel is worn smooth from extended thumb contact. A needle floats behind glass on the face. Obtained by Sage from Emanthur Wallerbridge in Setland.

## Mechanics

Requires overnight attunement (sleep with it while focusing on it). Once attuned, speak a surname aloud — the needle points to the closest living member of that family. Does not work on the dead. Does not indicate distance — only direction. The needle only reorients when the nearest living target changes — e.g., when the searcher moves far enough from one relative that a different, closer member of the same family takes over the heading.

**Behavior:** When drawn slowly side to side near a target, the needle pivots to continue tracking — confirming the target is the source rather than something beyond them.

**Will not register** Belmita as a Goodfeet blood relative if used to search that surname — mechanically exposing her identity theft.
**Will register** Delmuir Goodfeet (an actual clan member in Duskwall) as the nearest Goodfeet blood relative.
