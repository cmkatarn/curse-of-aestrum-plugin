## Description

At campaign start it lies on Elsinoor Trambeathen's remains in her mausoleum. Prevents its wearer from falling asleep — protecting against the Sleep effect of the Aestrum time loop. Once put on, the wearer cannot remove it. The skeletal guardians of the Trambeathen Crypt will pursue anyone who takes it, relentlessly, to retrieve it.

## Notable Property

- Worn through midnight, it lets its wearer stay conscious across the loop's reset and witness what is otherwise unobservable: living beings freeze in place, then teleport to their reset points.

## Conversion

The pendant's sleep-prevention essence can be extracted and bound to one or more new items. The irremovable property does not transfer. The pendant must first be removed — which is possible inside a dead zone. See [magical_item_conversion.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/magical_item_conversion.md).

## Clarification

The pendant is not cursed in the sense of carrying a magical affliction. Its two properties are: (1) prevents sleep, and (2) cannot be removed once worn. Neither is inherently harmful. Elsinoor's madness was the natural consequence of prolonged, total sleep deprivation — not a magical effect. Lisandre knew this and exploited it deliberately. Bari Khalid's manuscript describes the pendant as having "no actual curse," which is technically accurate. He does not know about the removal-prevention property.
