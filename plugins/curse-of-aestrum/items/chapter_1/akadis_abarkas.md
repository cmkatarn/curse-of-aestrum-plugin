## Description

A pair of magical footwear resembling Roman gladiator sandals. At campaign start they rest at the top of a giant tree in the Misty Forest, alongside a golden Caliconia flower. Extremely light, form-fitting enough to be worn inside other boots, radiates Transmutation magic.

## Mechanics

**Speed mechanic:** Grant the wearer **8× their normal movement speed** whenever they move beyond their base movement (>30 ft) in a single action. Normal walking (≤30 ft per action) is unaffected. When stopping after a high-speed move, the wearer must succeed on a **Dexterity saving throw** or lose control of the stop.

**Reference speeds** (wearer with 30 ft base movement): Single move = 240 ft / Dash = 480 ft / Triple move = 720 ft.

## Conversion

The Abarkas' speed multiplier can be extracted and distributed equally across up to 5 footwear items. The stopping save only applies to split items whose bonus exceeds +40 ft over base movement. See [magical_item_conversion.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/magical_item_conversion.md).
