## Description

A powerful magical tool utilized by The Shrikes during their infiltration of the Castle Vault. Belongs to the school of illusion. The mirror stores the physical appearance (not voice) of one individual at a time; anyone holding the mirror can then assume that stored appearance.

Used to disguise a team of Shrike operatives as identical members of the castle guard. After use during the vault heist, the mirror was handed off to a courier disguised as a pauper to be removed from the scene.

The mirror is a key tool used by Luca himself to conduct business under a disguise. Luca uses it to adopt a disguise whenever conducting business as "Luca" — in his true form he is heavily scarred and wanders Duskwall as a beggar. His voice is always his own regardless of the stored appearance.

Luca used an elderly-man Mirror disguise to purchase approximately 256 vials of alchemist's fire from Rylin T'Sarran at the waterfront — a separate disguise from his usual Luca persona, deliberately chosen to prevent the deal from being traced back to The Shrikes.
