## Overview

A divine prophecy from Selûne, revealed through Jiasha. The canonical delivery point is **the overnight stop at Jiasha's hut, before the party crosses into Aestrum** — while Jiasha can still hear her goddess. See [npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md) under "The Amber Crown Warning — Selûne's Gift, Mandatory Delivery" for full handling, including the fallback (divine intervention / temporary possession) if the party refuses the overnight stop.

**This quest is one of the most important in the campaign — especially for Chapter 2.** If the prophecy is never delivered, the Amber Crown thread is lost and Chapter 2's Amblecrown / Skaarsdaam architecture has no anchor. The mandatory-delivery framing in Jiasha's file exists to prevent this loss.

> *The daughter of Amber Crown, in her prime,*
> *Shall be lost to the dark, the shadow, the climb.*
> *By Shar's cold hand, she shall fall—*
> *A fate most cruel, that none can forestall.*
>
> *But heroes will come, from far and wide,*
> *To change her fate, and stem the tide.*
> *They shall save her—though not too soon,*
> *For the darkness grows in the waning moon.*
>
> *Find her, find her where shadows meet,*
> *In a place where light and darkness greet.*
> *Where steel on steel does ring through the air,*
> *In a place where a monster stalks the heir.*

## Interpretations

- "Daughter of Amber Crown" = Miri Amblecrown
- "Shar's cold hand" = Rowan Deckard, Shar cultist
- "A place where light and darkness greet" = the Shrine of Selûne desecrated by a Shar statue
- "Where steel on steel does ring" = The Hammered and Anvil (shares a forge wall with a blacksmith)
- "A monster stalks the heir" = Rowan Deckard

Jiasha will explain — when the party asks — that visions aren't perfect; things get misheard or lost in translation (e.g., "Amble Crown" vs. "Amber Crown"). The party is the prophecy's "heroes."
