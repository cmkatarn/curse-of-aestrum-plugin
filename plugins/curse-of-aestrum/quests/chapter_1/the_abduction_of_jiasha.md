## Overview

After the party crosses into Aestrum, [Mieke]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/mieke.md)'s Black Arrows-contracted mercenary band — pursuing the Galadiil bounty placed by [Quellenna Ilphekiir]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/quellenna_ilphekiir.md) (see [the_lions_den.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md)) — arrives at [Jiasha's hut]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/jiasha_hut.md), burns it, and takes Jiasha across the boundary as a guide. They are wrong about her usefulness (she does not know Aestrum), and once inside they camp on the [hunting trail in the Misty Forest]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/mieke_mercenary_camp.md). The loop snapshots them there. Jiasha becomes loop-bound and held captive at the camp.

The party discovers the burned hut when they try to leave Aestrum and find both the boundary sealed and the hut destroyed. They cannot reach Jiasha by following tracks (the loop erases physical evidence inside Aestrum). The path to her runs through [Aliss Perisdottir]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/aliss_perisdottir.md) and a divinatory breadcrumb trail.

## Triggering — Two Paths

### Timeline A — No road encounter

If the party does not encounter Mieke's band on the road (the [Lion's Den fallback encounter]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md) was not fired because at least one PC already learned of the bounty through the Lion's Den, Black Arrows ties, the contact-house, or inn rumor), the band crosses into Aestrum independently — **at least a full day after the party crosses**, ideally two or three. Sequence:

- The party spends the night at the hut, receives amulets (or refuses them; see [jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md)), and crosses into Aestrum the following morning. They begin to learn about the duchy.
- Mieke's band arrives at the hut at least a day later — exact timing flexible. They have been moving more cautiously than the party (no reason to rush, and the contract permits patience). They find Jiasha alone, recognize her as the local hermit who would know the region, and seize her as a guide. They burn the hut to deny pursuit and erase any record of their stop.
- They cross into Aestrum with Jiasha and continue east. The first night catches them somewhere off the maintained road — Mieke's call to camp off-route was a routine security measure, and the loop's snapshot fires before they understand what is happening. They wake the next morning on the hunting trail in the Misty Forest. That becomes their permanent reset point.

### Timeline B — Road encounter fired

If the party fights and defeats the road-ambush band (the [Lion's Den fallback encounter]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md) fired because no PC had surfaced the bounty by the time they reached the forested foothills), the chain runs differently:

- The road-ambush band is a small forward team — three to four operatives Mieke dispatched ahead to scout and skirmish any competing claimants. The party wipes them.
- A survivor (or the bodies, if pursued) makes it back to Mieke's main camp on the Setland side (a temporary forward base, not the eventual Misty Forest camp). Mieke regroups his main band — larger, hardier — and moves east himself for retribution and intel.
- Mieke's band arrives at Jiasha's hut after the party has moved on. They learn from the hut and from Jiasha herself that the party stopped here. They burn the hut, take Jiasha across the boundary to extract information about the party's identity and direction.
- They cross into Aestrum and follow the same sequence as Timeline A from there: first night off the road, snapshot fixes them on the Misty Forest hunting trail, camp becomes permanent.

In both timelines, the end state is identical: Jiasha is held at [Mieke's camp in the Misty Forest]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/mieke_mercenary_camp.md), the hut on the Setland side is burned to the ground, and the band is loop-bound.

## The Party Discovers the Hut Burned

The party encounters this scene when they attempt to leave Aestrum the way they came. Mechanics:

- **The boundary won't let them out.** They have snapshots (see [snapshot.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/snapshot.md) and [time_loop.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/time_loop.md)). The bubble pushes them back. They can see across to the Setland side but cannot cross.
- **Looking across, they see the burned hut.** It is 200 feet beyond the boundary — close enough to make out clearly. The walls have collapsed; the thatch is gone. Smoke does not rise anymore; the burn is days or weeks old. The clearing is empty. No body, no Jiasha.
- **No tracks lead anywhere they can follow.** The Aestrum-side road between the boundary and the 4WI was traveled by the band when they crossed with Jiasha — but those tracks have reset many times since. The Setland-side ground beyond the boundary holds whatever tracks the band left, but the party cannot reach it to read them. They are left with the certainty of what happened and no physical lead.

This is the intended discovery beat. Hold the moment. The party realizes they are sealed in, that their host's home was destroyed shortly after they left it, and that the person who gave them their amulets is missing. Do not race past it.

## The Path to Aliss

The party reaches Jiasha through [Aliss Perisdottir]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/aliss_perisdottir.md). The mechanism is layered:

### Step 1 — The Aliss rumor in Duskwall (and elsewhere)

Loop NPCs in Duskwall, the inns along the road, and Aestrum-side travelers carry a stable rumor about a fortune teller in the Misty Forest who helps people find lost things. The rumor is baked into the day's loop pattern — it predates the abduction and exists independent of it. **Surface it organically when the party is asking around** about missing people, the woods, the duchy's stranger inhabitants, or anything adjacent.

Sample carriers:

- **A Duskwall innkeeper or barmaid** who mentions, in passing, *"the fortune teller in the forest — Aliss something — finds things for people who lose them. She's expensive. Or free. Depends on when you go."*
- **A market patron** who tells a story of having lost a ring or a child or a relative and finding them through Aliss's reading. The story is the same every day in the loop, told to whoever is in earshot. The party can hear it at any time after Day 2.
- **A traveler on the road** who recommends her unprompted when the party mentions the Misty Forest. *"If you're going into the woods, see Aliss first. She'll tell you what you're walking into."*

The party does not need a check or special prompt. The rumor is in the air; they hear it the next time they ask about anything tangentially relevant.

### Step 2 — Visiting Aliss

The party travels to Aliss's house at the center of the Misty Forest via the maintained walking path off the 4WI–3WI road (see [routes.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/routes.md)). Standard travel; the walking path is navigable.

**Aliss's behavior depends on the hour:**

- **Early morning visit (before she has read her notes):** she is functionally not loop-aware. She will read for the party — for a fee — but the reading will be ordinary divination, not specific. She will not yet know who they are. If they describe the situation, she will tell them she sees *"a woman in the forest, restrained, waiting"* but cannot specify further. She may ask them to return after the morning.
- **Midday or afternoon visit (after she has read her notes):** she is loop-aware. The reading is on the house. She produces the scrap of paper that describes them — the standard recognition moment, see [aliss_perisdottir.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/aliss_perisdottir.md) DM Notes. After that beat lands, she pivots to Jiasha.

### Step 3 — The Vision

Aliss receives, mid-reading, a structured vision of Jiasha's location delivered as a sequence of three **signals** the party will encounter along the route. The vision is Selûne speaking through Aliss's gift — though Aliss does not name it that way; she experiences it as a particularly clear divination.

The signals are anchored to three points along the path:

- **Signal 1** — at the four-way crossroads (4WI).
- **Signal 2** — at or near the entry to the Misty Forest Hunting Trail (the spur east off the 4WI–Rockwood road).
- **Signal 3** — close to the camp.

**Default canonical signals** (DM may substitute equivalents to fit table tone):

| Signal | Location | Image |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At 4WI, on or near the milestone marker | A pure white raven perched and unmoving, watching the road. White ravens do not occur in this region; the bird does not flee when approached. |
| 2 | At the hunting-trail spur entry, on a low branch arching over the path | A torn strip of cloth knotted around the branch, the color of the dress Jiasha was wearing the night the party last saw her. The knot is deliberate; the cloth is fresh. |
| 3 | Roughly fifty yards before the camp clearing | A single moonstone, palm-sized, resting in a small natural depression in a fallen log. The stone is unworked and not native to this forest. It catches what little light reaches the canopy. |

**The vision is delivered in Aliss's full theatrical register.** Her diction sharpens; the cards turn slowly; she speaks each image aloud as it forms. She tells the party plainly: *"You will see these things in this order. When you see the third, she is close. Move quietly from there."* She does not explain what the signals mean or who placed them. She does not know. The vision arrived; she delivers it; the party acts on it.

### Step 4 — The Signals Manifest

As the party travels, the signals appear at the predicted locations. Each one validates the previous and builds confidence:

- **Signal 1 at 4WI.** The raven is exactly where Aliss said it would be. It watches them. It does not fly. When they leave the crossroads, it remains.
- **Signal 2 at the hunting-trail entry.** The torn cloth is unmistakable — they recognize it from their evening at the hut. Someone tied this here recently and someone has been here since they left Jiasha's clearing.
- **Signal 3 near the camp.** The moonstone is unambiguously a sign; it does not belong here. They pocket it or leave it. Either way, the camp is within sound.

**Run each signal as a small beat.** Do not race past them. The vision is paying off in real time; let the players feel the prediction land. By the third signal, they should be moving with the deliberate hush of people who know they are about to find what they came for.

**If the party deviates from the route**, the signals do not appear at the wrong locations. They are bound to the path Aliss saw. A party that bypasses 4WI and tries to reach the camp another way (cross-country through the forest, by air, from the dead-zone direction) does not see Signal 1; they do not get the validation. The path is the prediction. The DM may surface a single signal out-of-sequence if the players need a nudge, but the canonical mechanism rewards taking Aliss's route.

## The Rescue

See [mieke_mercenary_camp.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/mieke_mercenary_camp.md) for layout, sightlines, and the band's positions. Approach options:

- **Direct combat** — viable for a party of 4–5 at appropriate level; tune Mieke's band size per the encounter notes there.
- **Stealth infiltration** — the dead-zone-side approach is the cleanest; the band avoids the dead zone and the party can stage from there.
- **Negotiation** — Mieke is responsive to operational information. A party that demonstrates the loop in language he can verify within his day can shift him from hostile to confused. Hard to do convincingly inside one day; rare path but available.
- **The dead-zone permanent-kill mechanic** — see [mieke.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/mieke.md). The party can permanently end the camp by moving Mieke and key band members into the Misty Forest dead zone before midnight. Same mechanic that ends Rowan.

After Jiasha is freed:

- **She is exhausted but cooperative.** She walks where the party leads. She does not speak much on the trail back. She accepts food and water.
- **She is loop-bound at the camp.** The band's path crossed her first non-dead-zone midnight onto the hunting trail inside Aestrum, and her snapshot fixed her there per [snapshot.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/snapshot.md). Her reset point is the game trail; she wakes there each midnight disoriented and captive with no memory of how she arrived (see [jiasha.md:142-145]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md)). Rescuing her removes her from the camp but does NOT remove her from the loop. Unless she sleeps in a dead zone or wears a Moon Amulet, every midnight she will be Teleported back to the game trail and revert to the captive-Jiasha snapshot state.
- **Housing her after the rescue is therefore urgent.** Two viable options:
  - **Move her into a dead zone before midnight.** The Deckard Estate is the most accessible and the party's likely base. A PC who sleeps with her in the dead zone keeps her inside the antimagic field through the reset, and she is not affected. This is the standard option.
  - **Give her a Moon Amulet.** She did not make one for herself ([jiasha.md:154]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md)). If the party can spare one (a PC who refused theirs, an extra commissioned through Thalamin Perisdottir, an amulet recovered or improvised), it would preserve her memory regardless of where she sleeps. The party may realize this on their own; surface the option if they don't.
- **If the party fails to house her, the reset undoes the rescue.** She wakes the next morning at the camp, captive again. The band, also reset, holds her again. The rescue becomes Sisyphean. This is the bleak version; the party that figures out the housing problem on the night of the rescue gets the clean outcome.

## DM Notes

- **The Aliss path is the canonical solution.** The amulets were considered as a directional-pull mechanism and explicitly rejected during design — they retain their canonical function (memory protection only). The rescue route runs through Aliss, the rumor, and the three-signal vision.
- **The rumor must surface organically.** Do not have an NPC walk up to the party and announce "you should see Aliss." The rumor lives in the loop's ambient texture; the party hears it when they engage with the duchy. If by Day 5–6 they still have not surfaced it, escalate gently — have an NPC overtly recommend her in response to a related question, or have a fuzzball-friction encounter surface her name.
- **The vision is reliable but not omniscient.** Aliss can see *where* Jiasha is and *what signals will validate the path*. She cannot see the band's numbers, Mieke's identity, or the tactical situation at the camp. The party should still scout. The vision delivers them to the door, not into the room.
- **If Rowan has been displaced before the rescue,** the camp is in danger. Aliss's vision will reflect this — she will deliver the signals with urgency, and may add a fourth: *"Smoke that is not from a cookfire. Hurry."* The party arriving at a camp Rowan has slaughtered finds a different scene than one they expected; Jiasha may be alive but injured, or dead, depending on how late they are. This is the bleaker branch; let it land if it has been earned.
- **Aliss does not accompany the rescue.** She has been waiting for the party to be cycle-breakers; she does not see herself as a combat asset. She will offer to come if the party explicitly asks and the situation seems to warrant it (per her [joining hook]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/aliss_perisdottir.md) DM note), but the default is she stays at her house and the party returns to her with news.
- **The post-rescue beat is Jiasha's recovery.** She has been held for days, sleeping bound, suppressed from Selûne, witness to professionals who treated her as failed equipment. Hold the recovery quietly. She is not broken; she is tired. The party that takes her seriously through this beat earns her trust permanently.

## Design Note — why the path runs through Aliss

A physical trail was considered as the route to Jiasha and **rejected**: tracks and
drag-marks cannot persist across the loop's resets, so no party can follow spoor to
the camp. The Aliss route above — the ambient rumor, the reading, the three signals —
exists to solve that, and is the only canonical mechanism. Do not reintroduce a
followable trail.

The Runic Skeleton belongs to the Dunleaven Deadery and is a single entity; it is not
stationed at the camp. If it appears near the rescue it is because something drew it
out of the crypt (see [dunleaven.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/dunleaven.md) — an
unclosed magic-immune door lets it range and track), not because a second one exists.
