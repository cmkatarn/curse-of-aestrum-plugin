## Overview

Notes in the Dundelver Estate describe a temple in the Nahamkate Desert visited by Darwinnith the First. A map of its general location lies on his desk. Journal entries suggest he has been increasingly intent on returning before he dies; he claims to have "the key" but can't remember where he put it. (The key is the Mounted Ruby, in a locked box elsewhere on the estate.)

The temple sits in a box canyon deep in the Nahamkate Desert, marked by smoke from everlasting torches. A quarter-mile off the main path lies the Nahamkate Desert Dead Zone (magical charring marks the boundary; antimagic field within). The temple is an ancient structure dedicated to the Yuan-Ti deity Sathraza. Inside, a group of Yuan-Ti led by the priestess Assaneela (claiming to excavate religious relics of Ssath'razza) holds the upper chambers. A puzzle room requires specific gemstones placed on pedestals; the Mounted Ruby solves it. A hidden chamber contains the large tangerine quartz flame relic. A rear tunnel exits past a mummy and several traps.

## DM Notes

Assaneela has been trapped in the Nahamkate Temple for years — the desert falls within Aestrum's region and is subject to the loop. She is not a recent arrival. Her research suggested relics of Ssath'razza were in the temple, but when she arrived she found the chamber stripped. She cooperated with the party solely because they possessed the Mounted Ruby, which was required to open the puzzle room door she needed to pass through.

The Yuan-Ti are not intended to resurface as a significant faction — they were a set-piece encounter.
