## Overview

Duke Malak du Leon of Setland tasked the group with retrieving 10 years of unpaid taxes from Aestrum. His goal: fund an expanded military to seize Nortmunde's vacant throne following the death of its king — with Aestrum's subjugation as the first strategic step before turning on the other duchies.

## Lifecycle

- **Trigger:** Quellenna / Malak give the party the Aestrum tax-collection mandate.
- **Complete:** The party hands vault contents (any amount) to a Setland
  representative — most plausibly Aidra Calemthor — in discharge of the mandate.
- **Fail:** The party explicitly declines to hand any vault contents to a
  Setland representative.

One of the three Ducats quests (Flock, Duchy, Party) may resolve as `completed`
per campaign instance — see [ducats_for_the_party.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/ducats_for_the_party.md)
for the trilemma matrix and shared badge unlock.

## DM Notes

Malak has no awareness of Shar's involvement in Aestrum or anywhere else. His interest is purely political and financial. He would never lead from the front — he sent Aidra Calemthor to command the invasion.

If the party fails to discharge the mandate before Malak loses patience, the Setland army crosses Aestrum's border and encamps at the base of the road to Setland — sealing the road each morning at the new loop snapshot position. Sage's standing bounty in Setland remains relevant if the party later attempts to negotiate passage.
