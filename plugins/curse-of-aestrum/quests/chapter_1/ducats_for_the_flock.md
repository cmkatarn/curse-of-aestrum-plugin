## Overview

A quest forced upon the party by Luca of The Shrikes — ostensibly to infiltrate the Duskwall Castle vault and steal its contents. The true objective was the assassination of Duke Evandur Tallwood, with the heist as cover.

## Lifecycle

- **Trigger:** Luca assigns the heist to the party.
- **Complete:** The party hands the vault contents they took (on any day) to Luca.
- **Fail:** The party explicitly declines to hand the contents to Luca.

One of the three Ducats quests (Flock, Duchy, Party) may resolve as `completed`
per campaign instance — see [ducats_for_the_party.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/ducats_for_the_party.md)
for the trilemma matrix and shared badge unlock.

## DM Notes

The vault remains an infinite wealth source: valuables stolen and moved to a dead zone remain there permanently, while the vault's contents reset each morning. The party is likely to run the maze repeatedly. Haladon's team resets before the heist each day — any given maze run risks crossing paths with the Shrike team mid-operation.

Galadiil Ilphekiir provided the magical key and bronze vault token to the party, believing she was facilitating a gang robbery. She did not know the true objective was assassination. This is Shar's Ledger Secret on her — burning it at the right moment severs two relationships simultaneously.
