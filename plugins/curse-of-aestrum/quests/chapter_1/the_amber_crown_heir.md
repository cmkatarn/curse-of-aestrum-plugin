## Overview

Activates when the party identifies Miri Amblecrown as the subject of the
Amber Crown Prophecy. The prophecy's lines, interpreted against known events
in Aestrum, point unambiguously to her. Jiasha can help — visions aren't
perfect, and "Amble Crown" vs. "Amber Crown" is the kind of slippage she's
seen before.

## DM Notes

Miri was traveling through Aestrum on her return from a diplomatic mission to Cormyr — attempting to secure military backing ahead of the Nortmunde succession crisis. Her father Duke Amblecrown of Skaarsdaam sent her to create a two-front deterrent against Setland. She was caught when the loop began.

Duke Amblecrown has believed his daughter missing for ten years, and **does not know where she vanished.** Her last word home was that she was homeward bound; then nothing. Aestrum sits on one plausible route back to Skaarsdaam, but not the only one, and no search has ever had a place to begin. He was never deterred from looking — the duchies were at peace until the King died, and envoys could have gone wherever he sent them. He simply had nowhere in particular to send them, which is why the treasury drained into covert searching rather than a single expedition.

Skaarsdaam's wizards have since identified the magical wave passing nightly over Nortmunde, which gives him a suspicion about Aestrum and no proof of anything about Miri; the two remain adjacent guesses. Cormyr has been waiting on a pact that was never delivered.

Chapter 2 hook: Duke Amblecrown, the undelivered Cormyr pact, and Skaarsdaam's military situation all await resolution.
