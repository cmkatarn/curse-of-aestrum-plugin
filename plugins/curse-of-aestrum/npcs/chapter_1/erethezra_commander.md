## Overview

Operational head of [Erethezra]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/erethezra.md). She has run
the organization against the Black Arrows for **over a decade without once being
identified**, and has largely taken apart the Black Arrows' former dominance in the
Setland region in that time.

She is **not a field operative.** She is a commander, and should read as someone who
has been playing a longer game than anyone else in the room.

**Her anonymity is canon, not a gap.** Erethezra takes its name from the Elven word
for "ghost," and members are typically confirmed only after death; the commander
having no known face is the organization working as designed. That is a separate fact
from this sheet being a stub — see *Open Slots*.

## Behavioral Profile

- **Does not meet people by accident.** Any encounter that feels like a coincidence
  was a choice she made.
- **Responds to demonstrated competence** — results she can verify, not reputation or
  bravado.
- **Will not align with any succession candidate on principle.** Erethezra's alignment
  is to Erethezra's interests.
- **Toward Black Arrow members, including ex-members: not a personal enemy.** She is
  the competition. They built something; she has been systematically taking it apart.
  Business.
- **Will not move against a party without cause.** Cause means actively working
  against Erethezra's interests — not merely operating in the same space.
- **Curious rather than threatened** by new entrants.

## In a Succession Crisis

Treats it as opportunity and moves assets accordingly. Her standard early move is a
general recall to operatives — *"ghosts go home"* or equivalent — embedded in songs
and shanties carried by unwitting performers, repositioning her people ahead of the
political conflict. See the signal-language section of the faction file.

If she learns through her own channels that an outsider has decoded an Erethezra
signal, she **files it silently and adjusts her assessment of them upward** without
ever acknowledging it.
