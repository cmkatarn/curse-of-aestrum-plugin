## Overview

Captain of a Black Arrows-contracted mercenary band sent from Setland into Aestrum to claim the Galadiil bounty placed by Quellenna Ilphekiir (see [the_lions_den.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md) and [factions/chapter_1/black_arrow.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/black_arrow.md)). His band is the one the party encounters at the camp in the Misty Forest, where Jiasha is held captive (see [the_abduction_of_jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/the_abduction_of_jiasha.md)).

He has been inside Aestrum since shortly after the party crossed the boundary. His contract is now structurally impossible — the target is a moving figure inside a loop he cannot understand, and the guide he took to navigate the duchy turned out not to know the duchy at all — but he does not have the cross-day memory to realize it. He runs the contract again every day, hits the same wall every day, and resets.

## Appearance

Mid-forties. Broad-shouldered, weather-worn, the build of someone who fought for a living before he led for a living. Dark hair cut short and going grey at the temples. A neat dark beard. Hard pale eyes that read people before they read the room. The lower-right leg is gone below the knee, replaced by a wood-and-iron prosthetic that he wears as comfortably as a boot — the wound is old and he has long since stopped favoring it. He moves with a steady three-beat gait that he has made fluent.

Practical leather armor reinforced with steel plate at the chest and shoulders. A longsword at his hip and a shortbow slung across his back. A small silver pin shaped like a black-headed arrow on his collar — Black Arrows mark, worn openly inside Setland and tucked away in mixed company.

## Background

A Setland-born professional. Came up through the Black Arrows in his twenties, worked his way to captain over fifteen years of contract work — escort, assassination, recovery, the full menu of what the Arrows offered the duchy and its private clients. Lost the leg on a job in the Skaarsdaam border country, refused the disability stipend, and was back in the field inside six months. The Arrows respect him; the duchy respects him; he respects neither in particular. He works for pay and for the satisfaction of completed jobs.

The Galadiil contract was unusual — high payment, eye-and-hand-with-ring proof, a Charnelhold writ moving quietly through the standard channels. Mieke took it because the money was good and because contracts that specific tend to be paid out cleanly. He selected a small band, supplied them himself, and rode east. He had no reason to expect anything stranger than a difficult crossing and a difficult target.

He did not know about the loop. No one in Setland told him about the loop. The Arrows briefing was the Arrows briefing: target, location, proof, payment.

## Behavioral Profile

- **Professional, not cruel.** Mieke does not enjoy violence; he uses it. Captives are kept alive if they have operational value and killed cleanly if they do not. Jiasha is alive because she might still know something useful, and because killing her with no payoff would be a waste of a resource he might still figure out how to use. He does not torture her.
- **Bad-tempered at the failing mission.** Every day he wakes with the same growing frustration that something about this duchy is wrong, the same suspicion that his guide is useless, the same anger that the contract is not closing. He carries it across the day but cannot carry it across the reset.
- **Loyal to his band.** He brought them. He is responsible for them. He does not abandon his own. This is the lever the party can use if a negotiation ever opens.
- **Reads the party as competition.** When the party arrives, his first read is *Charnelhold sent more than one team and didn't tell us.* This is the conclusion he reaches every time he encounters them across the loop. He acts on it accordingly — hostile but not reckless; he wants to know who they are before he decides whether to fight or talk.

## Speech Pattern

Setland-accented Common, plain and direct. Soldier's diction — short sentences, no padding, names for things rather than descriptions. He does not raise his voice. He does not threaten unless he is about to act. When he asks a question, he asks it once.

*"You're not Charnelhold's. So who sent you."*
*"The cleric stays where she is. Step back."*
*"I'm not interested in your story. I'm interested in why you're on this trail."*

## Combat Profile

*Fighter (Battle Master) 6 — capable mid-tier opponent; the band's anchor.*

**Stats:** STR 16 | DEX 12 | CON 14 | INT 11 | WIS 13 | CHA 10
**HP:** 58 | **AC:** 17 (plate-reinforced leather + Defense fighting style) | **Initiative:** +1 | **Speed:** 25 ft (prosthetic) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3
**Saving Throws:** STR +6, CON +5
**Skills:** Athletics +6, Insight +4, Intimidation +3, Perception +4, Survival +4
**Languages:** Common, Thieves' Cant

**Class Features:**
- **Second Wind** (1/short rest): bonus action, regain 1d10+6 HP
- **Action Surge** (1/short rest): one extra action on his turn
- **Combat Superiority:** 5 superiority dice (d8). Maneuvers: **Disarming Attack**, **Riposte**, **Trip Attack**, **Commander's Strike** (directs an ally's attack as bonus action)
- **Know Your Enemy:** one minute of observation reveals two of the target's STR/DEX/CON, AC, HP, class levels
- **Extra Attack:** two attacks per Attack action

**Equipment:**
- **Longsword** (+6 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing) — primary weapon, used one-handed with shield
- **Shortbow** (+4 to hit, 1d6+1 piercing, range 80/320) — for opening exchanges
- **Heavy steel shield** (+2 AC, included in the 17 above)
- Plate-reinforced leather, light helm worn in combat
- Black Arrows pin (concealed in combat)
- A folded copy of the bounty writ in a sealed oilcloth on his person — same text as the road-encounter writ in [the_lions_den.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md), but signed and dated

**Combat behavior:** Mieke leads from the center of his line. He opens with a longbow exchange if range permits, closes to longsword work, and uses Commander's Strike to coordinate the band's focus on the most dangerous-looking PC. Trip Attack on a heavy-armor fighter; Disarming Attack on an obvious caster; Riposte saved for when a melee attacker overcommits. Second Wind goes early if he takes a clean opening hit. Action Surge goes when he sees the fight tipping — usually on his second or third turn against a competent party. He does not pursue if the party retreats with Jiasha; he holds the camp and the line.

## The Band

Tune to party level. As a baseline (Chapter 1, party of 4–5):

- **Three to five Black-Arrows operatives** — Scout statistics with longbows, shortswords, leather armor and Black Arrows pins. Skirmishers; flank and snipe.
- **One lieutenant** — Veteran statistics with longsword, shortbow, and chain mail. Mieke's second; takes command if Mieke goes down or is occupied.
- **Optional reinforcement** for parties of 5 or harder difficulty: a small group of hired or summoned undead, tuned to party level. This is a difficulty dial only — the Runic Skeleton is a single entity anchored to the Dunleaven Deadery and is **not** part of this encounter.

The full band fits in the camp without crowding (see [mieke_mercenary_camp.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/mieke_mercenary_camp.md)).

## Cycle Behavior

- Resets each midnight to the camp baseline. Wakes at 6 AM with his snapshot self.
- The amputated leg is part of the snapshot — wakes with the prosthetic on, leg gone as it was on the night he crossed into Aestrum.
- The band reconstitutes around him at the camp every morning. Any band members killed during the day are restored at 1:30 AM at the camp.
- **Permanent kill option:** the same rule that applies to Rowan (see [misty_forest.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/misty_forest.md) DM Notes) applies to Mieke and his band — a body in a dead zone at midnight cannot be Recreated. The Misty Forest dead zone is roughly 100 yards from the camp via the Shar shrine. The party can permanently kill Mieke or any band member by moving their corpse into the dead zone before midnight. This is the same mechanic that ends Rowan; the party that figures it out for one threat figures it out for both.
- **Without the dead-zone treatment, the camp resets and the rescue resets with it.** Jiasha's snapshot is the game trail itself — she is loop-bound here ([jiasha.md:142-145]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md)). At the next midnight after a rescue, Teleport returns her to the camp and Revert restores the captive-Jiasha snapshot state unless the party has either (a) housed her inside a dead zone for that midnight, or (b) given her a Moon Amulet. See [the_abduction_of_jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/the_abduction_of_jiasha.md) for the post-rescue housing problem.

## DM Notes

- **Mieke is loop-bound but not cycle-aware.** Do not let him reason across days. Each day he hits the same wall, has the same suspicions, makes the same conclusions, and never carries them forward. This is the bleak texture of the camp: a professional who is good at his work, trapped in a job he cannot finish, every day, forever.
- **He is not a villain in the moral sense.** He is a mercenary captain with a contract. The party can fight him, kill him, or — given the right approach — negotiate with him. Mieke responds to information he can act on; a party that opens with "we're not Charnelhold's" gets a different conversation than one that opens with arrows.
- **Negotiation handles:** the contract is structurally impossible (he cannot exit Aestrum to collect, and his guide cannot guide). A party who explains the loop in language he can verify within his own day's experience — show him something that resets, point at the cycle — may shift him from hostile to confused-and-curious. This is hard to do convincingly inside one day; most parties will fight.
- **The bounty writ on his person is a loot item.** If the party defeats him and searches the body, the writ is there. Same text as the road-encounter version. A party that took the road-encounter version (timeline B) finds the same writ here — confirmation of the chain. A party that did not take the road version (timeline A) finds it here for the first time. Either way, this is the bounty's hard-evidence touchpoint.
- **He does not know Quellenna placed the contract.** Black Arrows commissioners are not named on writs. He knows the contract came through a Charnelhold contact. A party that asks him who hired him will get exactly that — *"Charnelhold contact, standard channel. Names are not on the writ."*
- **He believes Jiasha is from Aestrum.** He doesn't know she's a Setland-side hermit; his band grabbed her near the boundary and crossed her before any of them realized the duchy worked differently. To him, she's "the local woman who lied about knowing the area." This misunderstanding is part of why he keeps her — he's still trying to make her be the guide he thought he was taking.
- **Roleplay register:** plain, hard, professional. Do not perform villainy. Mieke is the kind of NPC who makes the encounter feel like a job interview gone wrong, not a moral confrontation. The horror, if any, is the structural one: a man who is very good at his work, ruined by a duchy he cannot understand.
