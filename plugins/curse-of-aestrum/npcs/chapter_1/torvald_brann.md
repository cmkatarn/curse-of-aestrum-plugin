## Overview

Bosun of *The Sapphire Serpent*, and [Zephyra]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/party/zephyra_stormchaser.md)'s
boarding partner. Where she goes over a rail, he goes over it a half-step behind and to
her left, and has done for long enough that neither of them discusses it.

## Why He Matters

**He is not unsettled by the biting.** Zephyra's sword is a deflection instrument; the
bite is the goal, and she fights to close distance until her jaws can reach. Most people
who see that up close need a moment afterward. Torvald has seen it from arm's length,
repeatedly, and treats it the way he treats any other thing that works — he positions
for it. Their shorthand on a boarding deck is mostly silence and one or two words.

That is worth holding onto because it is **the only relationship in her life that
predates the expedition**, and the only place her fighting has never had to be explained.

## Behavioral Profile

- **Bosun first.** He thinks in rigging, watches, and stores. The ship's condition is
  his standing anxiety, and the current repairs are personally insulting to him.
- **Dry, brief, and hard to alarm.** He is the crewman who says the short true thing
  while everyone else is still deciding whether to panic.
