## Overview

Captain of *The Sapphire Serpent*, a privateer sailing under letter of marque and
hunting pirates for the bounty on them. Currently moored in the **Bay of Basteen** in
Setland, waiting out roughly two tendays of rigging repairs earned by running down a
pirate ship harder than the rigging could stand.

She is the reason [Zephyra]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/party/zephyra_stormchaser.md) has a berth. **Setland
is xenophobic and Zephyra is a dragonborn** — Vosk's view is that a crew is judged by
what it lands, not by what it looks like walking up the gangway, and she has said so
in port to people who needed to hear it.

## Behavioral Profile

- **Keeps a clean ledger and expects the same.** A privateer who takes what the
  commission allows and nothing beyond it. The line between privateer and pirate is a
  paper one, and she is fastidious about which side of the paper she is on.
- **Hard, and fair in that order.** She does not soften a judgment to be liked, and she
  does not revisit one to seem consistent.
- **One standing rule about prisoners** — they arrive alive and reasonably whole,
  because that is what the bounty pays for and because it is the rule. She has had to
  say this to Zephyra more than once, in a tone that did not need repeating a third
  time.
