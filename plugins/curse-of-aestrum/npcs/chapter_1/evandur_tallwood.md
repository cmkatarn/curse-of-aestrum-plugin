## Overview

The Duke of Aestrum. Organized a funeral at sea for his father Lognan aboard the royal ship *The Survivor*, then quietly disembarked via dinghy before ordering the castle's siege ballistae to destroy the vessel — killing his brothers Lucretius, Jaugr, and Malston in a single calculated act. He was 24 years old at the time (1477 DR). Loop-frozen since 1480 at his actual age of 27 — his face has not changed in ten years.

## Appearance

Healthy build. Jaw-length wavy dark brown hair. Stubble that appears at all times of day — it grows back immediately after shaving, as though his face refuses the pretense of softness. Wears a grey and black doublet with fine silver embroidery. His expression is fixed at serious; he does not smile.

## Background

His paranoia regarding potential heirs is an inherited family trait; his grandfather's succession crisis involved 13 siblings murdering one another. He was originally betrothed to Quellenna Ilphekiir, but the death of her sister's arranged prince bumped Galadiil into the arrangement instead. He has been observed insisting Duchess Galadiil drink her tea before leaving the lunch table — the party suspects this is Hylea's Mead, a known contraceptive. Galadiil is fully aware and powerless to refuse. She has experienced several miscarriages as a result.

He is now hearing whispers of an assassination plot. A name has surfaced: Luca. He does not know whether this is the same Luca he thought he killed — Lucretius — or a coincidence. He turns the question over. He wonders what Luca would say if he were the one holding the knife. He knows exactly what he himself would say: *"Do it."*

The guilt is not redemptive. It has not made him gentler or more hesitant. It lives in him as a fixed cost — fully paid, still owed.

## Behavioral Profile

- Calculated, patient, ruthless — the man who orchestrated the deaths of his siblings from a dinghy with a clear view of the result. He does not act impulsively.
- His prevention of an heir is deliberate. His reasons are not yet known to the party, but they are rooted in the same logic that put him in that dinghy: he will not create another generation of this.
- His paranoia about succession is bone-deep. He killed to stop the cycle. He has never stopped watching for it to restart.
- Will not react immediately to party actions. When he does act, it will be thorough.
- Does not perform calm. He simply is calm — or something that functions identically to calm from the outside. A successful Insight check reads not serenity but total self-governance: nothing gets out that he has not decided to show.

## DM Notes

- His entire life has been shaped by the Tallwood cycle of violence. He did not choose to participate in it. He chose to end it, by becoming its last act. When Lognan died, Evandur had a clear-eyed read on what came next: his siblings had been watching him the same way he had been watching them. He acted first. He has hated himself ever since.
- Evandur has no knowledge of any assassination plot — not Luca's, not the Setland expedition's. He has never heard the name "Luca." He dies each night in the 11:30 PM ambush and wakes each morning with no memory of it.
- His own wife facilitated the vault heist out of spite, believing it was purely a robbery — she did not know the Shrikes' true objective.
- Explosion of *The Survivor*: 1477 DR, Evandur age 24. Dead: Lognan (the father, whose body was the cover), Lucretius, Jaugr, Malston.
