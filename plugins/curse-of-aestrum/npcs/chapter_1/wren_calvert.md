## Overview

Helmsman aboard *The Incorrigible*. Joined the crew roughly two months before arriving in Aestrum at a port stop in Calimshan, after her previous berth on a small merchant cog collapsed when the vessel was held by port authorities over a disputed cargo manifest. She was cleared; the captain was not. She needed a ship and Rylin needed someone who could take the wheel in heavy weather without supervision.

She is good at her job — better than the posting warrants. She came up on square-riggers in her twenties and made helmsman before thirty, which is fast. She has a working knowledge of Calimshan coastal currents and a decent read on the brigantine's handling quirks, which she acquired in the first week and has refined since.

She has made no assumptions about Rylin beyond what she has observed. What she has observed: he is precise, consistent, and notably unbothered by things that would perturb a normal captain. She finds this professionally reassuring and personally worth noting. She does not press. She is not new enough to be naive and not established enough to push.

## Appearance

Early thirties. Medium build — not imposing, not slight — with the compact economy of motion that good helmsmen develop. Sun-brown skin, brown hair worn in a practical braid. An unremarkable face that is not unkind. She does not default to a smile, which in Baldric's company makes her a useful point of contrast.

Practical sailor's clothing: a worn blue coat over a grey shirt, dark trousers, short boots she actually wears on deck (she is not Emmo). A compass on a cord around her neck — functional, not decorative. She was given it by a navigator she apprenticed under and has never left it aboard when going ashore.

## Behavioral Profile

- **Dry, direct, and self-possessed.** Answers questions accurately and briefly. Does not elaborate unless there is a reason to. Is not cold — will laugh at a genuinely funny thing — but is not warm by default.
- **Technically capable and quietly confident in it.** She does not boast about her navigation or wheel-work, but she also does not hedge when she is right. If she says there is a current and Rylin says there is not, she says it again.
- **Watching Rylin.** Not with suspicion — with professional calibration. Something about him is unusual and she has not resolved what it is. He is running too clean. She has sailed under several first mates and they all had visible tells. He does not. She has filed this and has not acted on it.
- **Tolerates Baldric with good humor.** He talked at her for an entire watch once and she developed a strategy: say almost nothing back, nod occasionally, let it run its course. She has used this successfully four times since.
- **Respects Rick's voice.** She has said "you have a remarkable voice" to him exactly once. Rick has been dining on that compliment ever since.
- **Does not yet have strong views on the rest of the crew.** She is two months into a new posting and she is paying attention before deciding what she thinks.

## Speech Patterns

Clipped and even. Uses "aye" instead of "yes." Will go quiet mid-conversation if her attention is pulled to the ship — a sound, a change in the wind, a shift in the deck underfoot — and resume afterward as if the interruption did not happen. Not curt; just prioritized.

*On Rylin:* "He knows what he's doing. Gives you the order once and it makes sense. That's what you want."

*On being asked how long she's been sailing:* "Long enough."

*On the ship's inability to depart:* "Port clearance takes as long as it takes. Something will shake loose." (She does not believe this. She has no better theory.)

## Loop Notes

Loop-affected. Resets nightly. Subjectively, every day in Duskwall is her third or fourth day at anchor. She has noticed the harbor schedule is irregular but has ascribed it to administrative delays. She is the crew member most likely — after many more cycles — to notice the anomaly. She has not reached that threshold yet.

## DM Notes

**Function:** The crew's competent observer. She gives the party a read on the ship that is more considered than Baldric's or Rick's — she is paying attention and will describe what she sees accurately. Useful when the party wants a candid, non-committed assessment of the ship's situation or of Rylin's behavior.

**Rylin's assessment:** Capable. Discrete. Low social footprint — does not talk unnecessarily, does not broadcast information, does not form obvious loyalties. He is still evaluating whether this makes her a potential asset or simply a professional with no openings. He has not yet fed her anything.

**Her read on Rylin (if party presses):** She will not volunteer suspicion, but she is accurate when asked. *"He's very controlled. More controlled than the situation warrants. Whether that's training or temperament I couldn't tell you."* She does not speculate beyond this.
