## Overview

Topman aboard *The Incorrigible*. Joined the crew in Calimshan approximately three months before arriving in Aestrum, replacing two hands who quietly withdrew when the captain confirmed the nature of the cargo run. Emmo did not ask what they were replacing them on. He needed a berth; the pay was fair; the first mate seemed like a man who ran a tight ship. That was enough.

He is the crew member responsible for the brigantine's square-rigged foremast — setting and furling the square sails in heavy weather, going aloft when the situation demands speed rather than caution. He does this without visible apprehension and without visible enthusiasm. He does it because it is his job.

He has been a sailor for over twenty years across a half-dozen Calimshan merchant outfits. He has sailed under three captains who died in service and does not consider this statistically unusual. He considers Rylin a competent officer — precise, fair, does not give an order twice. That is all Emmo requires of a captain. He harbors no personal warmth toward the man and expects none in return.

## Appearance

Late forties. Heavily built for a topman — dense through the shoulders and arms from years aloft — with a face that has been hit by sun and wind until the lines are set in permanently. Dark olive skin, black hair gone substantially grey, cropped short. A scar along the left jaw from a line-snap he does not discuss. Habitually expressionless in the way that working sailors sometimes are: not unfriendly, just default-blank.

Work clothes: a practical canvas jacket over a grey linen shirt, rough trousers, bare feet on deck. He owns a pair of boots and wears them ashore. On deck he does not bother.

## Behavioral Profile

- **Quiet by habit, not temperament.** He will answer questions directly and without evasion. He simply does not ask them himself. He takes no pleasure in small talk and will not fill silence.
- **Competent and reliable.** Goes where he is told, does what is asked, does not cut corners on the rigging. Rylin has noticed. No comment has been made in either direction.
- **New enough to have no opinions yet.** Three months is not long enough to have grievances or loyalties aboard a working ship. He finds Baldric's chatter mildly grating but not worth addressing. He finds Rick's shore-leave privileges slightly unusual but not his business.
- **Will defend the ship.** He is not a violent man but he is a large one, and if the ship is threatened he will commit to it without instruction.
- **No awareness of Rylin's intelligence work.** He is too new and too uninterested in the ship's politics for Rylin to have made any use of him. He is a rigger. He rigs.

## Loop Notes

Loop-affected. Resets nightly. Subjectively, every day in Duskwall is his second or third day at this anchorage. The inability to depart puzzles him mildly. He has chalked it up to port clearance delays and has not pressed the first mate on it.

## DM Notes

**Function:** Background texture for the voyage. He is a credible working sailor who makes the brigantine feel properly crewed. Useful for conveying that the ship functions as a ship — Rylin gives him a quiet order, he carries it out, the sail sets or the line holds.

**Rylin's assessment:** Useful. Discreet by temperament. Not yet evaluated as an intelligence asset — three months is not enough time and Emmo shows no obvious exploitable qualities. He is not a channel, not a source, not yet a liability. He is currently just a rigger, which is what Rylin hired him to be.
