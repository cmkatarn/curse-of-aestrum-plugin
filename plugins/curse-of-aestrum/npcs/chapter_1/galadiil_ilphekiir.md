## Overview

The Duchess of Aestrum, wife of Duke Evandur Tallwood, older sister to Quellenna Ilphekiir. Originally arranged to marry a prince to the north before his death from illness; subsequently substituted into Evandur's marriage contract in place of her younger sister. Working with Luca out of spite — her sole objective was to embarrass and damage Evandur's reputation. She believed she was facilitating a gang robbery.

Met the party in the royal garden as their contact for the heist, providing them with the magical key and bronze vault token.

## Appearance

Appears 22. Half-elf. Biologically 34 — she was 24 at loop entry (1480 DR) and has not aged since. Her younger sister Quellenna now appears older than her, which is noticeable to anyone who knew them both before the loop.

## Background

Galadiil is fully aware that Evandur is forcing her to drink Hylea's Mead tea as a contraceptive. She is powerless to refuse — the Duke waits until she has drunk it before allowing her to leave the table. She has experienced several miscarriages as a result.

She does not know Luca is Lucretius Tallwood. She arrived in Aestrum after *The Survivor* incident and never met the younger Tallwood brothers.

**The party's suspicion is wrong.** They believe she intended or knew of the assassination. She did not. She was not capable of knowingly participating in a murder plot, and it never occurred to her that granting vault access to criminals would also grant access to the castle above. She thought in terms of embarrassment and theft, and never extended the logic to violence. Her naivety is the operative factor, not manipulation by Luca.

## Behavioral Profile

- Knows she was substituted into the marriage for convenience. She is not confused about her situation.
- The worst part of her situation is not the marriage itself — it is that she privately wishes Quellenna had ended up here instead. This is a guilty wish she has never spoken aloud.
- Is actively working against Evandur, but does not understand that the heist she facilitated was cover for assassination.
- Does not know Luca is Lucretius Tallwood.
- Trusts the party to the degree that she provided vault access. This trust has limits she has not yet been tested on.

## The Ilphekiir Ring

A family heirloom she never wears publicly — Evandur would not permit a visible symbol of her pre-Aestrum identity. Kept hidden in her chambers. It predates everything: the displacement, the marriage, Quellenna's resentment. It is from a time when she and her sister were simply sisters, before either of them became a political instrument.

She cannot articulate precisely why she will not leave without it. Only that leaving it behind would feel like agreeing that the woman who wore it no longer exists.

**Chapter 2 significance:** Quellenna's death bounty on Galadiil requires the right eye and the right hand — the hand must bear this ring as proof of identification. The ring alone is not sufficient to claim the bounty. However, a player who possesses the ring or accurately describes it to Quellenna is immediately credible as someone who has genuinely encountered Galadiil. It is proof of meeting, not proof of death — but Quellenna would treat it as confirmation that the bounty is executable.

**Party-NPC writ seeding.** If the campaign was assembled with two or more random NPC party slots, one of those NPCs will have been secretly seeded with this writ during party creation, with downstream spread-and-betrayal mechanics. See [overrides/create-party.md § Random-Slot Seeding of the Galadiil Bounty]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/overrides/create-party.md#random-slot-seeding-of-the-galadiil-bounty).

**What Galadiil knows:** She does not know about the bounty or that the ring has been named as identification. She knows only that it is hers and that it is the last thing in her possession that belongs entirely to who she was before Aestrum.

## Limits

Politically aware, not operationally trained. She arranged the vault plot out of spite and expected criminals to handle the mechanics. She does not know how criminal operations work, has no tradecraft instincts, and will not improvise as though she does. She passes the key and token directly — there is no chain, no passphrase, no intermediary she has established. A stranger appearing in her garden is alarming, not a protocol matter to be managed smoothly. She does not know what the items do; she will describe them only as things Evandur keeps hidden in connection with the passage beneath the castle.

## DM Notes

- She does not know about the Setland expedition or any other plot against Evandur. When the 11:30 PM castle ambush occurs each night, she flees into the bowels of the castle — her memory resets with everyone else at midnight.
- The party witnessed Evandur requiring her to finish her tea before she was permitted to leave the lunch table.
- **Campaign vault attempts:** The Shrikes are ill-equipped to navigate the vault maze independently even with Galadiil's assistance. The party managed to get the Shrikes into the castle on one occasion. On a second attempt, the party interrupted a Shrike operation to place Alchemist's Fire in the maze — and were involved in detonating it, destroying the castle. Galadiil has no retained memory of either event.
- **Ledger Secret:** Luca framed the entire vault operation as a heist. Galadiil does not know the true objective was her husband's assassination. She actively assisted a murder plot against the Duke — unwittingly, but that distinction may not matter to her when she learns it. Shar holds this as a potential lever: burning it at the right moment severs two relationships (between the party and both Luca and Galadiil) simultaneously.
- Her younger sister Quellenna now appears older than her due to the loop — a detail noticeable to anyone who knew them both before 1480.

## Post-Loop Arc — "The Gift to Charnelhold"

*This is the canonical default arc if the loop breaks, the Setland main army (Aidra's force) marches on Duskwall, and the party does not intervene in Galadiil's situation. Replace or modify as needed when party action alters the conditions.*

**Phase 1 — Surface.** When the loop breaks, Galadiil's pattern-correction fuzzball dissolves and she surfaces into full unfiltered awareness in a single rush: the Hylea's Mead, the miscarriages, the vault plot that was cover for Evandur's assassination, ten years of repeated daily horror compressed into one moment of comprehension. She does not perform composure. She has spent ten years calibrated to being a hostage of her own life; she does not unlearn that posture in an hour. Evandur is dead. The castle is half-destroyed. The ring is on her finger.

**Phase 2 — Capture.** Aidra Calemthor, securing Duskwall as senior Setland commander, interviews Galadiil as the surviving Duchess. Galadiil tells her everything she now knows. Aidra recognizes the name *Ilphekiir* and makes an independent judgment to bring Galadiil to Charnelhold — strategic (no unguarded Aestrum noble for rival claimants to weaponize) and personal (this is the unsaid wound in Quellenna's interior life, and Aidra is returning home from war). Aidra does not know about the bounty. Galadiil is handled as a high-value prisoner — escorted, supervised, not cruel. See [aidra_calemthor.md] post-loop DM notes.

**Phase 3 — The sisters' conversation.** Quellenna receives her in private audience. Galadiil does not apologize for the substitution — it was not hers to apologize for. She says aloud, for the first time, that she has spent ten years secretly wishing it had been Quellenna in that chair, and that she is sick with relief her wish was not granted. She tells Quellenna what Aestrum actually was. The marriage Quellenna was bumped out of was not a stolen kingdom; it was a slow execution. See [conversation_states/galadiil_quellenna_charnelhold.md].

**Phase 4 — Quellenna's decisions.** Quellenna does not renounce Shar. She does three things: revokes the bounty through her original Charnelhold contact (cancellation routes back through the Black Arrows; the contract goes cold); places Galadiil under indefinite house arrest in a private interior apartment in Charnelhold — not the Lower Levels, no bones at eye level — for the duration of the succession war; and visits, not warmly, not often, but enough.

**Bounty cancellation.** The Black Arrows treat the cancellation as a courtesy to the principal. The ring is no longer a working death warrant. Galadiil does not know the bounty existed or that it has been revoked. See [factions/black_arrow.md].

**What Galadiil knows post-arc.** Full memory of Aestrum (unfuzzed). She was captured by Setland soldiers under Aidra Calemthor's personal escort, transported to Charnelhold, presented to her sister. She does not know about the bounty (placed or revoked), Shar's role in Aestrum, or that Aidra is leveraged against Quellenna. She knows her sister visits her sometimes. She still wears the ring. She does not know what comes after the war.

**Her interior baseline post-arc.** Not broken. Not grateful. Calibrated — the ten years of hostage-posture do not dissolve with the fuzzball. She has the rest of a life to figure out, and no practice doing so. The ring on her finger predates everything; it is the only continuous thread she has to a self she has not been allowed to be.

**Alternative downstream beat (held, not committed):** Post-succession-war, if Malak loses or Shar's hold on Quellenna further erodes, Galadiil may be released — to attempt a return to Aestrum's ruin, to find Ilphekiir family elsewhere, or to remain near Quellenna and Aidra into whatever post-war life Aidra has been hoping for. Not decided here.
