## The Time Loop

Aestrum is trapped in a magical daily cycle. Dead zones are immune to the reset. The party retains memories due to Moon Amulets and/or sheltering in dead zones. The current in-game year is **1490**; the cycle began in approximately **1480**, so NPC residents believe it is still that year.

**External imperceptibility (canon — do not violate).** From the outside, Aestrum is indistinguishable from any ordinary duchy. Anyone approaching from beyond the border notices **nothing** out of the ordinary in the land itself — no visible boundary, no atmospheric "wrongness," no sensory tell of any kind that an outsider can catch. The loop does not announce itself at the border or anywhere a normal observer can perceive it. The **only** external sign is social rather than magical: Aestrum's residents tend not to remember outside visitors from one trip to the next, because the daily Modify Memory reset wipes those encounters. The suppression is perceptible only to someone attuned to *what* is being suppressed — e.g., a devotee of Selûne registers the **absence of the goddess's voice** inside the border, where Her reach is cut off. Ordinary senses register nothing. When narrating an approach to or crossing of Aestrum's border, the land must look, sound, and feel completely ordinary.

**Fixed population (canon — do not violate).** The people inside Aestrum are the *same* people it held when the loop locked, roughly ten years ago (~1480): the born residents and every trader, traveller, and visitor who happened to be within the border at that moment, all trapped together and reliving the one day. No one has left in the ten years since — the boundary holds them, and the nightly reset re-seats them where it found them — and effectively no one new arrives (a fresh outsider crossing in is a once-in-a-decade anomaly; the party is exactly that). The standing population therefore does **not** shrink, grow, or turn over; it is a full day's-worth of people, frozen in place. **Never render Duskwall — or any Aestrum settlement — as depopulated, sparse, or emptied.** These are normally, fully peopled places. Their wrongness is not absence but *stasis and forgetting*: a full, living, busy town that runs the identical day forever, with no new faces and no flux, its crowd at the same tasks it held ten years ago, none of them remembering the strangers who move among them. That is eerier than empty streets — and it is the correct texture. (The decade of silence outsiders perceive — no taxes, trade, or correspondence leaving — is the *outward* face of the same fact: nothing, and no one, comes back out. See Political Situation.)

**True origin (DM only):** Shar created the loop deliberately — a precision divine trap constructed around a single moment of opportunity when Miri Amblecrown passed through Aestrum. What Shar gained:

1. **Trapping Selûne's Chosen (Miri):** Stranded in a region where Selûne's influence could be suppressed and Shar's agent (Rowan Deckard) could murder her repeatedly — preventing the prophecy from unfolding.
2. **Trapping Galadiil Ilphekiir:** Galadiil is Quellenna's sister. Keeping her isolated in Aestrum removes a potentially complicating variable from Quellenna's political work in Setland and Nortmunde.
3. **Silencing a Selûne-devoted region:** Aestrum historically worshipped Selûne above all other deities. The loop suppresses that devotion — residents cycle endlessly through days without spiritual growth, memory, or connection to their goddess.
4. **Seizing control of Nortmunde:** Shar's ultimate goal is to install a puppet ruler over Nortmunde through Quellenna Ilphekiir. With the king dead and Aestrum neutralized, Setland's aggressive rise under Malak — steered by Quellenna — becomes the vehicle for that takeover.

**What the party knows:** They know Shar is involved — the desecrated shrine, Rowan Deckard, and her direct confrontation in the Antechamber all point to her. They do not yet know she *created* the loop, nor that the loop was engineered around Miri's presence.

## The Dead Zones — Origin

Created by the Acolypyrrhic Battles (~1280 DR), when multi-deity warfare concentrated massive magical energies across Aestrum. A god directly intervened at what is now the Deckard Estate / Rowan clan land, quashing all magic — creating the permanent dead zone there. Locations of battles correspond to dead zones across the region.

Three major dead zones documented on the Salient Cartographer's map:
- **The Deckard Estate** — largest, site of the divine intervention; the party's base
- **Nahamkate Desert area** — where the Nahamkate Temple is located
- **Misty Forest dead zone** — where the Shar effigy shrine is located; Rowan shelters here nightly to preserve his memory

Numerous minor dead zone pockets are scattered throughout Aestrum. Mechanical behavior is codified in [dead_zones.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/dead_zones.md).

## Political Situation

Aestrum is an isolated duchy within the kingdom of Nortmunde, bordered exclusively by Setland — all roads in or out pass through Setland territory.

The political situation has two phases hinged on the King's death (**Day 0**; see [the regional timeline]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/timelines/chapter_1/nortmunde_regional.md)). Day 0 is a **movable anchor**: the world stays in the *Before Day 0* state for the entire span the party is inside the loop, and the DM fires the King's death only near the cycle-break endgame.

### Before Day 0 (campaign start, Day −14 onward — unchanged until the King dies)

- **The King of Nortmunde is alive**, aging and in visible decline that his court reads as natural age. The throne is occupied; there is **no succession crisis** and the duchies are nominally at peace. *(DM only: the decline is engineered — Drethnal is slow-poisoning the King with Greymist. Succession has not been triggered.)*
- **Aestrum has been silent for roughly ten years** — no taxes, no correspondence, no trade have left the duchy since the loop locked. The mechanism is unknown to outsiders; the place simply "went quiet." This decade of silence is the standing fact the Nortmunde side reacts to.
- **Setland / Duke Malak du Leon:** on Day −14 Malak dispatches a contracted party (the player party) to recover Aestrum's decade of unpaid taxes and tribute — the **inciting job and its stated, public purpose.** The kingdom is on a peaceful footing; the road into and out of Aestrum is **open**; no army has moved. *(DM only: the same day, Malak begins secret invasion planning — the tax expedition doubles as the pretext. None of it is public or yet in the field.)*
- **The party's frame:** hired into a stable kingdom under a living king for a straightforward-looking — if daunting — tax-recovery contract on Nortmunde's behalf. They do not know the King will die, that an invasion is being planned, or what Aestrum's silence actually is.

### Starting on Day 0 (the King's death — the succession crisis the party returns to)

The state the world has moved into by the time the party emerges from the loop. The party is **not** meant to learn of any of it until Aestrum is invaded.

**The succession crisis:** The King of Nortmunde is dead with no heir, triggering a crisis among the duchies. Duke Malak du Leon of Setland is the most aggressive claimant, marching on Aestrum to secure his flank before turning military force on the other duchies (Skaarsdaam, Mikaelvad, Dalihad). The Setland army has crossed inside Aestrum's border; the road out is sealed.

**Shar's long game (DM only):** Shar is running two simultaneous operations:
1. **The Aestrum suppression** — the time loop, the desecrated shrine, Rowan's murder cycle, Jiasha's disconnection. Her primary focus in the region.
2. **The Nortmunde long game** — installing a puppet king through Quellenna Ilphekiir. Quellenna has been shown a vision: Malak as King of Nortmunde, herself as his Queen, Aidra Calemthor as Maîtresse-en-titre. She is working toward it with full conviction. Malak believes he is pursuing Nortmunde for his own ambitions. Neither has the full picture.

## Divine Conflict

Shar is actively suppressing Selûne's presence in Aestrum:
- The Selûne shrine in Duskwall has been desecrated — Shar's statue installed, Hati Heldrivver running it as a Shar shrine
- Rowan Deckard is a fanatical Shar cultist murdering Selûne's Chosen nightly
- Jiasha is cut off from her goddess inside Aestrum
- The Amber Crown Prophecy tasks the party with saving Miri Amblecrown — Selûne's instrument for breaking the suppression

**The loop anchor mechanic and breaking-the-loop conditions are codified in [loop_anchors.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/loop_anchors.md)**, including the qualifying-prayer rule, the two anchor shrines, the current prayer sources, and the two viable approaches for breaking the loop.

**Quellenna's role (DM only):** Shar's Nortmunde acolyte. Does not know the full scope of Shar's operations — she is a tool, not a knowing architect. Her psychological vulnerability (displacement by her sister Galadiil, resentment of her reassignment to Setland) made her susceptible. She believes she is pursuing her destiny.
