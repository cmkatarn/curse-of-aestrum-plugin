## Overview

Mid-valley market town in the western Duchy of Mikaelvad, several days' travel from Mireval along the duchy's secondary trade roads. Tannmark sits at the meeting of three upper valleys and serves as the trade hub for the hill hamlets above it — Nebelhof and similar small settlements that have no markets of their own. Roughly six hundred residents; one functional market square, two inns, three smiths, a tannery on the lower river, a small temple to Chauntea that doubles as the town's only formal place of public worship.

The fog is less dense here than further up the valley but never wholly absent. Mornings begin gray and clear by midday; in late autumn and winter, the gray does not lift at all.

## What's Here

- **The Market Square.** Central, paved in old uneven stone, with permanent stalls along the north and east edges and itinerant traders setting up between them on the two formal market days each tenday. Goats, sheep, wool, leather, grain, cheese, root crops. Cattle are bought and sold on the south edge, in a separate pen-yard with its own scale and tax-collector.
- **The Two Inns.** *The Stone Goat* — the older, used by the hill villagers and the farmers from the upper valleys; rough, cheap, beer-and-stew. *The Lantern* — the newer, used by traders from Mireval and the few travelers passing through to other duchies; cleaner, more expensive, employs a cook who can produce something resembling a city meal.
- **The Tannery.** On the river below the town's lower edge. The smell sets the town's western perimeter; no one builds homes within easy range of it.
- **Chauntea's Hall.** Small, single-priest, run by an elderly cleric whose chief work is overseeing the harvest blessings and presiding over weddings, naming days, and funerals. No vampire-or-undead-detecting capacity worth speaking of; the priest is a kind man with grain knowledge and a steady prayer.
- **The Cattle Yard.** Separate from the main market square. Permanent fencing, a weigh-scale, a tax-collector's booth — where the upper-valley hamlets come to buy and sell stock.

## Tannmark and the Upper Hamlets

The relationship between Tannmark and the upper hamlets is one-directional: the hamlets come down to Tannmark for what they cannot make, and Tannmark does not go up to the hamlets at all. A hill villager arriving at the cattle yard with coin is a known and unremarkable figure; a Tannmark merchant going up the trails is not a figure that exists. Supply trips here are anonymous in the way they are anonymous for any hill villager — recognized by face after enough visits, never investigated.

## A Quiet Town's Blind Spots

Tannmark is the kind of place where a careful traveler passes unremarked, and where the occasional quiet wrongness goes unexamined — useful to know if a scene ever turns on what the town did or didn't notice. *The Lantern*, where Mireval traders and through-travelers congregate, is where a stranger is most *seen*; *The Stone Goat* and the upper-valley crowd, least. Whatever a town like this fails to look at can sometimes be reconstructed after the fact through inn ledgers and witness memory — when a scene calls for it.

## What Is Not Here

- **No high-tier clergy.** Chauntea's resident priest cannot detect undead or distinguish a dhampir from a tired traveler. Higher capacity does not exist in Tannmark.
- **No Shar pipeline presence.** Like Nebelhof, Tannmark is below the threshold of relevance to the duchy-wide Shar operation. Holst's network does not run here.
- **No standing duchy garrison.** The town is policed by a small constabulary — four constables and a captain — concerned with theft, drunkenness, and the occasional knife fight at *The Stone Goat*. They do not investigate disappearances that occur outside town limits.
