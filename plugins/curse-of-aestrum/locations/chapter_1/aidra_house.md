## Overview

The private residence of [Aidra Calemthor]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/aidra_calemthor.md),
Commander of Setland's forces — **outside Charnelhold's walls, on the Setland City
side, sited against the shared wall.**

That siting is the whole reason the house matters. It is close enough to the castle
that [Quellenna Ilphekiir]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/quellenna_ilphekiir.md) could have a
hundred-yard tunnel driven under the wall from her own quarters to its cellar — and
she did, *after* Aidra took up residence, because Aidra's home was the destination
worth building toward.

**It is reachable from the city, not from the castle.** A party investigating the
residence approaches from the Setland City side.

## Who Is Here — and When

**At campaign start this is Aidra's anchor.** She lives here, and it is where she is
found when she is not on castle grounds. Her sheet places her here.

**That changes on Nortmunde Day 0.** The King dies, Malak's invasion order comes, and
Aidra moves to [Aestrum Field Command]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/aestrum_field_command.md) — first staging on
the Setland side, then across the border. From that day the house stands **empty**, and
a party entering finds it quiet and unoccupied.

| | Aidra | The house |
|---|---|---|
| **Campaign start → Day 0** | anchored here | occupied |
| **Day 0 onward** | anchored at the Field Command | **empty** |

The house is hers throughout. Which of those two states is live depends entirely on
where the campaign's outside clock sits — check it before rendering the building, and
see the Field Command's DM notes on reading that clock from inside the loop.

**Post-arc she comes home.** Her sheet's post-arc disposition returns her to this
house, with Quellenna still arriving each night through the tunnel.

## The Cellar

The [escape tunnel]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/charnelhold_escape_tunnel.md) surfaces here. From this end it is
**accessible without a key** — the concealment is entirely at Quellenna's end, behind
the furniture in her quarters that staff have never been permitted to move.

A party coming up through the cellar surfaces inside the house and must either
navigate out to the street or continue back down the tunnel to reach Quellenna's
quarters. **Discovering the tunnel from this end reveals Quellenna's quarters as its
destination**; it does not reveal anything else about her.

## What the House Is For

Quellenna comes here **every night.** She locks her door, applies a touch of cinnamon
perfume, and walks the tunnel. She considers Charnelhold a prison and has never been
permitted to leave it; this house is the other half of her life, and the tunnel was
never only an escape route.

**Aidra has never gone the other way.** She has never entered the tunnel from her end
and has never been inside Quellenna's chambers. Quellenna always comes to her.

If Quellenna's escape plan ever fires, it ends here: perfume on, the same as every
night, and she walks the tunnel taking nothing. She is not fleeing. She is going home.

## DM Notes

**The campaign does not lead the party here.** Aidra is introduced professionally and
publicly on castle grounds — the General crossing a courtyard, a servant's passing
reference — and her house is deliberately not on the campaign's surface. **If a player
investigates it anyway, let them**: asking around the city, following her after court,
scouting the wall. The information is discoverable and the campaign does not pretend
otherwise.

**But the campaign has teeth.** Scaling the shared wall or breaking into a Setland
general's home is exactly what Charnelhold's security exists to answer — guards, the
Lower Levels, and death for the egregious or the repeat offender. See
[setland_city.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/setland_city.md).

**If Aidra has returned, the cellar is a trap of a different kind.** Anyone surfacing
there who is not Quellenna meets an Aidra who immediately assumes the worst — that
something has happened to Quellenna, and that the people coming up out of her floor
are responsible. **She does not pause to ask.** Defusing it means either explaining
the tunnel (which raises its own question of how they know) or restraining her long
enough to establish they mean Quellenna no harm. That encounter fires **only** if she
has come back — that is, before Day 0 or after her return. While she is away with the Field Command it does not.

## Open Slots — To Develop in Play

- **[layout]** The house above the cellar — rooms, floors, how a party gets from the
  cellar stair to the street. Nothing establishes it.
- **[appearance]** What it looks like from the lane, and how it sits against the wall.
- **[relation]** Whether anyone keeps it while she is away — staff, a neighbour, no
  one. Unestablished.
- **[item]** Aidra keeps a pair of Quellenna's embroidered court gloves here, left
  behind one night and never retrieved. She carries one whenever she leaves. Where
  the other one sits in this house is not established, and it is the single most
  identifying object in the building.
