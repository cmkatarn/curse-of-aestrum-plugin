## Overview

A small homestead cabin set well off the road, perhaps a quarter mile east of Dunleaven across a stretch of long grass and a low rise. From the village proper the cabin is hard to see — the rise hides most of the structure, and a stand of trees breaks up the rest. Only the chimney smoke gives it away on cold mornings, and only to someone looking.

The cabin itself is plain and well kept: a single room with a sleeping loft, a porch facing the rising sun, a small painter's easel set up near the rail when the weather allows, a vegetable garden along the south wall, a hen coop, a goat pen, and a small stable that has held a horse for as long as anyone in Dunleaven can remember. The yard is swept. The fence is mended. The whole place has the quiet, deliberate competence of someone who has been keeping it up for a very long time without complaint.

## Discovery

The cabin is **not visible from the road or the well** to a casual observer. It is gated behind:

- **Passive Perception 16+** while in or moving through Dunleaven, OR
- **Active searching of the surroundings** ("what else is out here?", looking from elevation, climbing the church or a tree, scouting the perimeter), OR
- **Direct inquiry** with a Dunleaven resident about the cabin specifically ("is anyone else out here?", "whose smoke is that?", "what's east of the village?")

Dunleaven residents know about Dolores. They will not volunteer the information unprompted. Asked directly, they answer plainly — *"Dolores lives out east. Quiet woman. Keeps to herself. She's all right."* — and move on. No one in the village treats her as a curiosity; she has been here long enough that she is simply part of the landscape.

## What's Here

- **The cabin** — single room, sleeping loft, hearth, a long plank table, a rocker by the fire, a painted tin lantern, an oil painting hanging above the mantel that Dolores will not discuss.
- **The porch** — easel and a small stool. Half-finished landscapes in oils, all of them looking east toward the horizon at different times of day.
- **The garden and animals** — sufficient to feed her with surplus. She trades eggs and goat's milk in Dunleaven occasionally; this is how the village knows her.
- **The stable** — a buckskin mare named Trotter. Saddle and tack maintained. Dolores rides her in long slow loops around the property and rarely much farther.
- **Above the door, inside** — a hand crossbow on two pegs. Polished. Loaded. Belonged to her father.

## DM Notes

- The cabin is a quiet, optional space. Most parties will never find it. That is the intended outcome — its discovery is a perception reward, not a quest beat.
- A party that does find it gets a meeting with Dolores ([dolores.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/dolores.md)) and nothing else by default. There is no hook here unless the party makes one.
- The painting above the mantel is of a young couple standing in front of a different cabin, in a different country, in a softer light. Dolores does not know who they are. She believes she does — she will name them if asked — but the names do not match anything in Aestrum's records, and the landscape behind them is not Faerûn. This is for the DM to hold. Dolores cannot answer questions she does not know to ask herself.
- The hand crossbow is not decorative. See Dolores's combat profile.
