## Overview

A short passage — roughly a hundred yards — running from Quellenna's quarters beneath the shared wall between Charnelhold and Setland City, terminating in the cellar of Aidra Calemthor's house on the city side. It is not on any official floor plan. Quellenna had it built after Aidra took up residence — Aidra's home, sited against the shared wall, was the destination worth building toward. She uses it every night.

Aidra has never entered the tunnel from her end. She has never been inside Quellenna's chambers. Quellenna always comes to her.

## The Two Ends

**Quellenna's end:** A concealed entrance built into the wall behind a heavy piece of furniture in her quarters — the piece she has never permitted staff to move. The entrance is not visible without moving the furniture. Each night, she locks her door, applies a touch of cinnamon perfume, and walks the tunnel.

**Aidra's end:** The tunnel exits into the cellar of Aidra's house outside the castle walls. Accessible from this end without a key. A party entering from Aidra's cellar surfaces inside the house and must navigate to the street, or continue through the tunnel to reach Quellenna's quarters.

## DM Notes

**The tunnel as infiltration vector:** A party that knows the tunnel exists can approach Charnelhold from outside through Aidra's house — quieter than a frontal approach. Anyone surfacing in Aidra's cellar who is not Quellenna will be met with an Aidra who immediately assumes the worst: that something has happened to Quellenna, and that the people emerging from her floor are responsible. She does not pause to ask. Defusing this requires either explaining the tunnel's existence (raising its own questions about how they know) or restraining her long enough to establish they mean no harm to Quellenna.

**Note — Aidra currently absent:** Aidra is commanding Setland's forces in Aestrum. Her house is unoccupied. A party entering from her cellar during this period finds a quiet, empty house. The Aidra encounter fires only if she has returned. See `locations/saved/charnelhold.md`.

**Quellenna's escape plan:** Activated only if her Shar connection is exposed or Malak's candidacy collapses beyond recovery. Cinnamon perfume goes on — the same as every night — and she walks the tunnel. She does not take anything. She is not fleeing. She is going home, for the last time.

**One-way knowledge:** Discovering the tunnel from Quellenna's end reveals Aidra's house as the exit. Discovering it from Aidra's end reveals Quellenna's quarters as the destination. Neither end reveals the other until the tunnel is traversed.
