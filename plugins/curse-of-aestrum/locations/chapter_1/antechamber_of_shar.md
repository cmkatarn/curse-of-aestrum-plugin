## Arrival

The world goes dark. Seconds pass. The subject cannot move, cannot speak, cannot breathe. The seconds become minutes, then hours. Eons pass in motionless float between the stars.

Then forms appear in the blackness. What seemed an eternal nothingness slowly resolves into a defined space — finite distance becomes walls, and yet nothing about this space is right. It is fluid, and stretched, all wrong angles. Tendrils extend from one wall until they make contact with and are absorbed by another. Smokeless black flames flit from the ceiling. Humanoid forms strain against the mucous-like edges of the space before they relent and disappear.

Ink-black eyes blink from the shadows.

Then: a purple glow grows in intensity — a circle, located on the singular flat surface to be found among the walls. This is the presence of Shar.

## Shar's Presence

She does not manifest in humanoid form. She is the disc — a purple circle of light on the only flat wall, watching. Without moving, it looks into the very being of each subject present.

There is no sound in this space. Yet each subject hears the words of a god thunder within their skull, with silence as the only reprieve between words.

## Physical Effects

Shar exercises precise, surgical violence here. When she chooses to demonstrate capability, each subject is suspended spread-eagle — invisible force, no contact, no ceremony. Dark tendrils extend outward from the body in all directions as the bones split lengthwise and the marrow is drawn from within. The subject's mouth opens in a scream that produces no sound. This is not a killing blow. It is a memo. It ends when she chooses, and she chooses exactly when the point has been made.

## Exit

When Shar dismisses the subjects, the darkness fades and each subject feels a sensation of falling. Darkness gives way to blinding light. They find themselves in their body again, collapsing to the ground at their point of entry.

**On exit, all subjects make the following saves (in order).** Effects escalate with each visit — Shar's patience is not infinite, and the escalation is architectural, not emotional.

| Visit | DEX DC | Fail: fall + dmg | CON DC | Fail: vomit + dmg | All subjects |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 14 | fall, 1 dmg | 16 | vomit | −10 movement |
| 3 | 14 | fall, 1d6 dmg | 18 | vomit + 1d4 dmg | −15 movement, 1 exhaustion |
| 4 | 14 | fall, 2d6 dmg | 20 | vomit + 1d6 dmg | −20 movement, 2 exhaustion |
| 5+ | 14 | fall, 3d6 dmg | auto-fail | vomit + 2d6 dmg | −30 movement, 3 exhaustion |

DEX DC stays flat — a subject's ability to catch themselves may genuinely improve. CON DC and damage escalate. Movement penalty and exhaustion worsen regardless of saves.

## DM Notes

- The "eons" of floating are subjective — actual elapsed time at the shrine is seconds. The compression of perceived time is part of the experience.
- Shar communicates exclusively through the disc. She does not move, does not approach, does not gesture. She watches and she speaks inside skulls.
- The humanoid forms straining at the walls are not explained and should not be explained. They are available as horror texture.
- The bone-splitting pain is real within the antechamber and leaves no physical mark when subjects return. The memory of it is real, and a subject pulled in alone carries it with no witness to corroborate it.
- Transport to the Antechamber is triggered by destroying the Shar statue in the shrine. The statue resets each midnight, making this a repeatable trigger.
- This transport represents the limit of Shar's direct capacity. While managing the cycle, pulling subjects into her antechamber is the most she can do to them directly.
- The Void room in the Castle Vault maze is **not** the Antechamber — it *is* a pocket of Shar's actual realm. A subject who physically enters the Void room enters Shar's domain itself; Antechamber visitors do not.
