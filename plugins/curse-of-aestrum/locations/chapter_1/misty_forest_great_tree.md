## Overview

A single massive tree in the Misty Forest's southern interior — significantly older and larger than any tree around it, in a way that does not look natural. The canopy extends far beyond what its trunk would suggest. Orange flowers grow at the base in a formation too regular to be accidental. The clearing around it is unnaturally still.

The tree predates the forest. What it marks, or what it was before it became this, has not been established.

## Contents

**Stripe:** A Gargantuan Spider inhabiting the clearing. The spider territory of the eastern forest is an extension of its claimed range — Stripe is the reason that territory exists. The clearing is its anchor point. See `{{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/remaining_npcs.md` for Stripe's entry.

**Akadi's Abarkas:** Found at the base of the tree. Connection to the tree's origin not established.

**The Caliconia Flower:** Found at the base of the tree alongside the Abarkas.

## DM Notes

**Discovery trigger — the Kossuth relic:** The Great Tree's clearing cannot be stumbled upon. The orange beam from the Kossuth relic leads the party directly to it; without the relic, travelers moving through this area find only forest. The tree is not hidden — it is simply not findable without the right instrument.

**Stripe's territory:** The eastern spider infestation extends from this clearing. Stripe is the origin point. Defeating Stripe does not immediately dissolve the infestation, but removes its organizing intelligence.
