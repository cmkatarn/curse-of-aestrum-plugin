## Overview

The seat of the Kingdom of Nortmunde, and the city the Regent holds while the throne
stands empty. **The capital carries the same name as the kingdom** — one very large,
land-locked city at the geographical center of Nortmunde, with the five duchies
radiating outward from it, each bordering two others (see
[standalone_locations.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/standalone_locations.md), *Nortmunde
(Kingdom)*). The party has not been here.

The shared name is why this file is `loc_nortmunde_capital` and the kingdom is
`loc_nortmunde` — two entities, one name, distinguished by id.

## Who Holds It

**Tarwick Grale**, Regent of Nortmunde — tasked with executing the previous king's
standing rules and mandates until a new king is crowned, and carrying no real
political power of his own. See his roster entry
([remaining_npcs.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/remaining_npcs.md)); what he is hiding is
Chapter 2 material and lives in the
[chapter-2 overlay]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_2/remaining_npcs.md).

## Known Connections

- **Corva** was born here, third daughter of a merchant family comfortable enough to
  stop thinking about money and start thinking about appearances — the household
  whose managed silence around her grandmother's death shaped her. See
  [corva.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/corva.md).
