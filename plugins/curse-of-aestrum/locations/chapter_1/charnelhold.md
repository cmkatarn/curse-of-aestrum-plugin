## Campaign Opening — Path to the Lion's Den

**This is step 2 of the opening sequence (see [setland_city.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/setland_city.md)).** When the party arrives, the path through the castle is clear and the staff is expecting them:

1. **Approach via the Lion's Gate** — the internal gate in the shared wall. Two guards check names against the day's audience list; any PC who heard the criers and presents themselves for the expedition is on it.
2. **Through the outer courtyard** — an attendant signals them onward; they are not led, but the path is obvious.
3. **Up the main steps into the great hall** — petitioners for ordinary court business are routed left to a holding room; expedition candidates are routed right toward the Lion's Den.
4. **Into the Lion's Den at eleventh bell** — see [the_lions_den.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md).

**Guide, not leash.** The party can wander the public sections of the castle — the courtyard, the main hall, the trophy hall — without trouble. They cannot wander the private sections (the Ducal Apartments, Quellenna's wing, the Lower Levels) without triggering the consequences detailed in [rules/consequences.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/consequences.md). Tier 1 in the public halls; Tier 2 the moment they pass a "household only" threshold; Tier 3 if they target a canon NPC.

## Overview

The seat of ducal power in Setland, sited on the rise immediately north of Setland City and sharing its southern wall with the city below. From inside the city, the castle is always visible — dark stone walls against the sky, the lion banners moving in whatever wind is up that day. From inside the castle, the city is a tiered roofline falling away from the south parapet, and the only views of open country are northward across the duchy.

The castle's name is not metaphor: **the foundations of Charnelhold are built upon the bones of Setland's conquered enemies**, a practice dating back to the duchy's earliest wars. The use is layered.

Throughout the castle's load-bearing foundation, bones are **ground into the mortar itself** — invisible, structural, mixed into the binding agent that holds the whole castle together. Setland does not make martyrs. The enemies are consumed; the working stone above looks like working stone. Visitors see gray walls. They walk on a foundation made partly of people, and they do not know it.

In the deepest levels of the dungeon — the jail blocks farthest from the surface, and the torture rooms below those — the practice shifts from invisible to declarative. Down there the walls are **whole bones and skulls set into mortar at eye level**, jaw and rib and femur fitted into the masonry like a brick course, skulls placed at the corners and the doorframes. The mortar between them is gray with the ground bone of older enemies. The deepest rooms are openly, deliberately morbid — they are meant to be. A prisoner brought down here understands what Charnelhold is before the first question is asked. The architecture is the interrogation.

The castle is named for what is in the walls.

The castle itself is large and functional rather than ornate. Malak du Leon does not dress his power in gold. The power is in what he has already absorbed. Setland reds and blacks dominate the interior; lion-headed motifs appear at every transition — door pulls, hearth-surrounds, the brass at every fixture. Servants in dull crimson livery move on quiet routines. A household that runs smoothly because Quellenna runs it, although Quellenna is, of course, only the advisor.

Charnelhold is **outside Aestrum's loop entirely.** Time and magic behave normally. Nothing about the castle resets.

## What's Here

### Public Spaces (the party may pass through these freely at campaign start)

- **The Lion's Gate** — internal gate in the shared wall with Setland City. The only public approach. Two Lion's Hand guards, an audience-list clerk, polite but thorough.
- **The Outer Courtyard** — flagstone yard between the Lion's Gate and the main steps. Stables to the west, a small armory to the east, a covered walkway along the north running to the kitchens. **This is the most likely place for the party to glimpse Aidra Calemthor** — she crosses it regularly between the parade ground (in the city below) and the main hall.
- **The Main Hall** — the great public hall behind the main steps. High ceilings, lion banners on every column, a long central aisle. Petitioners wait here; servants pass through; nothing of substance happens here, which is by design.
- **The Trophy Hall** — a long second-floor gallery, accessible from the main hall by a wide staircase. Hunting trophies, gifts from foreign duchies, and a series of formal portraits of Malak at various ages. A bored servant on duty most hours. The party is welcome to walk it; it is the castle's least sensitive interior space and Malak likes to have it seen.
- **The Lion's Den** — Malak's court chamber, named colloquially after House du Leon's coat of arms. Lion iconography throughout. The party's destination. See [the_lions_den.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/the_lions_den.md).
- **The Throne Room** — a smaller adjoining chamber off the Lion's Den, used only for ceremonial moments that warrant the full throne. Closed except on those occasions. The party will not enter it at campaign start.

### Restricted Spaces (Tier 1/2 consequences if entered without invitation)

- **The Ducal Apartments** — upper floors of the south wing. Malak's private quarters: bedchamber, dressing room, small private dining hall, and a study he uses for show rather than work. Verus sleeps in the bedchamber.
- **Quellenna's Wing** — separate apartments across the Bridge Corridor from Malak's. The quarters are interior — limited windows, away from external walls — comfortable, well-furnished, well-staffed, completely controlled. A gilded cage. Her door has a lock; she is the one who uses it. The **concealed entrance to the escape tunnel** is in her bedchamber, hidden behind a heavy piece of furniture that staff are not permitted to move. See [charnelhold_escape_tunnel.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/charnelhold_escape_tunnel.md).
- **The Bridge Corridor** — a short hall connecting Malak's apartments to Quellenna's wing. Constructed at Malak's order in his second year as Duke. Quellenna uses it. Malak does not. Technically a public corridor; functionally private; staff understand not to traverse it without summons. The court understands what the corridor *implies* and has understood for over a decade that the implication is wrong.
- **The Kitchens and Servant Quarters** — the working spaces. Not secret, but not for guests.
- **The Lower Levels** — the prison and the active torture chambers, descending in tiers. The upper cells are conventional dungeon: stone, iron, dim lamps. As one descends, the walls change. By the lowest jail blocks the masonry visibly includes whole bones — ribs, long bones, the occasional skull set into the wall at eye level. The torture rooms at the bottom are walls of bone in mortar, lit by lamplight that catches the empty eye sockets. The effect is intentional and total. Prisoners know what they are looking at before they are asked anything. The Elakian family (the Black Arrows' ruling clan) has historically contracted use of the deepest chambers, providing services to the duchy in exchange for political cover and operational latitude. The arrangement is old enough that no one thinks of it as an arrangement anymore. **No one descends here without explicit authorization.**

### Adjacent

- **Aidra Calemthor's house** — built against the shared wall on the Setland City side, accessible from the city's streets. **Off-stage at campaign start.** Connected to Quellenna's quarters by the short escape tunnel beneath the wall; see [charnelhold_escape_tunnel.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/charnelhold_escape_tunnel.md). The campaign does not lead the party here, but does not gaslight them if they go looking — see DM Notes.
- **The Parade Ground** — on the city side of the shared wall, north of the barracks district. Aidra works from here. Quiet at campaign start (no muster); the party can see it from the south parapet if they walk that side of the castle.

## Aidra on the Grounds

**Introduce Aidra as Malak's General while the party is at Charnelhold.** She does not need a scene. The goal is *recognition* — when the party later encounters her in Aestrum, they should know who she is by name and face. Suggested beats:

- **Courtyard glimpse.** She crosses the outer courtyard with two junior officers in tow, returning from the parade ground. A servant nods to her by title — *"General"* — and steps aside. She does not stop. If the party is in the yard, she gives them the brief, unreadable attention a senior commander gives strangers on castle grounds, and moves on.
- **Lion's Den intrusion.** Mid-audience, she enters the Lion's Den to pass a brief message to Malak. He nods her through immediately; the audience pauses; she speaks low at his ear; he answers with a single word; she leaves. The petitioners in the room understand this is the General and that whatever she just said matters more than what they came for.
- **Servant reference.** If the party asks about her, the answer is *"the General is at the parade ground today"* or *"the General attends the Duke each morning before court."* Professional, neutral.

**Hold her register at this stage.** She is a competent senior officer, sworn to Setland, professionally cordial with Quellenna, openly willing to disagree with her on military questions. The court sees a Duke's general and a Duke's advisor occupying their proper sometimes-contradictory roles. The relationship between Aidra and Quellenna is not visible. They have been hiding it for years and they are very good at it.

## Faction Reach

- **Setland (Malak du Leon):** Absolute. This is the source. Every authority in Setland traces back here.
- **Quellenna (publicly):** Royal advisor. Invisible authority. See her file and [setland_city.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/setland_city.md) for how this is held.
- **The Lion's Hand (household guard):** Present in numbers. Stationed at the Lion's Gate, the main hall, the entrances to all restricted wings, and the Lower Levels. Competent and disciplined.
- **Black Arrows (Elakian):** Operational presence through the torture chamber contract. Not a permanent garrison — they come when called. The arrangement is old enough to be invisible. Quellenna routes commissions through Black Arrows channels via her city contact-house — see [setland_city.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/setland_city.md).
- **Shar (DM only):** Quellenna is her instrument here. Shar has no other active presence in Charnelhold. She does not need one.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Any elevated character can be assigned one of these at upgrade time.*

- The bones in the lower walls are not all enemies. Some belong to servants who learned things they weren't supposed to. The practice of adding to the foundations did not stop with the wars.
- The Elakian contract includes a clause no one has revisited in thirty years. What it originally covered is not what it currently authorizes.
- Quellenna has a piece of furniture in her quarters she has never permitted staff to move. No one has asked twice. *(The tunnel entrance is behind it.)*
- A junior chamberlain has noticed that certain dispatches from the Lion's Den are recorded in the duchy's books slightly differently than they were spoken in the room. He has not decided what this means. He has decided, prudently, not to ask.
- A Lower Levels jailer has, over the course of his career, recognized two prisoners who arrived without paperwork and left without papers — both, in retrospect, men Quellenna had recently had at court. He has never spoken of it. He understands he is not supposed to.

## DM Notes

- **The party's actual destination here is the Lion's Den.** Everything else is atmosphere and optional discovery. Hold the path light — the household knows they are expected; the route is obvious; the party should never have to ask twice where to go.
- **Public vs. restricted is the tier line.** The party crossing into a restricted wing without invitation is a Tier 1/2 event per [rules/consequences.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/consequences.md), depending on which wing and how persistent they are. The Lower Levels are a fast track to Tier 2; antagonizing a canon NPC inside the castle is a fast track to Tier 3.
- **The bones, layered.** Two distinct uses, both canon:
  - **Throughout the castle's foundation:** ground bone in the mortar. Invisible, structural. Setland does not display conquered enemies to visitors — it consumes them. Reference this obliquely in the upper castle ("the floor rests on what it rests on"); do not lecture the party.
  - **In the deepest jail blocks and torture rooms:** whole bones and skulls mortared into the walls at eye level. Openly morbid. This is interrogation architecture — a prisoner understands what Charnelhold is before the first question is asked. Use the visual texture directly when describing these spaces; the rooms are *meant* to be felt. The party should not see this at campaign start, but if they end up in the Lower Levels as captured prisoners (after off-rail play), play the imagery for full weight.
- **Aidra's introduction is the longest-tail value the party can take from this scene.** They will not engage with her here, but the recognition pays off significantly when they meet her in Aestrum.
- **Quellenna's invisibility is the discipline.** Even in her own home she presents as merely the advisor. If the party stays alert across multiple Lion's Den audiences they could eventually glimpse the pattern. At this stage they should see a duchy that runs unusually smoothly and an advisor who appears unusually devoted to the man she serves. That is all.
- **The household's expectation of the party is neutral-warm.** They are expedition candidates; they have been invited; the staff treats them as competent professionals on duchy business. Not friends, not threats. This shifts the moment they step into a restricted wing.
- **Aidra's house is reachable from the city, not from the castle.** If the party investigates her residence, they do it from the Setland City side — see [setland_city.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/setland_city.md). The shared-wall layout means the house is *adjacent* to the castle but is not *of* the castle, and the campaign treats them as separate locations for access purposes.
- **Verus is a campaign asset, not a tragedy.** The dog should be present at court and in occasional background beats. He is the warmest detail in Malak's character. Do not let him die for shock value at any point in the campaign. If Malak dies, Quellenna takes the dog.
- **The Lower Levels are off-limits at campaign start.** They exist in this file for completeness and for the Tier-2 consequence path. The party has no business there at the opening. If they end up there as captured prisoners (after off-rail play), the experience should be appropriately serious — the Elakian-contracted services are not theater.
- **Shar and Aidra (held in canon).** Shar holds Aidra as a potential correction mechanism — career pressure, whispered rumors, whatever thread can be pulled on a military commander's life. She has never used it. Aidra is a piece on a board that has not required moving. The threat exists; Quellenna knows it exists; the silence it enforces is real even though the lever has never been pulled.
- **Malak's blind spot (held in canon).** He believes he is the architect of his own rise. He has been advised for years by a woman quietly steered by a goddess. Neither has the full picture. Neither knows the other doesn't.
- **Standing authorial note — the relationship holds.** No matter what occurs in the campaign, Quellenna and Aidra remain together. If Quellenna's Shar connection is exposed, Aidra stays. Do not use the relationship as a tragedy. It is the tonal anchor.
