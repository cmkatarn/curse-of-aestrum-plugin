## Description

A small warm-fronted bakery on a side street just off Duskwall's market square — easy to find by nose long before the eye, the day's first loaves carrying half the block from before sunrise. A squat building with a propped top-half door, a plank sign reading **BUNLEAVEN BREADERY** (lettering worn and lately whitewashed), and a front window that always holds a single soft braided apricot loaf, set where anyone walking up the street would see it.

The counter is scrubbed board, laid each morning with the day's baking: dark heavy loaves and paler wheaten ones, small rolls, currant buns, flat seeded keeping-crackers for the road. Prices are chalked on a slate in coppers — a working street's prices, nothing dear. The ovens are the loudest thing in the place; the street outside is as quiet as the rest of the loop-held city.

## What It Provides

Cheap, excellent bread and the warmest welcome in Duskwall. The proprietor, **Albin Crumb**, is the campaign's comic-relief-turned-quiet-heartbreak baker — his voice, his obliviously ribald bread-talk, and the tragedy folded into the apricot loaf in the window are on his sheet: [../../../npcs/chapter_1/remaining_npcs.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/remaining_npcs.md) (§ Albin Crumb).

A stop for: a cheap meal, warmth and gossip (Albin knows every regular's family and troubles), and — on a second or third visit — the Hettie thread.
