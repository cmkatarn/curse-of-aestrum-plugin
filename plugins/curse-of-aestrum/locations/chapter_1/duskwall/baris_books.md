## Description

A distinctive two-story building on the Duskwall market street — dark stone facade, large amber bay windows on both floors that glow warmly from within day and night. A stained glass panel sits above the door. Easy to find; the building looks like nowhere else on the street.

Inside: bookshelves cover every wall from floor to ceiling, with a rolling ladder for the upper reaches. Books are filed wherever they fit — upright where there is room, sideways where there isn't. A central desk occupies the ground floor, lit by a blue glass lamp. The compass rose inlaid into the stone floor just inside the entrance is the only decoration that wasn't an afterthought. Additional shelves flank the exterior entrance on both sides, stocked with lower-value titles for browsing.

The shop smells of old paper, leather binding, and lamp oil. It is almost always quiet.

## What Bari Provides

Players seeking **historical information** about Aestrum, Nortmunde, or adjacent topics should be directed here — Bari is the campaign's primary lore source. He has read everything in the shop and written his own manuscript on Aestrum's history, and **commands the whole of it as active knowledge the moment a player engages him** (see [../../../npcs/chapter_1/bari_khalid.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/bari_khalid.md)). He turns to the manuscript to show a page, cite exact wording, or pin a date — never because he had to look something up to know it. He is accurate and does not embellish.

Players seeking **directions or maps** should be sent to The Salient Cartographer instead.

## The Manuscript

*An Abridged History of Aestrum* — Bari's ongoing personal project, and the primary in-world source for Aestrum historical information. Stored at `{{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/lore/abridged_history_of_aestrum.md`. **Load it together with [../../../npcs/chapter_1/bari_khalid.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/bari_khalid.md) whenever the party engages Bari — it IS his knowledge, not a text he consults out of ignorance.** Its full contents are his to speak on entry.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Assign to an elevated character at upgrade time.*

- The shop operates at a loss most months. Bari knows this. He has not adjusted his inventory, his hours, or his prices. A player who asks about the business gets a frank answer and no apparent distress.
- The blue lamp on the desk is not a candle and does not burn oil. Bari has never explained it and does not think it requires explanation.
- The manuscript is not finished. There are gaps Bari has marked with folded paper — topics he knows exist but cannot find adequate sources for. A player who asks to see the manuscript directly may notice what's missing as much as what's there.

## DM Notes

- The rolling ladder reaches the upper shelves; the upper shelves contain the older and more obscure material. A player who wants something Bari doesn't immediately surface should be directed upward.
- The exterior shelves are unlocked and accessible after hours. Nothing of significant value is kept there — but a player casing the shop after dark would find them open.
- The blue lamp: its nature is left deliberately unestablished. It is not a story element unless the DM chooses to make it one.
