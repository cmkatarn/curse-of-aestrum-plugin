## Overview

Duskwall's small respectable house — four rooms over a narrow front, on a side street
two turns off the Market District square. Where [The Last Resort]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/the_last_resort.md)
is the traveler's inn by the harbour, taking whoever comes up from the dockfront,
Sound Asleep takes bookings and prefers them made in advance. The two houses have
never competed for the same guest.

The name is the proprietor's one joke and it is made in earnest: the house is sound,
and you will sleep. Nobody has ever heard it delivered as a joke.

## What's Here

- **The front room** — small, low-ceilinged, four tables. Breakfast is served and
  supper is not; guests wanting an evening meal are directed to the square. There is
  no bar and no evening trade, which is the point of the place.
- **Four guest rooms** — two on each of the upper floors, all of them clean, plain,
  and properly furnished: real mattresses, a washstand, a window that closes. Linen
  is changed between guests. The doors have working locks and the guests are given
  the keys.
- **The back stair** — guests can reach their rooms without crossing the front room.
  Quietly the most valued feature of the house.

## Who Stays Here

Merchants' factors, minor functionaries, couriers carrying something worth not
losing, and travelers of some standing who want a quiet night rather than a cheap
one. Nobody drinks in the front room past dark. The house has a reputation for
minding its own business that it has earned honestly and would prefer not to
advertise.

## Ambient

Quiet, and conspicuously so for a city house — the side street carries no through
traffic and the Market District's noise arrives flattened, a block's worth of stone
between here and it. The front room smells of bread in the morning and of nothing in
particular the rest of the day. Floorboards on the upper landing announce anyone
using them.

- **[layout]** The upper floors beyond the room count.

## DM Notes

**Author-only.** This is where **Miri Amblecrown** wakes each morning of the cycle.
Having died the previous evening inside the Deckard Estate's dead zone — beyond the
loop's ordinary resurrection — she is recreated overnight by Shar and returns here,
with no memory of Rowan, the day, or her death. She then walks to the Market District
and the day runs again. See [miri_amblecrown.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/miri_amblecrown.md).

The house has no knowledge of any of this. To the staff she is simply a long-staying
guest of good standing — and unlike most things in Aestrum, **they have a record of
it.** The register and the running account are physical objects, so the Recreate step
at 1:30 AM restores them to snapshot state with everything else (see
[rules/revert.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/revert.md)).

The book shows her lodged here **close to two weeks** — the stay she had accumulated
when the loop locked, ~Kythorn 11, 1480 DR. It showed that yesterday. It will show it
tomorrow. **The figure never advances**, the account never settles and never grows,
and nobody who reads it each morning remembers having read it before — so in ten years
nobody has noticed that it doesn't move.

A party that gets a look at the register has a clean, physical demonstration of the
loop that costs no one's trust to obtain: a number that should be climbing and isn't.

**Kept deliberately separate from [The Last Resort]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/the_last_resort.md), which is the
party's reset point.** Miri's reset and the party's home base are different houses so
that a change at one does not disturb the other.
