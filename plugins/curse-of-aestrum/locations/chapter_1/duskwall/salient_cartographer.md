## Description

A cartography shop in Duskwall's market district. Walls lined with nooks stacked with scrolls and rolled maps; larger maps of regions outside Aestrum hang flat on the remaining wall space. The shop smells of parchment and ink. It is organized — not tidy, but organized, in the way of a person who knows exactly where everything is even if no one else could find it.

The shopkeeper is a cartographer, not a historian. He compiles maps from others' accounts — travelers, merchants, surveyors, soldiers — and is precise about this distinction. He will say what a map shows and where it came from. He will not speculate on what it means.

## Services

**Maps for sale** — organized by region. The shopkeeper can locate any map in stock quickly. Custom maps compiled from existing sources on request; cost and time depend on scope.

**Courier work** — the shop occasionally takes on couriers for map deliveries to other settlements. Requires someone who can follow a route without embellishing it. Pay is modest; the work is reliable.

**Reference** — travelers who need to know where something is should come here. Travelers who need to know what something *is* should go to Bari's Books.

## Map Stock — Available Hooks

Each of the following maps is available for purchase. A player who asks about Aestrum broadly will be shown several options; specific requests will be fulfilled directly.

**Dead Zone Map of Aestrum** — 15 gold. Documents the known magical dead zones within Aestrum. Two major zones marked: the Deckard Estate (south of Duskwall) and a zone in the Misty Forest to the northwest. Several minor pockets scattered throughout. The shopkeeper notes the estate dead zone has been there longer than anyone can document — the land simply stopped working. If a player asks whether the map is complete, or asks specifically about dead zones, the shopkeeper pauses and mentions the Nahamkate Desert Survey: it contains an annotation about charred ground with no fire source, and multiple merchant accounts of magic failing in the same area. He compiled the dead zone map from descriptions of places where magic stops working. The language matches. He didn't combine them because the surveyor who documented the Nahamkate section didn't use that framing — but he's noted the resemblance. He will not say what it means. He will describe what the two accounts have in common and let the player draw the conclusion. *(Hook: Deckard Estate, forest dead zone where Rowan may be sheltering, Nahamkate dead zone findable via cross-reference.)*

**Aestrum Regional Map** — 8 gold. Full regional map showing settlements, roads, rivers, and borders. Shows the Cathar Sea forming Aestrum's eastern border (Duskwall sits on its western shore), the Runnaheim Mountains forming the northern and northwestern border, and the Moonmaiden River running south to north from its source at the falls outside Dunleaven (springs in Mount Mumandad) up along the Misty Forest's eastern edge. The mountain pass through the Runnaheim range is marked; the shopkeeper notes it exits Aestrum into the Kingdom of Vrynholt to the north — onward roads from Vrynholt run southwest to Mikaelvad and Dalihad, but those are a long journey. *(Hook: Runnaheim pass as last-resort exit route, Dunleaven location, Moonmaiden Falls.)*

**Nahamkate Desert Survey** — 10 gold. Compiled from merchant accounts and one surveyor's notes. Shows the desert's known paths, wells, and marked dangerous areas. A section of the desert approximately a quarter-mile off the main path is annotated "charred ground — no fire source identified." Multiple merchant accounts report that magical goods ceased functioning in this area. The shopkeeper has no explanation for it. *(Hook: Nahamkate dead zone, Yuan-Ti temple area.)*

**Misty Forest Paths** — 6 gold. Rough map of known paths through the Misty Forest. Logging routes, hunter trails, and the interior features surveyed so far. Compiled from multiple sources; reliability varies by section. The shopkeeper notes the deep forest paths are the least verified — "people who go that far don't always come back to correct me." *(Hook: Caliconia tree area, forest interior.)*

**Dunleaven and Surrounds** — 5 gold. Shows Dunleaven, the Moonmaiden Falls, and the cave systems southwest of the town noted as "old crypts — local legend, not surveyed." The shopkeeper did not survey the crypts personally and cannot speak to their contents. *(Hook: Dunleaven Deadery crypt system.)*

**Setland and Border Regions** — 12 gold. Regional map extending northeast into Setland. Shows Charnelhold as the administrative seat, border roads, and the approximate positions of Setland's military installations as of the map's last compilation. The shopkeeper notes it is several years old — "borders don't move much, but armies do." *(Hook: Charnelhold location, Setland military disposition.)*

**Rockwood and Southern Highlands** — 7 gold. Covers the settlement of Rockwood and the mountain approaches south of it. Marks several ruins in the highlands including a structure labeled "razed temple — Kossuth worshippers, approx. 200 years." The shopkeeper compiled this from a surveyor's account and a merchant who passed through; he cannot confirm whether anything remains standing. *(Hook: Temple of Kossuth location.)*

**Mirot and the Desert Approaches** — 6 gold. Covers the estate district around Mirot and the roads leading into the Nahamkate Desert. Shows the Dundelver estate and several neighboring properties. The desert edge is marked with a note that the sands shift frequently and the map's interior desert detail should not be trusted beyond a few miles out. *(Filler with light hook: Mirot estate district, desert approach.)*

**Mikaelvad — Valley and Pass Routes** — 14 gold. The most expensive regional map in stock, because good information on Mikaelvad is difficult to get. The valley sits under near-permanent dense fog and few surveyors have worked there. What exists is compiled from trade route records. The shopkeeper is honest that the interior detail is thin. *(Filler: Chapter 2 duchy, establishes its geography.)*

**Dalihad — Stoneworks Region** — 9 gold. Covers the duchy of Dalihad, known for its quarrying operations. Shows the major quarry sites, the road network built to support them, and Dalihad's principal settlement. Compiled from trade and merchant records; the shopkeeper considers it reliable. *(Filler: Chapter 2 duchy.)*

**Skaarsdaam and Western Reaches** — 9 gold. Covers the western duchy of Skaarsdaam and its borders with Setland and Mikaelvad. Marks Duke Amblecrown's seat and the road connections west. The shopkeeper notes this map sees less demand than the eastern duchy maps — "not much reason to go that direction unless you're already from there." *(Filler: Chapter 2 duchy.)*

**Nortmunde Kingdom Overview** — 20 gold. A full kingdom map showing all five duchies, the Nortmunde capital at the kingdom's geographic center, major roads, and regional borders. The shopkeeper keeps only one copy and is reluctant to sell it without knowing a replacement is coming. Useful for anyone trying to understand how the pieces fit together. *(Filler with context: puts the full political map in a player's hands.)*

**Cathar Sea Coastal Chart** — 11 gold. Navigation chart of the Cathar Sea and Duskwall's harbor approaches. Marks depth soundings, known hazards, anchorage points, and the coastal settlements accessible by water. Compiled from ship's logs. The shopkeeper notes this is more useful to a captain than a traveler on foot. *(Filler: establishes the sea as navigable, coastal geography.)*

## DM Notes

- The shopkeeper volunteers what a map shows and its source. He does not interpret, speculate, or offer opinions on why a location is significant. A player who wants meaning will need to draw their own conclusions or consult Bari.
- The Dead Zone Map documents two zones; the Nahamkate is the third, findable only by a player who asks the right question or reads both maps and connects the descriptions. The shopkeeper will not volunteer the cross-reference unprompted — only if asked specifically about dead zones or whether the map is complete.
- All three dead zones are accessible to a thorough player: Dead Zone Map (Deckard Estate, Misty Forest) + shopkeeper's cross-reference mention + Nahamkate Desert Survey (charred ground, magic failing). A player who buys only the Dead Zone Map and moves on finds two. A player who asks gets all three.
- The Nahamkate "charred ground" annotation is accurate. The shopkeeper compiled it from merchant accounts of magic failing and a surveyor who documented the discoloration. He has privately noted the resemblance to dead zone descriptions but has not published a conclusion — that is not what he does.
- Maps can be a passive first contact with quest locations the party hasn't reached yet. A player browsing the full stock gets a geographical overview of the campaign's active zones without being told why they matter.
- Courier work is a legitimate income source for the party — use it to move them toward a location they'd otherwise miss.
