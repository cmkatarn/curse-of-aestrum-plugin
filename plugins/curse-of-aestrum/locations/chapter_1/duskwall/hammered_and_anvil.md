## Description

Set within the castle's inner courtyard, which houses a small cluster of shops — a grocer, a blacksmith, and the Hammered and Anvil sharing a wall with the smithy. Music carries from the tavern all the way to the castle gate; on quiet evenings, you can hear it from the road below.

Inside: the smells arrive before anything else — grilled meat, baked bread, spilt grog, and underneath it all the faint iron tang from next door. The floor is checkered stone, blue and white, worn smooth at the threshold. Hanging chandeliers provide faint candlelight. Enough to see, not enough to read.

The far wall holds the tavern counter — kegs stacked on shelves above it, one tapped and ready at the near end of the bar. Tables fill the middle of the room, occupied most evenings by castle guards, staff, and whoever has business in the ward. In the corner, a half-elf bard plays to enchanted instruments that keep time without him; he moves slowly to the melody, more performance than effort.

The shared fireplace dominates the left wall. Through its opening — between glowing ingots below and a bread rack above — you can see directly into the blacksmith's shop. The bread baking above the ingots is either perfectly done or burnt on the bottom, depending entirely on how hard the smithy was running when it went in.

## The Bread

An equal chance of perfect or burned on the bottom. If the smithy was busy, the furnace ran hot and the bread caught. If it was quiet, the bake was clean. A player paying attention to the bread quality on a given day has a rough measure of castle activity.

## Amber Crown Note

The line "where steel on steel does ring through the air" from the Amber Crown Prophecy was interpreted as pointing to this location — the clang of the smithy carries through the shared fireplace into the tavern throughout the day.

## Faction Reach

- **Aestrum (Tallwood):** Primary clientele is castle guards and staff. This is their tavern. The keeper operates within the castle's social gravity without any formal allegiance.
- **Shar:** Judith Asemyeer is stationed here. Her daily presence enables the gate directive. The tavern itself is not a Shar operation — only the person behind it.
- **Rowan Deckard:** No standing presence. His appearance here was a one-time event during a period of adjustment after being displaced from his ancestral home — not a regular pattern.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Assign to an elevated character at upgrade time.*

- The barmaiden moves through a crowded room like she has done it ten thousand times — never spills, never collides, never seems to actually be working hard. Castle guards who drink here every night have stopped noticing. A first-time visitor might.
- The bard in the corner is too good for this room and knows it. He plays here because the pay is reliable, not because he wants to be here. A player who buys him a drink will find someone with opinions about Duskwall he hasn't shared with the regulars.
- The bread is a reliable indicator. Burned bottom means the smithy was busy. A player who orders bread on multiple visits and pays attention will notice the correlation.

## DM Notes

- **Judith's operational role:** She works the floor, not the bar. Her composure is slightly above what the job requires — a detail available to observant players who spend time here. Her directive to hold the castle gate at 11:30 PM each night runs through her presence in this location. → Full detail in remaining_npcs.md (Judith Asemyeer) and duskwall_castle.md.
- **Rowan Deckard:** Not a regular. His single appearance here was a one-time event — he was adjusting to a new routine after being forced from his ancestral home. He does not reset here and should not be expected at this location.
- **The smithy:** The blacksmith next door is a separate establishment sharing the fireplace. The shared wall means sound and heat pass freely between the two spaces. The bread mechanic is a consistent feature, not a one-off.
- **Elyas:** Knows things about the castle ward that a regular observer would accumulate over time. Not a deep story thread — but a player who befriends him gets ambient intelligence about guard rotations, who drinks heavily, who doesn't come in anymore.
