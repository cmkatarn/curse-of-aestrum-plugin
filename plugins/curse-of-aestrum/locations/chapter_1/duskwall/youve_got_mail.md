## Description

An armor shop in Duskwall's market district. The sign above the door reads *You've Got Mail* in large, confident lettering. The shop sells armor.

Inside: weapons and armor fill the walls and stands throughout the space — practical stock, well-maintained, nothing exotic. The upper walls are lined with shields, many bearing ancient coats-of-arms from families and houses no longer in existence. A workbench near the back is where Yuvan does in-shop repairs. The proprietor is either at the bench or behind the counter, shirtless, in shorts, doing something that involves visible muscle use.

## The Name

The name has caused consistent confusion. Customers expecting a courier or postal service enter, look around, and leave. This has happened enough times that Yuvan is neither surprised nor interested in discussing it. He does not explain the name unless directly asked, and even then his answer is short.
