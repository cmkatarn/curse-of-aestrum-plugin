## Description

A messenger service in Duskwall's market district. The front of the shop is clean and functional — a counter, a wall of cubbies sorted by destination, a faint smell of birds from the loft above. The crows are kept in the back room and are not visible from the counter, but you can hear them shifting and rustling periodically through the wall.

The front windows look directly onto the market street. From behind the counter, Yuvan's shop is visible across the way. The proprietor is aware of this.

The service runs on trained crows for settlements within reliable range and human couriers for everything else. Deliveries are logged. The operation is organized in the way that any business handling other people's sensitive correspondence must be.

## Services

**Message delivery** — by trained crow to settlements within range, or by contracted courier for longer distances or sensitive cargo. Pricing by distance and urgency. Delivery is not guaranteed in all weather.

**Address registry** — the shop maintains a public registry of residents and businesses in Duskwall: name, occupation, and last known address. Updated as information comes in. Anyone may consult it; anyone may register themselves. There is no fee to look.

**Receiving address** — travelers without a fixed address in Duskwall can register the shop as their receiving address. Messages held until collected.

## The Address Registry

The registry is the most operationally useful thing in the shop. It covers most established residents and businesses — anyone who receives correspondence or wants to be findable. Consulting it takes a few minutes.

**What it can confirm:** Where a named person or business is located, if they're registered. Occupation as self-reported. Whether they've updated their entry recently.

**What it cannot confirm:** Whether the information is current. Whether the person still lives there. Whether the name given is real.

**Notable entries a player might find:**
- Lisandre Trambeathen — listed under The Wand and Whisk, market district
- Bari Khalid — listed under Bari's Books
- Most castle staff are not registered — they live within the castle walls and receive correspondence through the castle's own system
- Rowan Deckard — not listed. His estate is outside the city. A player searching for him by name finds nothing.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Assign to an elevated character at upgrade time.*

- The shop logs every message sent and received, by sender name and destination. The logs are not public, but they exist. A player who gets access to the logs has a record of who has been corresponding with whom, and when correspondence stopped.

## The Delivery Problem

Crows that approach the Aestrum boundary sense it and turn back, returning to the shop intact. Human couriers hit the barrier, feel the fuzzball-induced urge to turn back, and return with vague explanations — bad roads, blocked passes, bad weather. The proprietor has been logging an unusual number of failed deliveries and is attributing it to a rough season. He has not connected the pattern to anything larger.

Player-facing: "We'll send it today, but some routes have been unreliable lately — might need a retry." The retry never succeeds. A player who sends multiple messages over multiple days and checks back may eventually notice that nothing is getting out.

## The Proprietor

Bookish male, runs the shop alone. Organized, precise, takes the work seriously. Quietly suffering.

His desk faces the front window. Yuvan's shop is directly across the street. He watches more women walk into an armor shop than into his messenger service on a daily basis, and it does not sit right with him. He does not raise this topic unless pushed — but pushed even slightly, he has opinions, and they come out in the clipped tone of a man who has been composing this argument in his head for months.

He is not hostile to customers. He is professional. He is just also, at any given moment, approximately thirty seconds away from glancing out the window.

## DM Notes

- The registry is a player tool. When the party needs to locate a named Duskwall resident, this is the correct first stop.
- The correspondence logs are DM-only unless the party specifically pursues access. They could be used to establish who Lisandre has been writing to, or to confirm that someone stopped sending messages on a particular date.
- Rowan Deckard's absence from the registry is a detail available to an observant player — he is deliberately unfindable through normal channels.
- The castle staff gap is realistic and useful: players trying to locate a specific castle resident through this registry will be told the person isn't listed, which correctly redirects them toward the castle itself.
- The proprietor's Yuvan jealousy is a tone beat, not a story thread. Play it dry. He's not unhinged — he's just a man with a front-row seat to something that bothers him every day.
