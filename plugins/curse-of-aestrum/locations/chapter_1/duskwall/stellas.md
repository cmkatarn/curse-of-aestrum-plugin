## Description

A busy restaurant near Duskwall's market district. Higher-end than most of what the town offers — tablecloths, candles, the smell of something actually cooking rather than something being reheated. Popular with locals who have money to spend and off-duty guards who have just been paid.

The menu runs two directions: proper sit-down meals at one end, travel provisions at the other. Both are available at the counter for those who aren't staying.

## What Stella's Provides

**Dining** — sit-down meals, prepared food, wine. The goat cheesecake is the house specialty and is genuinely good. Not a place to come for a quick drink.

**Provisions** — cured meats, salted pork, travel rations. Priced fairly. Quality is consistent. A party stocking up before leaving Duskwall should stop here.

## Callback Slots

- "Stella" or "the proprietor" — whoever runs the front of house; assign when a player engages the person in charge by name or with a specific request

## DM Notes

- No current story significance. Functional location — food, provisions, atmosphere.
- Off-duty guards are regular clientele. A player wanting to overhear castle gossip in a relaxed setting has better odds here than at the Hammered and Anvil, where everyone knows each other.
- The goat cheesecake is real. If a player orders it, it's good. That's the whole bit.
