## Description

A modest storefront on one of Duskwall's market-adjacent streets — easy to miss from outside, impossible to forget once you've been in. The exterior is unremarkable: a painted sign, a door that sticks slightly in damp weather, a single display window too crowded to see through clearly.

Inside: shelves cover every wall floor to ceiling, glass-topped counters run the periphery, and every surface is fully stocked. The density is the point. Wands filed upright in ceramic jars. Rings on velvet trays beneath glass. Scrolls in labeled cubbies. Boots, cloaks, belts, and bracers folded or hung wherever they fit. The shop smells faintly of ozone and cedar. Moving through it requires care.

Nearly every item on display carries an enchantment. This incidentally makes the shop one of the more defensible spaces in Duskwall — the passive properties of several hundred magical objects create an environment hostile to casual theft. Mazikeen has never publicly confirmed this is intentional.

## Services

**Magic item identification** — Mazikeen examines an item and states its properties. Priced per item. He is accurate and does not embellish.

**Enchantment transfer** — An enchantment is moved from one item to another of the player's choosing. The source item is destroyed in the process. The service is reliable, consistent, and not cheap. Players wishing to repurpose a found item into something personally usable should ask.

**Essence extraction** — An existing magic item's core enchantment is extracted and bound to one or more new vessels. The source item is destroyed. The resulting items carry either the full effect (undivided) or a weakened form distributed among multiple wearers. Mazikeen is not loop-aware and will not understand why a party wants cycle-specific protection, but will not refuse a paying commission. See [magical_item_conversion.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/magical_item_conversion.md).

**Daily specials** — One or two items each day presented at Mazikeen's "special price": 20% off a price that already carries a 30% markup. The customer pays approximately 4% above base and leaves satisfied. Mazikeen presents these with genuine enthusiasm.

## Pricing

All goods are priced at a 30% markup above base market value. The special price is 20% off that figure. All sales are final without exception. Mazikeen does not negotiate on this policy, though he will negotiate on almost everything else.

If a player is looking for something reasonable — a common or uncommon magic item, a utility scroll, a standard service — Mazikeen has it or can source it. Implausible requests are still worth asking about.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Assign to an elevated character at upgrade time.*

- Mazikeen has never been robbed successfully. Several people have tried. He does not discuss what happened to them, but he will describe the attempts with the affectionate detail of someone recounting old acquaintances.
- The shop has no visible lock on the inside of the door. Regular customers have noticed this and stopped wondering about it.

## DM Notes

- The passive enchantment density is real protection — a player attempting to steal from the shop should face creative resistance proportional to their approach. Rule of cool applies in both directions: a clever theft attempt gets a fair chance; a grab-and-run does not.
- Mazikeen recognizes cons and scams immediately. He will name what the player attempted, note its flaws, and offer improvement advice. He is not offended. He is collegial about it.
- Inventory is deferred — do not generate specific stock lists. When a player asks for a specific item, determine availability by whether it is reasonable for an established magical goods shop to carry. When in doubt, Mazikeen has it.
