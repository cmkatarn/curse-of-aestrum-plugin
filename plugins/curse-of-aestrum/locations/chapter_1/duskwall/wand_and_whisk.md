## Description

A well-stocked apothecary and ingredients shop in Duskwall's market district. The exterior is tidy, the signage modest — *Trambeathen's Wand and Whisk* in clean lettering above the door. Easy to overlook from the street. Inside, the shop is dense with organized stock: dried herbs and spices in labeled jars along the back wall, potion vials in a locked glass case at the counter, specialty ingredients arranged by region of origin on the center shelves. The smell is layered — dried lavender and something sharper underneath, alchemical reagents sealed behind the counter glass.

Lisandre mans the counter himself. The arrangement is personal and deliberate — he knows the inventory, names the provenance of each import, and runs the transaction with the warm efficiency of someone who genuinely enjoys the theater of commerce. He is almost always alone in the shop. He is never unprepared.

## What the Shop Provides

**Cooking ingredients and specialty produce** — dried spices, herb bundles, preserved goods, regionally sourced imports. The variety is wider than any general market stock. Lisandre names provenance with relish: *freshly arrived from the spice markets of Calimshan, only two of these left in all of Aestrum.*

**Potions** — standard healing and utility stock behind the locked counter case. Priced above market, quality guaranteed. He does not negotiate prices but does not need to — the quality justifies them.

**Special offerings** — items not on visible display, offered situationally. Lisandre produces what a customer needs from behind the counter with the air of someone who anticipated the request. A player who asks about something unusual may find Lisandre already has it.

**Love Potion** — available at 500 gold. Duration: one full week. Not on display. Offered when relevant.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Assign to an elevated character at upgrade time.*

- Lisandre makes regular ingredient deliveries to Stella's restaurant. The contact who receives the deliveries knows him by name and considers the arrangement reliable. He does not vary the schedule.
- He pays for exceptional sourcing and expects exceptional sourcing in return. Merchants who try to pass off substitutions learn this the first time it happens; there is no second time.

## DM Notes

- Lisandre is always the shopkeeper here. The warm, inviting merchant persona is his public face — he inhabits it completely. Any tension that arises in this shop comes from below the surface, not from it.
- He does not lie if he can help it. Redirects, misdirects, answers technically without revealing. A player pressing him directly should feel the conversation sliding sideways rather than hitting a wall.
- The shop itself provides no reason for hostility unless the party gives him one.
- The Love Potion sale: offered at 500 gold, lasts one week, not negotiable. He presented it as a matter of course. He keeps several in stock. If asked about the price: *"You can't put a price on love, but I've given it a go, haven't I?"*
- **Morning scrying:** Each morning, as his first act of the day, Lisandre remotely activates the Eyeball Staff from this location to check in on it — he expects to find it where it belongs, within the crypt, and scries to verify. He can read only what the eye shows him.
