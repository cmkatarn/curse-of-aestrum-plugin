## Description

Duskwall Castle crowns the seaside cliffs above the city. The outer gate is set into a thick curtain wall of pale grey Dalihad stone, fitted without mortar in the guild mason tradition. The same stone covers the cliff face below — reinforced and finished smooth, carved directly into the rock. From the harbor, the castle appears to grow from the cliff itself.

The outer ward is open access during daylight hours — tradespeople, petitioners, and staff move through without challenge. The first real gatekeeping occurs at the entrance to the castle interior.

## Layout

### Upper Level — Great Hall, Dining Room, and Galadiil's Quarters

The great hall is the castle's primary formal space. Long, stone-floored, built into the cliff's upper face with two tall stained glass windows flanking the ducal throne — sea-facing, multi-colored glass that casts shifting light across the stone floor when the sun is right. Twin fountains stand to either side of the throne, fed from cisterns cut into the cliff. The hall is lit by Tallwood-house candles throughout — tall white pillar candles and smaller blue votives, in numbers that make the room warm and slightly theatrical when fully lit. A dark stone runner leads from the entrance to the dais.

Adjacent to the hall, separated by a thick doorway with a heavy curtain rather than a door: the dining room. Sized for private meals, not court display. When conversations carry — and they do, when voices are raised or when the room is quiet otherwise — the hall is the better place to hear them. The midday meal between the Duke and Duchess occurs here.

Off the service corridor behind the great hall: Galadiil's chambers. Upper level, separate from Evandur's lower-level quarters. The rooms are appropriate to the Duchess's rank — well-appointed, well-maintained, and quietly inescapable. Her window faces the garden rather than the sea; she has a view of the only outdoor space she is permitted to use.

The garden occupies the enclosed space behind the great hall, within the curtain wall. A low section of the outer wall on the castle's seaward flank — less finished than the rest, where the cliff does most of the structural work — can be scaled from the city road below. A DC 12 Stealth check clears the guard rotation near that section. A character who fails is heard, not seen; the guard at that post investigates but does not have line of sight to the wall itself.

### Lower Level — Barracks Hallway

A long stone corridor runs the castle's ground floor. In sequence from the near end: the stairwell up to the great hall and the barracks; Evandur's private quarters midway; the maze entrance door further along; and at the very end, the vault entrance — a separate locked door to the treasury, distinct from the maze.

The maze entrance door is nearly silent in its frame. No guards are posted on the corridor side — the hall beyond Evandur's quarters is empty under normal conditions. Evandur posted guards at the exterior cliff face entrance after Miklas of Thundertree was killed and his map surfaced in hostile hands; the interior corridor he left unguarded.

The vault entrance at the corridor's end requires a difficult lock to open directly. The maze, entered through its own door, provides an alternate route to the vault through the labyrinth — slower, and considerably more dangerous.

The maze also has an exterior entrance: a second access point set into the cliff face below the castle, reachable from outside the walls without passing through the main gate. This is the escape route's outer terminus — the exit Miklas intended for a fleeing Duke. In practice it is where the Setland expedition enters the complex.

## Areas

### Great Hall
Used for formal audiences, ducal appearances, and nothing else. Evandur does not hold court frequently. Staff move through during service hours and are otherwise absent. The candle arrangement requires regular tending; the staff member responsible for it is the most common presence in the hall outside of formal sessions.

Voices from the dining room can be heard clearly from inside the hall when the curtain is parted, and faintly through it when it is not.

### Dining Room
The midday meal between Evandur and Galadiil occurs here. Staff who serve the meal are present, then dismissed — Evandur does not want an audience for the meal itself. Once dismissed, they are gone. The meal is private. The ritual with the tea happens after the food is cleared, when no one official is in the room.

### Royal Garden
Enclosed by the curtain wall. Galadiil is most often found here when not confined to her chambers. Stone-paved, with a few plantings that receive the sea light. The garden has a view of the water over the wall's edge. The wall section that can be scaled from outside is on the garden's seaward side.

### Galadiil's Quarters
Upper level, off the service corridor behind the great hall. Well-appointed by rank, inescapable by design. Her window faces the garden — the only outdoor space she is permitted to use. From this window she can see the garden wall, the plantings, and the edge of the sea-light that reaches the garden in the afternoon. The upper-level windows facing the sea are in the great hall; hers face inward. She did not choose this arrangement and has never commented on it.

Hidden somewhere in these rooms: the Ilphekiir family ring. She does not wear it. She will not leave without it. → See `## The Ilphekiir Ring` in galadiil_ilphekiir.md.

### Evandur's Private Quarters
Lower level, midway down the barracks corridor. Sparse for the space. Functional furniture, no personal ornamentation. His desk holds correspondence awaiting his review; he clears it each night before retiring. He does not invite staff into the room and maintains it with minimal service — the room is swept, linens changed, and otherwise left alone.

Concealed within the room — well-hidden, not obviously placed — are three maze keys and three maze tokens. These are his escape supplies for the passage beneath the castle. He has never used them. Galadiil located and procured one of each without being caught; two of each remain.

### Barracks and Guard Quarters
Lower level, near-end of the hallway. The castle garrison is small — adequate for peacetime gate duty and petitioner management. Rotating shifts. Guards do not patrol beyond Evandur's quarters toward the vault end. This has been standing practice long enough that no one questions it.

Evandur's two interior guards hold position at the maze entrance door with an alarm pyre. They are legitimate Duskwall men — posted here specifically because Miklas's map reached hostile hands.

## Faction Reach

- **Aestrum (Tallwood):** Full legitimate authority. The castle is the duchy seat; Evandur's orders are the only ones the guard answers.
- **Setland (advance expedition):** Concealed external presence. Two members of a 15-person assassination team are disguised as guards at the exterior maze entrance in the cliff face below the castle. They kill the real guards posted there each morning and take their positions; the real guards reset at midnight and the cycle repeats. Ten more are camped in the nearest woods awaiting a success signal. None are loop-aware — they believe time is passing normally and they are down three men.
- **The Shrikes:** Operational access via the maze. The maze entrance door provides a route through the lower hallway to Evandur's quarters. The Shrikes are an ancillary assassination effort — a second concurrent plot, separate from the Setland operation.
- **Setland (external):** Military pressure from Malak's advancing army. No additional internal foothold beyond the embedded expedition.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Assign to an elevated character at upgrade time.*

- The Duchess's personal maid has been summoned twice for what other staff were told was "the Duchess's constitution." Both times, the episodes followed the midday meal with the Duke. The maid has not spoken about it. She has noticed the pattern. She has not decided what to do with having noticed.
- The stained glass windows in the great hall are sea-facing. On clear days, the colored light reaches the throne. Staff know that the Duke does not like the light on his face — sessions scheduled in the late morning are moved to afternoon whenever possible. The preference is enforced without explanation.
- The exterior maze entrance — a door in the cliff face below the castle, outside the walls — is guarded by two men who have never been seen to change rotation. They do not eat with the garrison. They do not participate in off-duty conversation. Duskwall guards assigned to that post don't last long; the replacements always look unfamiliar.

## DM Notes

- **The 11:30 PM attempt:** Each night, two Setland agents enter through the main castle gate — held open by Judith Asemyeer — and move to Evandur's quarters. Both Evandur and the agents reset at midnight. The agents wake each morning not knowing they have done this before, believing they are still working toward a first successful kill.
- **Judith's role:** Judith Asemyeer (barmaiden at the Hammered and Anvil, secret Shar acolyte) holds a Shar directive to keep the main castle gate passable at 11:30 PM each night. If Judith completes her morning routine at the Shrine of Selûne without interruption, the gate is open. If she is interrupted — by any means, before she receives her orders from Hati — the gate is not held. The Setland expedition's attack vector collapses. This is the thread.
- **The full expedition:** 15 members total, none loop-aware. 3 are permanently dead inside the maze. 2 are at the exterior cliff-face entrance disguised as guards, killing the real posted guards each morning (who reset and are killed again). 10 are camped in the nearest woods waiting on a success signal. → Full detail in [castle_vault.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/castle_vault.md).
- **Luca and the Shrikes** are a separate, concurrent assassination attempt using the interior maze route — a different entry entirely. The two plots share a target and are unaware of each other.
- **What's in the vault:** Behind a difficult lock at the maze's end — immense ducal wealth. Irrelevant to most approaches, but the vault also functions as a known infinite-wealth exploit: valuables removed and secured in a dead zone persist across resets while the vault restores to base state each morning. → Full detail in [castle_vault.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/castle_vault.md).
- **Galadiil's confinement:** She cannot leave the outer ward without escort. Evandur's standing order to the guard is that she is not to pass the outer gate unaccompanied. This is enforced — Sergeant Cael knows her face and knows his orders. A direct suggestion from another person can bypass the fuzzball and prompt her to try; she will get as far as the gate before being stopped, or as far as the Aestrum border before hitting the barrier. Either way she resets at midnight back in her quarters. The Ilphekiir ring is a secondary anchor — she will not attempt to leave without retrieving it first, which buys time for the window to close.
- **Evandur and staff:** He is a poor conversationalist and does not socialize with the people who work for him. Staff who do their jobs without requiring acknowledgment fare fine. Staff who expect warmth do not stay. The household has selected, over ten years, for people who prefer to be invisible.
- **The Hylea's Mead thread:** The maid callback slot is the independent flag. A player who pursues the castle's domestic staff, specifically asking about Galadiil's health or the midday meal, will find someone who has noticed the pattern without naming it. The maid will not volunteer that she knows what Hylea's Mead does — but a DC 14 Persuasion check opens her up. She will say: *"I never asked. I thought it wasn't my place."* She believes it. She's wrong that it wasn't her place. She knows that now.
