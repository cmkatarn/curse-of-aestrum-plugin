## Description

Located within Duskwall's city gates, near the road that climbs to the castle. Hung banners at the outer entrance mark it as a shrine. Entry is through a fissure in a rock wall — roughly 25 to 30 feet in length, wide and tall enough for a single person to walk through comfortably. Two people moving in opposite directions must squeeze past each other.

The inner shrine is a cave. Selûne iconography covers the walls — carved crescents, stars, open eyes. Two braziers burn at all times; the light they cast is warm and low. A natural opening in the ceiling admits daylight during the day and moonlight at night. Stone benches line the walls, enough for perhaps a dozen visitors to sit.

The back wall carries an ornate tapestry: black field, white stars and eyes, Selûne iconography — fine work, clearly commissioned. To a visitor it reads as nothing more than a devotional wall-hanging on the stone. *(DM-only: what the tapestry conceals is in DM Notes — a deep alcove and the Shar statue. **Never** narrate the alcove, the recess, or anything behind the tapestry when establishing the room; to any visitor the back wall is simply a tapestried wall until the cloth is disturbed.)*

The cave is large enough for combat. It almost certainly will see it.

## Faction Reach

- **Shar:** Primary operative control. Both the priestess and her operative are Shar agents. The shrine functions as a prayer harvesting point. Shar's statue resets each morning — this was the state at loop start.

**Why the statue is second-hand.** The Shar statue behind the tapestry was not made for this shrine: it was taken from the [Misty Forest shrine]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/misty_forest_shar_shrine.md) and installed here, with a lesser likeness left in its place. Shar had no time to obtain another — commissioning one takes months, and importing one means hauling several hundred pounds of carved stone across a border where it would be seen and remembered. The lock was being brought forward against a traveller's itinerary (see [`{{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/shar.md`]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/shar.md), *Why Aestrum — and Why Then*), and the only Shar statue already inside Aestrum was Rowan Deckard's. **His resentment at losing it starts here.**
- **Selûne:** Cover only. The shrine presents outwardly as a functioning Selûne shrine in good standing. No one in Duskwall has reason to suspect otherwise.

## Loop Anchor Mechanics

The Shar statue in this shrine is one of two prayer anchor points sustaining the cycle. Any prayer at this location interpretable as "I wish I could re-live today" — however loosely — is sufficient.

**Known prayer sources at this shrine:**
- **Judith Asemyeer** (~10 AM daily): Shar acolyte who visits each morning, receives orders from Hati, then prays for one more day to safely complete her mission. The fear drives the prayer. She does not know the prayer serves any mechanical purpose.
- **Delmuir Goodfeet** (late evening): Prays sincerely for another kiss tomorrow from Maris Vell. An entirely innocent prayer — a lovestruck man asking Selûne for one more good day — harvested by Shar through her replacement statue. He is not a Shar acolyte and has no awareness of the loop.

**How the loop is broken:** Both anchor shrines must be destroyed — this shrine AND Rowan's effigy at the Misty Forest shrine — BEFORE any qualifying prayer is completed at either location that day. Both shrines reset each morning. The Shar statue here resets to its current form; destroying it is not permanent unless done on the cycle-breaking day.

**Tactical note:** The fissure entry is single-file. Anyone retreating or arriving during a combat encounter in the cave must pass through it one at a time.

## Seed Secrets

*Vessel-agnostic. Any elevated character can carry these. Assign at upgrade time.*

- Judith Asemyeer visits the shrine every morning around 10 AM without fail — speaks privately with Hati before praying, then leaves for her shift at the Hammered and Anvil. The pattern is consistent enough that anyone watching the shrine would notice it within a day.
- Delmuir Goodfeet visits late in the evening, alone, and prays quietly before leaving. A regular worshipper who knows the shrine's rhythms would recognize him as a recent addition — he's only been coming since he arrived in Duskwall courting Maris Vell.
- The previous Selûne priestess — a woman who had tended the shrine for years — disappeared without explanation in the weeks before the loop began. Hati arrived shortly after and took over the role. Townsfolk accepted her because she dressed and acted the part. No one investigated the disappearance seriously. Someone in Duskwall remembers the old priestess.

## DM Notes

- Hati resets at the shrine each morning with no memory of prior visits, prior confrontations, or the statue's repeated destruction. She wakes as a priestess beginning her day.
- The Shar statue resets each morning — it was present at loop start. Destroying it is not permanent. On the cycle-breaking day, it must be destroyed before Judith's 10 AM prayer or Delmuir's evening prayer completes.
- Delmuir's prayer is the loop's most bittersweet sustaining mechanism — a man sincerely asking Selûne for one more good day, unknowingly feeding Shar's engine.
- **The concealed alcove and statue (hidden until the tapestry is disturbed).** Behind the tapestry, a deep alcove is cut into the back wall; where the original Selûne statue once stood, the Shar statue now sits — a lithe female form carved without detail, no face, no features, no texture, as void as Shar's realm; the absence of detail is the point. A player who pulls the tapestry aside sees a statue conspicuously wrong — not Selûne's open, lunar iconography but that void form; a DC 13 Religion check names it Shar's aesthetic. **The words *alcove*, *recess*, and *statue* are author-facing** — none belongs in any player-facing description of the shrine until the tapestry is moved. Hati's combat sequence begins the moment the tapestry is disturbed with apparent intent.
- The previous priestess's disappearance is unresolved. Her fate is a DM decision — she may be dead, she may be somewhere in Aestrum having been quietly removed by Hati's handlers, or she may be a thread that leads somewhere useful in Chapter 2.
