## Overview

The capital city of Aestrum, located on the western shores of the Cathar Sea (the inland sea forms Aestrum's eastern border; Duskwall sits at its western edge). Duskwall Castle sits atop seaside cliffs overlooking the city. The castle's outer walls and principal towers are built from pale grey Dalihad-quarried stone — dressed tight and fitted without mortar in the fashion Dalihad's guild masons are known for. The cliff face into which the lower castle is carved was reinforced and finished with the same material.

## Harbor & Maritime Presence — Grounding (DM)

Duskwall is a **sealed-in port.** Aestrum's boundary traps sea traffic as surely as it traps everyone on land: a vessel that tries to cross the boundary strikes it and founders (see [The Incorrigible]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/the_incorrigible.md)). External ships do **not** freely come and go, and cargo, news, and fresh arrivals do not wash in off the water the way they would at an ordinary port.

**Do not render the harbor with "busy seaport" ambient defaults** — incoming or departing ships, masts crowding the quay, sailors fresh off a boat, word carried in by sea — unless a specific documented vessel or state grounds it. A character looking at the harbor sees the *static* port: the dockfront and quay, the waterfront trades (Harbormaster, Holy Mackerel, etc. — see *Waterfront District* below), the water, the weather, the lamps — not asserted traffic.

**The one named harbor vessel is [The Incorrigible]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/the_incorrigible.md), and it anchors offshore on Day 9.** Before Day 9 it is not present. Any other craft in the harbor at a given time is undocumented: ground it in a file or leave it unrendered — do not conjure a fleet to fill a dusk-harbor image. This is the location-level companion to the gate's fabricated-concrete check (a sealed setting makes ambient world-filling concretes *unsafe* defaults, where a normal port would make them safe).
