## Overview

A disused warehouse at the edge of Duskwall's dockfront — empty, unwatched, and useful
to two different people who both believe they are the only one who knows it is there.

**It is Shrike ground, and it does not look like it.** A shell with a view of the quay,
empty most of the hours of the day — which is exactly why the city's criminal power uses
it, and exactly why a halfling looking for somewhere unwatched picked it too.

## Delmuir's Practice — a repeating cycle

He cannot practise in the open. He took the Loadstone from these docks, from people who
work them daily, and a halfling visibly making objects leap across the wharf is a
halfling who gets recognised.

**The item is what makes hiding work.** The Loadstone carries a load *from here to
there* — the effect lands at the target, not at the thrower. From inside this building
he can put a pebble in a plank crack, his cap on a seagull and a coin in a beggar's hand
clear across the wharf, and nobody watching any of those things sees a source. **His
targets are public; he is not.**

Five throws, every morning, forever. See
[the waterfront]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/waterfront.md) for the rest of his day.

## Luca's Use — a conditional branch

**This is where the Shrikes conscript.** Luca's standing play, when skilled outsiders
with no local ties surface in Duskwall, is to corner them somewhere private, identify
what they are good for, and put them on a vault run — with refusal not offered as an
option. This building is where that conversation happens.

It fires **only when a party has drawn his attention**; it is not a daily event and
should not be rendered as one. See [the_shrikes.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/the_shrikes.md)
for the recruitment pattern and what the vault run is actually for.

**Whoever does the cornering will not look like Luca**, because nobody knows what Luca
looks like — he wears a new face every morning. See *Recognition and the Daily Hand-off*
on his sheet.

## The Near Miss — every morning, by minutes

**Delmuir arrives at 9 AM, after the Shrikes have gone.** The hand-off happens earlier
and is finished before he gets there; he turns up to an empty building and throws
pebbles at seagulls in a room that was full a short while ago.

**9 AM is therefore the deadline the hand-off does not know it has.** Every morning it
must be done and dispersed before a halfling walks in — and every morning, for ten
years, it has been.

**They have never met, and the margin is thin.** He has been trespassing on Luca's
ground every morning for ten years, and the only reason nobody has noticed is that the
two routines sit end to end instead of overlapping. He is a duke's third son who found a
thing sitting on a dock; he has no idea whose room he is standing in.

The thinness is the asset. Ways it can pay:

- **A morning that runs long.** The hand-off overruns, or Delmuir comes early, and he
  walks in on Luca proving to his lieutenants that he is Luca. That is the single worst
  thing a cheerful, talkative halfling could witness.
- **A conscription is not a morning event.** It fires when outsiders surface, at whatever
  hour Luca chooses — so a party cornered here in the middle of the day may well have a
  halfling in the back of the room, mid-practice, entirely unplanned.
- **A party looking for one thread finds the other.** Delmuir's practice spot and Shrike
  ground are one address.
- **Delmuir could tell you what happens here** — not that he knows it matters. He would
  simply mention, warmly and at length, the men who use the place before he does.

**None of this has fired in canon.** It is a loaded coincidence produced by two
established routines sharing one address; treat it as available, not as scheduled.
