## Overview

A small austere hut in a clearing at the eastern edge of the forested foothills, set back about thirty yards north of the east road. Jiasha lives here alone. The hut sits roughly **200 feet from the Aestrum boundary** — close enough that the road past the clearing leads directly into the loop, far enough that the hut itself is on Setland soil and unaffected.

The hut and the clearing both read **quiet**, in a way that is not accidental. Jiasha keeps the place tidy and undecorated. Travelers who pass through remember it as warm and unremarkable, and that is what she wants them to remember.

The hut is **outside Aestrum's loop entirely.** Time, magic, and memory all behave normally.

## Approach From the Road

The road runs east through the foothill forest. A narrow side-path branches off to the north, marked at the roadside by a **flat stone with a small carved crescent** at ankle-height — easy to miss if you are not looking, easy to find if you are. The side-path winds about a hundred feet through trees and emerges into the clearing.

The clearing itself is small — perhaps fifty feet across — with the hut at its center and the surrounding forest pressing in on three sides. The fourth side, west, opens slightly toward the road. A wisp of woodsmoke from the chimney is usually visible from the road in cool weather.

## The Hut — Exterior

A single-story log-and-thatch structure, smaller than it looks from the road because the trees hide its proportions. Weathered grey timber walls, a steeply pitched thatched roof patched in several places, a stone chimney rising from the north side. The door faces the path.

**Around the hut:**

- **A small kitchen garden** along the south wall — herbs, root vegetables, a few stubborn tomatoes in season. Tidy, productive, and modest.
- **A woodpile** under a lean-to against the east wall, well-stocked.
- **A small chicken coop** at the rear, three hens and (sometimes) a rooster. Eggs.
- **A hand-pump well** near the path approach, with a stone basin.
- **No outbuildings beyond these.** No barn, no workshop separated from the hut, no guest cabin. Travelers stay inside or under the lean-to depending on the size of the party and the weather.

## The Hut — Interior

A single room. Roughly twenty feet by fifteen feet. The space is **functional, clean, and spare** — Jiasha doesn't want for much and doesn't have much.

**The fittings:**

- **A stone hearth** on the north wall with a small iron stove built into it. A kettle is usually on. A cooking pot hangs from a swing-arm above the coals.
- **A heavy wooden table** at the center, six wooden chairs arranged around it. The table is older than the hut and was clearly hauled in piece by piece.
- **A sleeping pallet** in the southeast corner — Jiasha's. A plain wool blanket, a folded change of clothes on a chair beside it. No headboard, no curtain.
- **Shelves along the west wall** holding what she owns: a few clay pots and jars, a small stack of books (devotional, herbalist, almanac), a sewing kit, a wooden bowl of dried herbs, a candle stand. Nothing decorative.
- **A small wooden altar** in the northeast corner. A simple carved crescent on a stand, a candle in front of it, an offering bowl with a few coins and a sprig of dried something. **Selûne's symbol is the only religious iconography in the hut, and the altar is the only place it appears.** It is not hidden, but it is also not the centerpiece of the room — the table and hearth occupy that space.
- **A small workbench** along the east wall under the window. Carving tools, a few pieces of unworked stone/bone/wood, a small heat-lamp, a leather apron on a peg. **This is where she has spent the past several weeks making a specific set of moon amulets at Selûne's direction.** They are her only Moon Amulets — see DM Notes and [npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md).
- **Spare bedding** in a chest against the south wall: enough wool blankets and straw-stuffed bedrolls to lay out four or five guests on the floor in front of the hearth.

**Lighting:** two windows (one east, one south), both small and shuttered. Candles and the hearth for evening light. The hut is dim by design — Jiasha prefers it that way.

## Hosting Travelers

Jiasha receives traveling parties on the way to Aestrum as a quiet matter of routine. **The arrangement is standardized but not transactional:**

- She greets them at the path or the door. She is unhurried and unfussy.
- She offers shelter for the night — *"There's room. It's a night. The road will still be there tomorrow."*
- Bedding is laid out in front of the hearth. The table seats six; if the party is larger, some eat in shifts.
- Dinner is simple — whatever she has. Root vegetable stew, bread, eggs, herb tea. She does not ask travelers to contribute; if they offer coin or supplies, she accepts graciously without insisting.
- **She does not ask questions about who they are or why they are going.** The road east leads to Aestrum and that is, by itself, sufficient context for her to know what to offer.
- The amulet exchange happens in the evening — see [npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md) for the canonical handling, including timing, personalization, and what to do if a PC refuses.

**Layered against this:** Jiasha is not naive. She watches. She forms quiet impressions of every party that passes through her hut, and those impressions will color her future behavior with them. A party that is rude, threatening, or visibly cruel to her or to each other will receive the amulets but will not receive the warmth — and Jiasha will carry the impression into Aestrum (if she ever crosses).

## Faction Reach

- **Selûne (acolyte presence):** the hut is Jiasha's hermitage and her workshop. She is the only active Selûne presence in this corner of Setland. Her connection to Selûne is intact while she is outside Aestrum; once inside, it is suppressed by Shar's regional architecture. See Jiasha's NPC file.
- **Setland (Malak du Leon):** nominal jurisdiction only. The duchy considers this stretch of road unremarkable; no patrols visit the hut and no tax collector has ever appeared. Jiasha pays no formal tithe.
- **Shar / Aestrum loop:** the hut is outside the boundary and not subject to loop effects. Shar has no operatives here at campaign start.

## Seed Secrets

- The carved crescent stone at the roadside path-junction is **far older than Jiasha** — older than the hut, older than the hut before this one, and older than the loop itself. The site has held a Selûne acolyte continuously for as long as anyone in the region remembers; **Jiasha is the latest in a quiet lineage stretching back centuries.** The current hut sits on the foundation of the previous one, which sat on the foundation of another. The lineage's succession mechanism is not documented anywhere Jiasha has been able to find — each acolyte's calling somehow places the next, and the work continues. Jiasha knows she is part of this line and that she is not the last. She does not know how she will know when she is done. See [npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md) for the canonical handling and DM-only authorial reserve.
- The workbench has, under its surface, a **shallow drawer Jiasha keeps locked.** Inside: the **written instructions Selûne gave her** for the amulet commission — the materials, the count, the deadline of "tonight." Jiasha cannot say whether she wrote them down or whether they appeared as she received the vision; she has been afraid to examine the handwriting too closely. She keeps the drawer locked because the instructions still feel sacred and private even now that the work is done.
- The altar's offering bowl contains a small folded note Jiasha has not opened. It arrived in the bowl one morning years ago — left there overnight by someone she did not see. She has kept it sealed since. *(Author's reserve: contents up to the DM. Could be a Selûne blessing, a Shar curse, or something else entirely.)*

## DM Notes

- **Campaign-running content lives in Jiasha's NPC file, not here.** The overnight stop, the moon amulet exchange (timing, personalization rules, what happens if refused), the rain-as-motivator, and the tier discipline if the party turns on her — all of that lives in [npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md) under the "First Encounter — At Her Hut Before Aestrum" section. This file describes the building; that file describes what happens in it.
- **The hut is austere by deliberate character choice, not poverty.** Jiasha could ask Selûne for more or accept more from travelers; she has chosen the spare life. Hold this in narration. The hut should never feel like deprivation. It should feel like discipline.
- **Selûne iconography is present but not loud.** The altar in the corner, the crescent on the path-stone. Nothing else. Jiasha is devout in private, not publicly. A PC who scans the room casually may not register the religious context; a PC who looks at the altar will.
- **The amulet workbench is a discoverable detail.** A PC who looks at the workbench during the visit sees the tools, the unworked materials, and the in-progress or just-completed amulets, and can correctly infer that Jiasha made them herself — she is not distributing pre-made artifacts from elsewhere. This is fine to surface. **What is not immediately obvious is that this is the only set of amulets she has ever made**, and that they are specifically for this party at Selûne's direction. A PC who asks gets the answer; she will not volunteer it.
- **The locked ledger drawer is a Tier-2 reach.** Picking the lock or rifling the workbench without permission is a clear violation of hospitality. Jiasha will notice and will, depending on the rest of the party's behavior, either gently ask why or stop offering hospitality going forward.
- **The hut burns mid-Chapter-1, off-screen.** [Mieke]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/mieke.md)'s Black Arrows-contracted band (pursuing the Galadiil bounty) arrives at the hut after the party has crossed into Aestrum — at least one full day later in timeline A, or after a road-encounter chain in timeline B (see [the_abduction_of_jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/the_abduction_of_jiasha.md)). They seize Jiasha as a guide, burn the hut to deny pursuit and erase evidence, and cross with her into Aestrum. This is a canonical event that fires regardless of party action. When it fires in a given playthrough, the burned state should be recorded in `saved/jiasha_hut.md` (overlay) — the base file remains intact-hut. The party may later discover the burned ruin when they attempt to leave Aestrum and find the boundary closed.
- **Chapter 2 state.** By Chapter 2 the hut has been burned for some time AND the surrounding foothills have been clear-cut. Both effects compound. Hut-specific Chapter 2 content lives in the corridor overlay at [chapter_2/setland_east_corridor.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_2/setland_east_corridor.md). A separate `chapter_2/jiasha_hut.md` is not necessary unless future authoring requires it.
- **Tier discipline.** Targeting Jiasha or the hut while she is present is Tier 2, not Tier 3 — see [npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md) and [rules/consequences.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/consequences.md). The hut is a non-canon-critical structure. The party can burn it themselves if they choose; the world reacts proportionally (Selûne's quiet displeasure, the loss of Jiasha's services, eventual notice from someone who cared about the place).
- **The hut is not a fortress.** It will not survive a serious attack. The walls are log-and-thatch; the door is a wooden plank with an iron latch; the windows are unshuttered glass. A party that wants to defend it can, but the building is not the defense.
- **Snapshot positioning — why the overnight matters mechanically.** The hut sits 200 feet outside the Aestrum boundary, on Setland soil. A party that spends the night here does *not* receive their snapshot that night (no midnight inside Aestrum). They cross into Aestrum the following morning and have a full day of travel before their first non-dead-zone midnight catches them — and that first midnight's location becomes their permanent reset point per [rules/snapshot.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/snapshot.md). Staying at Jiasha's is therefore not just a moon-amulet handoff and a warm meal: it is the campaign's built-in opportunity to push the snapshot's reset point deeper into the duchy, away from the western border. A party that declines the overnight and pushes into Aestrum the same evening will likely be snapshotted near the boundary or in Duskwall — a usable but materially worse reset point. Do not surface this rationale out-of-character unless a PC has IC reason to know (Jiasha may suggest the overnight plainly per her standard offer; she will not explain mechanics).
