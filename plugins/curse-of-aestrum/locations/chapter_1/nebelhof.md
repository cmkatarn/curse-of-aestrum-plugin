## Overview

Small fog-bound hamlet in the upper valleys of the Duchy of Mikaelvad, several days' travel west of Mireval along the higher trade trails. Four to five subsistence households — goats, sheep, terraced barley plots cut into the hillside, a single forge that doubles as smith and tinker. No temple. No inn; travelers, when they come, sleep in the forge's outbuilding for a copper and are not asked their business.

The hamlet sits high enough that Mikaelvad's persistent valley fog never fully lifts here. Visibility above a few hundred yards is unusual. Above the village the hills disappear into white by midmorning and stay that way until sundown.

## What's Here

- **The track.** A single packed-dirt road runs through the hamlet and out the upper end toward stone-walled goat pasture. It is the village's only formal feature.
- **The forge.** Run by a family that has held it for three generations. Shoes the goats, repairs farm tools, occasionally re-forges blades for travelers from Tannmark a half-day's walk below. The outbuilding behind the forge is the village's only guest accommodation.
- **The barley plots.** Terraced into the south-facing hillside above the houses. The harvest is modest; most of it stays in Nebelhof. What surplus exists goes down to Tannmark twice a year by goat-cart.
- **The upper pasture.** Stone-walled goat enclosures stretching up into the fog line. Past the upper wall the trail continues into rougher country — hill paths, a few abandoned shepherds' huts, and one stone cottage on a shelf about half a day's climb above the village.

## The Hill Cottage

Stone walls, slate roof, two small windows, a low door, a root cellar dug into the hill behind. Goat pasture below stretches down toward Nebelhof's upper fields. A solitary's dwelling — the kind of place a recluse takes when they want distance from the village below. Whether it is currently tenanted, recently vacated, or long empty is a DM dial set per playthrough.

The villagers leave the cottage and its occupants alone as a matter of habit — they pay whoever lives up there no particular regard, present or absent. A cottage left behind would hold whatever its tenant could not carry: the leavings of a quiet, private life.

## The Villagers

Quiet. Suspicious of outsiders in the practical rather than hostile way. Uninterested in either Mikaelvad's politics or Nortmunde's broader affairs. The Duke of Mikaelvad is a distant rumor. Tannmark is the closest market and the limit of most villagers' lived geography.

## What Is Not Here

- **No temple.** No resident priest. No one with the training or inclination to detect what walks among them.
- **No Shar pipeline.** The duchy-wide Shar operation through Duke Holst ([factions/chapter_1/shar.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/shar.md)) does not reach this far up the valley. Nebelhof is too small and too remote to matter to that work.
- **No fixed road to Mireval.** The trail down to Tannmark connects to the broader Mikaelvad road network from there. Travel directly between Nebelhof and the duchy capital is not practical.

## Why It Matters

Nebelhof is a place where a person who does not want to be looked at can live and be left mostly alone. The fog is functional cover; the village's smallness is functional anonymity; the absence of clerical presence means a creature whose nature would be visible to trained eyes can pass unmarked. It is the kind of place vampires, dhampir, fugitives, and the unwilling-recluse end up when they have stopped looking for somewhere better and started looking for somewhere quieter.

If refugees of that kind exist anywhere in Mikaelvad — fugitives, recluses, creatures whose nature would be visible to trained eyes — the upper valleys are the kind of place they end up.
