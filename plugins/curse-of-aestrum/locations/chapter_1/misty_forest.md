## Overview

A large, dense forest occupying a significant stretch of Aestrum's interior. The mist is consistent and sourceless — it does not lift in the afternoon, does not thin at the edges, does not respond to wind. The trees are old enough that their canopies have closed off most of the sky. Navigation is disorienting without a reference point. The forest does not feel hostile, exactly. It feels occupied.

## Geography

The forest's key landmarks fall along a clear **west-to-east** line. Running west to east:

- **The 4WI ↔ Rockwood road** — the maintained road along the forest's western edge.
- **The hunting trail and mercenary camp** — a spur leaving the road and running east into the trees. The trail is always there, but stands **empty unless Jiasha has been taken captive and brought into the Duchy;** when she has, [Mieke's mercenary camp]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/mieke_mercenary_camp.md) — the Black Arrows-contracted band holding her — makes camp on it. When occupied it is the nearest thing to habitual *trespassers* on Rowan's ground: roughly a quarter-mile **west of the Shar shrine,** on the trail between the shrine and the road. The camp resets each morning with the loop. See [the_abduction_of_jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/the_abduction_of_jiasha.md).
- **The Shar shrine** (see Key Areas) — deliberately ~100 yards **west of the dead zone** (Shar cannot hear prayers made within an antimagic field; the distance matters).
- **The dead zone** — a magic-immune pocket, detectable at its boundary but unmarked; the loop's reset does not penetrate. Rowan can shelter here if forced off his estate anchor, but by default returns to the Deckard Estate to sleep (see *Rowan's displacement* in DM Notes).
- **The Perisdottir path** — the easternmost feature, the walking-path / road-spur running to Aliss Perisdottir's house.

**North to south:** the 4WI road runs along the **north** (out to the Perisdottir spur); the **dead zone, shrine, and hunting trail sit at roughly the same latitude** in the middle band; and to the **south** stands the great tree — older and larger than anything around it, marked by an orange flower growth at its base (Akadi's Abarkas and the Caliconia flower were found here; **Stripe's hunting grounds**).

**East:** Spider-infested territory stretches from the forest's eastern interior all the way to the Moonmaiden River — not a single nest but a claimed territory spanning the full eastern corridor.

## Key Areas

**Aliss Perisdottir's House:** A three-story stone house at the forest's center, built with tight-fitted mortar-free stonework characteristic of Dalihad craftsmanship — not locally built, and visibly out of place. Ground floor is her husband's smithy; second floor is kitchen and dining room; third floor is her bedroom and psychic parlor where she receives clients.

She is cycle-aware and has been since shortly after the loop began. She keeps meticulous notes on every client — stored in a magical dead box immune to the daily reset — and uses the loop to expand her clientele. Each day she gains new customers who have no memory of prior visits. She recognized the party as newcomers immediately and identified them as the ones destined to remove the curse. This is genuine foresight, not flattery.

Her husband is loop-aware only because she told him. He does not speak of it.

**The Hunting Trail:** A spur leaving the 4WI ↔ Rockwood maintained road and running east into the forest interior, west of the Shar shrine. Used historically by hunters and travelers. It stands empty unless Jiasha has been taken captive in the Duchy; when she has, it holds [Mieke's mercenary camp]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/mieke_mercenary_camp.md), roughly a quarter-mile west of the shrine, between shrine and road. Rowan considers the trail and its surrounding area his territory and has not yet moved against the camp, but proximity to his hunting range makes the camp vulnerable if his cycle is disrupted (see [Rowan's displaced-position note](#dm-notes) and [the_abduction_of_jiasha.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/the_abduction_of_jiasha.md) DM Notes for the "Rowan displaced → camp slaughtered" branch).

**The Spider Territory:** The eastern portion of the forest, from roughly the center line to the Moonmaiden River, belongs to a giant spider infestation. Not a single nest — a claimed and inhabited territory. The river forms the eastern boundary. Travelers moving through the forest's eastern corridor move through this territory.

**The Dead Zone:** Northwest of Aliss's house — relative north and relative west of it, just west of the Perisdottir path (see Geography). The same qualities as the Deckard Estate: a slight change in the air, a stillness, a torch that burns without flicker. All magic fails within it. The loop's reset does not penetrate. By default Rowan does **not** sleep here — his anchor is the Deckard Estate, and he returns there each night. If he is forced off that anchor (see *Rowan's displacement* in DM Notes), this dead zone becomes his fallback: it preserves his memory and suppresses his lycanthropy, but it is not home.

**The Shar Shrine:** Approximately 100 yards west of the dead zone boundary — outside it deliberately. A ruined structure of dark stone with only one wall still standing. Two stained glass windows remain in that wall, both stained deep magenta — intact in a structure that has lost everything else. The effigy Rowan built occupies the center of the ruin: a crude personal replacement for the original Shar statue Hati Heldrivver relocated to the Duskwall shrine on Shar's behalf. The resentment of losing the original is visible in the construction.

Rowan prays here each morning, facing the effigy. The shrine's position outside the dead zone is what makes the prayer functional — Shar cannot hear anything spoken within an antimagic field. He does not know this prayer sustains the loop.

## Rumors

*Safe to surface in any conversation about the Misty Forest. Does not reference the Great Tree or its location.*

- Travelers who've gone too deep into the forest sometimes come back describing sounds they can't place — silk dragging on bark, movement in the canopy too large for birds. Most don't go back to check.
- The spider territory in the east is known to locals. Anyone familiar with Aestrum will advise against the eastern corridor without a specific reason to cross it.
- Aliss Perisdottir is a known quantity — the fortune teller in the forest. People in Duskwall know she exists and that she charges for readings. Her accuracy is a matter of local debate.

## Faction Reach

- **Shar:** The shrine is her anchor point in the forest. Rowan operates as an unmanaged asset — she has never directed him toward Miri, but has never redirected him away.
- **Spiders:** The eastern forest is theirs.

## DM Notes

**Permanent removal mechanic:** Rowan resets each midnight unless his corpse is inside a dead zone by then — the loop's resurrection cannot reach within one. He does not need to die inside the dead zone. The party can kill him anywhere and move the body before midnight. Both the Misty Forest dead zone and the Deckard Estate dead zone are viable. The constraint is time.

**Destroying the effigy:** Breaking the effigy before Rowan's morning prayer severs one of two loop anchors. The effigy's position outside the dead zone is what makes it functional as an anchor — magic-based prayer cannot be heard from within an antimagic field. Destroying the effigy does not stop Rowan from praying, but Shar cannot draw on a broken anchor. See `{{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/quests/chapter_1/breaking_the_cycle.md`.

**Rowan's werewolf nature is NOT seeded for discovery here — do not hint it at this location.** There is **no** ambient wolf-spoor to find at the shrine or in the surrounding wood:

- Rowan has had **no reason to transform in a long while,** so there are **no fresh tracks** to read; and any sign there ever was would reset with the cycle regardless.
- There is **no** "wolf-prints become boot-prints" trail, and there must never be one — a transformation destroys worn clothing and boots and does not conjure new ones, so such a paw-to-boot track-boundary is physically impossible and must not be narrated.
- His lycanthropy (and its dead-zone suppression) is **DM-facing canon** — real, but revealed only through means the campaign authors *deliberately* (a direct encounter, a knowing NPC choosing to tell it, etc.), **never** as physical spoor a party stumbles on at this location. Examining the shrine ground yields the shrine (a Shar site: the crude effigy, the ruin, the kneeling-earth) — not the wolf.

**Aliss and the party:** Her identification of the party as cycle-breakers is genuine foresight. She wants the loop broken more than almost anyone in Aestrum — she is a seer whose gift depends on a future that never arrives. Her notes in the dead box represent years of accumulated intelligence on Aestrum's residents. She may be the single best informational resource in the region if the party thinks to ask the right questions.

**Rowan's displacement (conditional — not the default state):** Rowan is anchored to and sleeps at the Deckard Estate; that dead zone is his true anchor. *If a party occupies the estate* and forces him off it, he is driven to shelter in this Misty Forest dead zone instead — where his memory is still preserved but he is off-rhythm, more volatile, and aware that someone has taken the one place he was fully himself. This is a triggered branch, **not** baseline. A campaign in which it has fired records the actualized displacement in that campaign's `{{PROJECT_ROOT}}/campaign_state/<C>/locations/saved/misty_forest.md` overlay; canon here stays at the default (estate-anchored).
