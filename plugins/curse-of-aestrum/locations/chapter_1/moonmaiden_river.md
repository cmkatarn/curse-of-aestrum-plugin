## Overview

Aestrum's river, running **south to north** through the duchy. Its source is the pond
at [Moonmaiden Falls]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/moonmaiden_falls.md) outside Dunleaven — fed by freshwater
springs high on the slopes of Mount Mumandad, not by Runnaheim snowmelt, despite
older notes to the contrary. From the pond it runs north along the eastern edge of
the [Misty Forest]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/misty_forest.md).

"Moonmaiden" is a poetic title of Selûne. The name predates the current divine
conflict and carries no implication about the shrine's present state.

## Course

- **Source** — the pond beneath Moonmaiden Falls, a short walk south of Dunleaven.
- **Southern leg** — runs parallel to the road between Tine Cross and Dunleaven; see
  [routes.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/routes.md) for the segment's distances and hazards.
- **Northern run** — along the Misty Forest's eastern edge, then north through the
  duchy.

## Crossings

The **dilapidated bridge** at Dunleaven is the named crossing and has been out for
years — carts must ford or turn back (see [dunleaven.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/dunleaven.md)). A **ford
upriver near Dunleaven** is the fallback crossing.

## Boundary Function

The river is the **eastern boundary of the Misty Forest's spider territory**. The
infestation's claimed range runs from roughly the forest's center line east to the
water and stops there. A party moving up the forest's eastern corridor is moving
inside that territory until they reach the bank.

## Where It Appears

The river is drawn on the **Aestrum Regional Map** sold at the Salient Cartographer
in Duskwall — source at the falls outside Dunleaven, running north along the Misty
Forest's eastern edge.
