## Overview

Ex-Black Arrow tiefling rogue. Came to Aestrum specifically to find Theren Elakian using the Bloodline Compass, after witnessing Malak's conscription decree and overhearing Roland Elakian and Quellenna discussing the expedition. Ex-Black Arrow status now disclosed to the party. Has no Moon Amulet and relies on sheltering in dead zones during resets to preserve memory.

## Background

Closest friend: Emanthur "Manny" Wallerbridge in Setland (enchanter, Manny's Mysterium) — they have known each other for nearly twenty years. Manny provided the Bloodline Compass before she left for Aestrum.

**Castle infiltration (Day 0 — Kythorn 25):** Sage was in Setland city when the King's death was announced and Malak immediately declared military preparations. She spotted Roland alongside the Duke entering the castle and followed, disguised as a conscript. She overheard Roland and Quellenna: Quellenna pressing Roland to support the march, noting the expedition had been in Aestrum for over a week with no word. Roland dismissed concern for Theren, calling the group "disposable and doomed to fail," while revealing he had a son on the expedition. A third person was present — a pale, compact woman with red hair cut jaw-length and pulled back, standing with them. She went unnamed. Sage did not hear her speak. (This was Aidra Calemthor.) Sage broke off and procured the Bloodline Compass from Manny before departing.

**Route to Aestrum:** Disguised as a conscript to march with an advance contingent of Malak's forces toward Aestrum. At the frontline, swapped to a woodcutter disguise and slipped away into the forest.

**Setland soldiers killed:** Moving through the forest toward Aestrum, Sage came across the ruins of Jiasha's hut and spent the night there. In the morning she was found by two Setland scouts. She killed both — if either reported back, Roland would have identified her. The party does not know this. The two scouts are dead in the forest near Jiasha's ruins.

**How she joined the party:** Sage encountered a large wolf (Rowan Deckard in werewolf form) in the clearing near the Misty Forest Shar shrine and wisely avoided it. She smelled brimstone from Belmita's angel summon during the Stripe fight and followed it, finding the party over Stripe's slain body.

**How the Bloodline Compass works for her:** Keyed to "Elakian" — it pointed toward Roland initially. She physically walked away from him until the compass reoriented toward Theren, then followed it into Aestrum. She confirmed Theren's identity by walking among the party — he was the only half-elf present.

**Intel she brought the party:** Malak's military buildup and conscription, Roland Elakian's open alliance with the Duke, the impending invasion of Aestrum, and Roland's private opinion that the expedition was "disposable." The party relayed the "disposable" quote to Theren; Theren believes his father said it.

**The Belmita connection:** Sage overheard Quellenna mention reaching out to the Black Arrows for "special assistance." She has tied this to Theren via his wrist tattoo identifying him as a Black Arrow, but does not know what Quellenna's "special assistance" entailed — the Galadiil bounty Theren carries is known only to Theren and his parents.

Has a cat named Ms. Squeaky who accompanies her in a sling. No magical significance established.

## Appearance

- **Skin:** Purple
- **Hair:** Dark, near-black; shoulder length or slightly shorter
- **Eyes:** Brown
- **Build:** Medium, athletic
- **Horns:** Protrude from higher on the forehead (not the temples), follow the curvature of the skull upward, terminate pointing straight up — no curl or forward hook. Shorter than most artistic depictions. When hooded, two small points push up through the fabric without poking through.
- **Tail:** Present; tucked under the cloak when hooded.
- **Cloak:** Hooded, earthen colors (browns, grey-greens) for forest camouflage. Hood worn up in populated or unfamiliar spaces.
- **Gear:** Close-fitting adventure gear beneath the cloak — not armor. Form-fitting by necessity, not fashion.
- **Sling:** Light green blanket worn as a sling across the chest; Ms. Squeaky sleeps in it while traveling.
- **Tattoos:** Thorny vines on the left side of the neck — visible to anyone talking with her in reasonable light when her hood is down. Underneath the vines: a Black Arrow tattoo (the original mark, covered when she deserted). Discernible to someone who knows what to look for, if she lets her guard down long enough. **No one in the party has seen the underlying Black Arrow tattoo yet.**
- **Weapons:** Twin daggers (purchased from Lasimir in Setland)

## Behavioral Profile

- Ex-Black Arrow with a standing bounty in Setland. Cannot safely return. The party does not know this.
- Came to Aestrum with a specific mission — find Theren — and has executed it methodically. Her connection to him is professional and mission-based, not personal.
- Made the connection between Hylea's Mead's documented contraceptive properties and Duke Evandur insisting Galadiil finish her tea before leaving the table.
- Knows Erethezra signal language well enough to recognize it embedded in Rick Hastley's shanty — this is advanced but not impossible knowledge for someone at her level of Black Arrow experience.
- Player-driven: her choices in moments of moral weight are left entirely to her player. Do not predetermine her decisions.

## DM Notes

- **Deserter status:** Sage deserted the Black Arrows. Standing bounty on her in Setland — Lasimir threatened to report her and she paid his price. Not safe returning to Setland. The party does not know she is a deserter or that a bounty exists.
- Does not know Theren from prior Black Arrow history. She knew Roland had a son on the expedition; she did not know which member he was until she arrived.
- Knows The Shrikes as a gang that collapsed ~10 years ago in Setland. Does not know they operate in Duskwall under Luca/Lucretius.
- Was transported to Shar's Antechamber during the second party visit (with Zephyra, Thag, and Miri). Carries Shar's Antechamber pressure — a sensing/resonance mark, not a damage condition. She can feel Shar's presence when it manifests nearby (e.g., Miri's Debt disc).
- Whether she understands the full Quellenna → Elakian → Theren bounty chain is undeveloped. She knows Quellenna asked the Black Arrows for "special assistance" and that Theren is a Black Arrow, but does not know the Galadiil bounty is what Quellenna requested or that Theren is the contracted hunter.
