## Overview

Son of Roland Elakian, patriarch of The Black Arrows. Arrived on the Aestrum tax expedition as an official Setland agent, carrying an unofficial personal mission: a bounty on Duchess Galadiil Ilphekiir (right eye and right hand, 1,000 gold) assigned by his father as a test of readiness for promotion. His identity as a Black Arrow and Roland's son is concealed at sign-on.

## Background

Last saw Roland at the private pre-departure briefing where Roland delivered the Galadiil bounty (Kythorn 11, 1490 DR — the day the expedition departed).

Theren signed onto the expedition with the bounty as his real assignment and the tax cover as the vehicle. **His operational model was: get in, find Galadiil, do the work, slip out with the column.** He had no forecast of the cover failing and held no shared strategic pessimism with Roland — when he signed, the cover looked sound to him and Roland did not say otherwise. Roland's **"disposable and doomed to fail"** framing formed only after Roland reviewed the expedition's roster; it is Roland's racial contempt for the party's composition expressed as operational forecast, not a judgment Roland and Theren held together.

**The bounty:** Roland summoned Theren to a private meeting before departure. The Galadiil bounty (1,000 gold, right eye and right hand including heirloom ring) had arrived via a Charnelhold contact commissioned by Quellenna. Roland presented it as an assignment and a test. The official tax expedition was Roland's cover to get Theren an audience that would include the Duchess. Roland warned him that the contract was open and others might pursue it. **Only three people know Theren holds this commission: Theren himself, Roland, and Theren's mother.** Theren has never disclosed it.

**The Shrikes knowledge gap:** Theren knows The Shrikes as a defunct gang from Setland's recent history. He does not know Lucretius survived *The Survivor*, infiltrated Setland's underworld, took over The Shrikes, then vanished when Aestrum's loop began in 1480 DR.

**Charnelhold knowledge:** Through the Black Arrows' contracted use of Charnelhold's torture chambers, Theren has second-hand knowledge of the castle's foundations — literally built on the bones of Setland's conquered enemies. He did not learn this from official sources. The family talks shop at the dinner table. He has never been inside Charnelhold himself.

## Appearance

5'10". Athletic build. Blue eyes. Dark brown hair.

**Observational habit:** Watches hands, not faces or words. Grew up in Roland Elakian's household reading rooms for survival — learned early that hands reveal what expressions conceal. Where a hand rests. What it stays near.

## DM Notes

- The bounty chain: Quellenna commissioned through a Charnelhold contact → Black Arrows → Roland → Theren.
- The last time Theren spoke to Roland was shortly before the expedition departed — the private meeting where Roland gave him the bounty.
- Does not know how his father leads the Black Arrows, what Erethezra represents to him, or what Roland's actual relationship with Quellenna involves beyond surface alliance.
