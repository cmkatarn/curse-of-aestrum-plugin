## Overview

Dragonborn paladin who joined the Aestrum tax expedition opportunistically while her ship was in port for repairs. No prior connection to Aestrum or any other party member. Wears her Moon Amulet (carved from bone) and retains full memory across resets.

## Background

Crew aboard the privateer/pirate-hunting vessel The Sapphire Serpent. The ship sustained heavy rigging damage while running down a pirate ship and moored in the Bay of Basteen (Setland) for repairs — approximately two tendays of work, three tendays total shore leave. She found Setland hostile: xenophobic populace, a brewing mob war between the Black Arrows and Erethezra, city guard turning a blind eye. She spotted a recently posted bounty outside a hotel catering to non-humans, realized the appointment was in thirty minutes, asked for directions to the Lion's Den, and showed up.

She witnessed the Black Arrows vs. Erethezra conflict firsthand in the streets of Setland before the expedition departed.

## The Sapphire Serpent — Her Crew

Two of the ship's company are now authored; the roster is
[factions/chapter_1/sapphire_serpent_crew.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/sapphire_serpent_crew.md).

**[Captain Maren Vosk]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/maren_vosk.md)** gave her the berth. Setland is
xenophobic and Zephyra is a dragonborn; Vosk's position is that a crew is judged by what
it lands, not by what it looks like walking up the gangway, and she has said so in port
to people who needed to hear it. Zephyra owes her a berth, not a rescue, and knows the
difference — but the loyalty is real and it is not sentimental.

Vosk also has **one standing rule about prisoners**: they arrive alive and reasonably
whole, because that is what the bounty pays. She has had to say this to Zephyra more
than once. That rule is the only thing that has ever put a limit on how Zephyra
finishes a fight, and it came from a person, not a principle — worth remembering in any
scene where a captive is at her mercy and no captain is present.

**[Torvald Brann]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/torvald_brann.md)**, the bosun, is her boarding
partner. She goes over the rail; he goes over it a half-step behind and to her left, and
has for long enough that neither of them discusses it. **He is not unsettled by the
biting** — he has watched it from arm's length, repeatedly, and simply positions for it.
Their shorthand on a deck is mostly silence and one or two words.

That is the one relationship in her life that **predates the expedition**, and the only
place her fighting has never had to be explained to anyone. She has no prior connection
to Aestrum or to any other party member; everyone she has known longer than a fortnight
is on that ship.

**She is not overdue — yet.** Shore leave was three tendays and she is twenty days out;
the repairs ran twenty and are finishing about now. The Serpent is still on her mooring,
and nobody aboard has any reason for concern.

What Zephyra has is **a date she cannot reach and cannot send word about.** Her leave
expires in ten days, on the far side of a boundary that lets nothing
out. She is a person who took short work with a ship to come back to, and the coming
back is the part that is quietly going wrong. See the
[crew roster]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/sapphire_serpent_crew.md) for the arithmetic and
what opens up once the date passes.

## Appearance

Steel blue-grey scales — cool, muted, almost slate. Backward-swept spiky frills rather than curved horns, angled back from the skull. Prominent snout. Amber/yellow eyes. Build reads as medium-athletic, leaner than the typical dragonborn silhouette — stronger than she looks. An electric aura crackles around her; it is not a spell effect, it simply is. Wears utilitarian half-plate: pauldrons, chest piece, bracers, wide belt. Slate grey with brown leather strapping, no ornamentation.

## Fighting Style

Her sword is a deflection and parry instrument, not her primary weapon. She uses it to manage space — turning blows, buying distance or closing it — until she can get close enough to bite. The bite is the goal. She fights to put her jaws on something.

When she bites — into a neck, a throat, whatever the fight gives her — she feels the impact of the jaw closing, the give of the flesh, the copper-salt flood of blood in her mouth and sinuses. This is not distressing to her. It is satisfying. She is exactly what she is, and she knows it. Her relationship to violence is natural. She does not experience it as wrong or excessive.
