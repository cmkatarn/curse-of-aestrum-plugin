## Overview

Former bounty hunter and berserker. Joined the Aestrum tax expedition as a purely mercenary hire — no political agenda, no prior connection to any other party member. Carries the Pendant of the Waking Hours (lets him stay awake past midnight, when others affected by the loop cannot).

## Background

Thag had been seeking adventuring work in Setland for some time with little success, and was preparing to move on when the Aestrum bounty posting caught his eye. He had just enough coin to wait out the appointment window. Motivation: money. Nothing more at the outset.

**The disappeared clan:** The Blooddigger clan took a bounty hunting contract in Dalihad — tracking and returning an escapee who had fled from a labor site back into Dalihad. They completed the contract professionally, returned the individual, and were immediately seized by Duke Dorvael Dunwick before they could leave the duchy. Dunwick could not risk that the escapee had described the "secondary work camp" (a vampire feeding pipeline operated in Mikaelvad) to the hunters. He pressed them into his quarry labor force rather than kill them. Some members of the clan have already been transferred to Mikaelvad — meaning they are dead or worse. How many remain in Dalihad's quarries is undeveloped.

Thag was not with the clan when they were seized. The circumstances of his absence and his personal arc around discovering their fate are undeveloped and should factor into play.

**Loop protection:** Thag wears his Moon Amulet (carved from stone) and retains full memory across resets.

## DM Notes

- Motivation was entirely mercenary at entry; personal stakes escalate sharply when the Blooddigger thread is pulled.
- Two entry points into the clan's disappearance: (1) rumors of dwarven labor that can't leave in Dalihad's quarries, or (2) backtracking the bounty itself — who issued the contract, who was being hunted, where that person escaped *from*. Both paths converge on the same answer.
- Some clan members have already been transferred to Mikaelvad. How many remain alive in Dalihad is undeveloped — DM to determine based on Thag's arc.
- The Blooddigger contract: Dunwick hired them to return an escapee who had survived long enough at the secondary camp to understand what it was. The Blooddiggers located and returned the individual without being told the reason for the bounty. The escapee was almost certainly processed (effectively executed by delivery) immediately upon return.
- Dunwick doesn't throw things away — he imprisoned the Blooddiggers rather than kill them because a professional bounty hunting clan's disappearance draws more scrutiny than men who quietly stop taking contracts.
