# The Boundary Bubble

The bubble that holds snapshotted subjects inside Aestrum is **a property of the snapshot, not of the duchy boundary.** Its mechanics — what stops, what passes, what the subject can and cannot validly infer, and what removes the curse — are codified in [snapshot.md § Boundary-Bubble Mechanic]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/snapshot.md#boundary-bubble-mechanic), under the canon line that justifies the placement: *"The snapshot is the binding mechanism, not the boundary itself."*

This file exists as a discoverable filename for future authors searching for *"boundary bubble"* as a concept. It contains no mechanics of its own; the mechanics live with the snapshot, because the snapshot is the thing that does the binding.
