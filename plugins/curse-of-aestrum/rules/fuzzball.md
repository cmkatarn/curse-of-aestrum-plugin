## Overview

After the [revert]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/revert.md) fires, Shar plants a pattern-correcting sub-process in each subject — the **fuzzball**. It is a knot of thoughts: pulling on any one thread tightens the knot. Try a different thread — it tightens further. On and on, until the subject loses interest in what they were originally pursuing and gives up.

The fuzzball is not a wall. It is **friction that compounds until the subject stops pushing**.

The fuzzball's other job is **smoothing over the gap** between the subject's snapshot-state memory and the actual state of the world around them. A reverted subject wakes believing it is the morning they first arrived, but the world has moved on by years. The fuzzball quietly papers over the discontinuity — the subject does not question why they are where they are, why they recognize a face they have "never met," or why a routine they have "never performed" feels habitual.

---

## The Knot Metaphor — Key Behavioral Tell

A subject working through the fuzzball will:
- Appear to lose their train of thought mid-sentence.
- Change the subject without seeming to notice.
- Abruptly decide the matter isn't worth pursuing — not because they've concluded it's false, but because the thread stopped being pullable.

Run this as a play-at-the-table tell. Drift the NPC's attention. Have them ask about the weather. Have them suddenly remember an errand.

## Escalation by Exposure Level

The fuzzball's strength scales with how directly the subject is confronted with the anomaly.

### Hint
*Something seems subtly amiss — an overheard comment, a stranger who seems to know them, a door that shouldn't be open.*

The fuzzball activates. The subject's attention slides off the anomaly. They may register a vague unease, but decide whatever is wrong can't be that bad — and return to routine.

### Direct Statement
*Told outright that something is wrong — the loop, that they've met before, that it's actually a different year.*

The subject is more likely to wave it off as insanity than to engage seriously. The fuzzball reframes the **source of the information** as the problem, not the information itself. They may become mildly irritated or concerned for the speaker's wellbeing.

### Proof
*Shown physical evidence, demonstrated the loop mechanically, confronted with something that cannot be explained away.*

The fuzzball cannot fully suppress this. The smoothing-over breaks down: the subject begins actively questioning where they are, what they were doing, why a place or person feels familiar that they have no memory of, why their routine doesn't match their last clear recollection. (Note: the revert has erased everything since the snapshot, so there are no buried "real" memories surfacing — what surfaces is the **absence of explanation** for the subject's current situation, no longer being papered over.) **This state persists until their next reset.** At midnight, the revert erases the breakthrough entirely and the fuzzball reasserts clean.

A subject in proof-state is distressed, possibly non-functional, and will not find resolution before midnight takes them.

## Exceptions

- **Dead zones:** The fuzzball is a magical construct and does not function inside a [dead zone]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/dead_zones.md). Subjects inside one do not carry an active fuzzball while inside. The fuzzball reasserts the next time they revert outside a dead zone.
- **Moon Amulets:** Bearers are still planted with a fuzzball — the amulet blocks the [revert]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/rules/revert.md), not the fuzzball. However, because the amulet-bearer's memories already match the real state of the world, the fuzzball has nothing to smooth over and effectively does nothing. It only manifests when the bearer's experience diverges from reality, which for an unbroken amulet-bearer it does not. Treat the fuzzball as **benign** for amulet-bearers under normal conditions.

## Post-Loop Dissolution

When Shar's hold over Aestrum is broken, the fuzzballs are no longer sustained by her divine power. They dissolve over a short period.

NPCs will experience:
- A gradual lifting of the pattern-correction.
- Disorientation as they notice strangeness they have been sliding past — the unexplained familiarity of places and people, routines that feel habitual without origin, the gap between their snapshot-state memory and the world in front of them.
- Eventually, a full (and potentially overwhelming) confrontation with the discontinuity: their last clear memory is of arriving, and everything since is unaccounted for.

This is a roleplay opportunity post-resolution — the town slowly waking up.
