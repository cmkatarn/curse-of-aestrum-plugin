## Overview

Standard 5e death-save and stabilization rules apply. Settings with reset / loop / time-rewind / divine-intervention / resurrection-modifying mechanics may modify what happens *after* a creature dies — those modifications live in the game override, not in this file.

---

## Death Saves and Stabilization (RAW)

- A PC at 0 HP makes a death save each turn: 10+ succeeds, under 10 fails. Three failures = death. Three successes = stabilized at 0 HP. A nat 1 counts as two failures; a nat 20 restores 1 HP.
- Damage taken at 0 HP causes one failure (two on a crit).
- **Massive damage instant death** (RAW): a single hit that deals damage equal to or exceeding the PC's HP maximum *while they are at 0* kills them outright.
- **Massive damage from a hit that takes them to 0** (RAW): if a single hit's remaining damage after reducing the PC to 0 is equal to or exceeds their HP maximum, they die instantly without death saves.
- Stabilization via Medicine (DC 10), Spare the Dying, or any HP recovery.
- The player rolls their own death saves visibly in text. No secret death saves by default.

---

## Resurrection Magic

Standard 5e resurrection magic (Revivify, Raise Dead, Resurrection, True Resurrection, Reincarnate) functions per RAW. Spell slots, material components, and timing windows all apply as written.

---

## Setting Modifications

If the consuming game has reset / loop / restoration mechanics, the game override specifies how death interacts with them. Common shapes the override may address:

- "A dead PC is automatically restored at a specific event (next reset, next cycle, next dawn)."
- "Specific locations or conditions prevent automatic restoration."
- "Resurrection magic costs are refunded or modified at the next setting-defined cut."
- "Certain categories of creatures (familiars, summons, mounts, hirelings) are excluded from automatic restoration."
- "Memory of dying carries through restoration under specific conditions (an artifact, a class feature, a divine pact)."

Absent any such override, RAW applies: dead is dead unless successfully resurrected.

---

## Animals, Familiars, and Summons (RAW)

Familiars, Beast Master companions, Find Steed mounts, and similar bonded creatures follow their own RAW (re-summon via the relevant ritual/spell after a long rest; Find Familiar substitutes a new familiar after the previous one's death). Spell-summoned creatures end when the spell ends or when they're reduced to 0 HP per the spell's text.

Settings with reset mechanics may treat these categories differently — see the override.
