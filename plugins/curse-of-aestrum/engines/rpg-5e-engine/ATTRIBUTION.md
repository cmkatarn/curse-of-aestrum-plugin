## Scope of this notice

The SRD is one slice of the published ruleset, released under CC BY 4.0. It is not
the whole game. This notice attributes SRD-covered material; it does not, and
cannot, license material that falls outside the SRD, and no claim is made here
that every rule expressed in this engine is SRD-covered.

Two things follow, and both are open rather than settled:

- **Mechanics versus expression.** Game systems and procedures are generally
  understood to be unprotectable; the specific wording, tables, and arrangement
  that express them are not. Rules restated in independent wording stand on that
  distinction rather than on this attribution.
- **Content outside the SRD.** Subclasses, monsters, settings, and named product
  identity that never appeared in the SRD are not covered by CC BY 4.0. Any such
  material in this engine, or in a campaign built on it, needs its own answer.

**No audit has been performed.** Nobody has yet gone through this engine rule by
rule against the SRD to establish which material is SRD-covered, which is
independently worded mechanics, and which is neither. Until that happens, this
notice records what is attributed and on what basis — not a finding that the
engine's contents have been checked against it. Doing that audit, and scrubbing
or clearing anything that falls outside, is the open work this file stands in for.
