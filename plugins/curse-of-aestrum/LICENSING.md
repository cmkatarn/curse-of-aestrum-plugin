# Licensing

Two licenses, split by what the material is rather than by which repository it
came from.

| Part | License | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Engines** — `engines/prose-engine`, `engines/story-engine`, `engines/rpg-5e-engine`, `engines/fiction-host` | MIT (`LICENSE`) | They exist to be built on. Take them, write your own campaign, ship it however you like. |
| **Tooling** — `build/`, `scripts/` | MIT (`LICENSE`) | Ordinary code: the assembler, the save flush, the scene-context loader, the gate launcher. |
| **Campaign content** — `npcs/`, `locations/`, `lore/`, `quests/`, `items/`, `factions/`, `timelines/`, `party/`, `overrides/`, `rules/`, `skills/` | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`) | This is the writing. Play it, remix it, pass it on — but it isn't someone else's to sell. |

The short version: **the engines are a platform, the campaign is a book.**

## Third-party material

The 5e engine (`engines/rpg-5e-engine`, codename Bailly) implements the rules of
a tabletop system owned by Wizards of the Coast LLC. See
`engines/rpg-5e-engine/ATTRIBUTION.md` for what that means and what is
attributed.

The MIT and CC licenses here cover the author's own expression. Neither grants
rights in any third party's intellectual property, and nothing here is a claim to
own the underlying game system or any trademark.
