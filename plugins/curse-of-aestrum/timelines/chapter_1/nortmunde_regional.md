## ERA I — DEEP HISTORY
*Events predating the Greymist plot. Ordered by narrative logic; exact dates unestablished.*

---

**[~1482 DR — approximately 8 years before campaign]** — *Bertram Holst is turned vampire by the previous Duke of Mikaelvad* ⚓
Holst was turned by his predecessor — the circumstances of that relationship are unrecorded. He assumed the Dukedom shortly after. Has since operated through a stand-in for all daytime functions; only appears at night. Frozen at his apparent early-forties.

Now, 8 years later, the populace has begun murmuring that he looks remarkably good for his age. Holst recognizes the clock: he cannot hold this position much longer without someone drawing the right conclusion. He is quietly beginning to plan who leads Mikaelvad next — but wants to see the Nortmunde succession event through to its conclusion before he steps back. The succession outcome will shape what kind of duchy he's handing off.
↳ **Shar knows.** Filed in the Ledger. **Holst knows he is running out of runway.** No public knowledge of his nature — yet. The murmuring is not yet accusation.
*Cross-ref: Holst (NPC), Holst (Ledger)*

---

**[~Pre-1488 DR — approximate]** — *Holst/Dunwick feeding pipeline established*
Dunwick transfers "spent" Dalihad quarry laborers to a secondary work site in Mikaelvad, framed as lighter-duty employment. Holst receives transfers as a discrete food supply. No transferred worker has ever returned or sent correspondence.
↳ **Dunwick knows there is a vampire presence.** He has not asked further. **Holst knows everything.** **Shar knows.**
*Cross-ref: Dunwick (NPC, Ledger), Holst (NPC, Ledger)*

---

**[1477 DR — approximately 13 years before campaign]** — *Evandur Tallwood destroys The Survivor; Lucretius survives* ⚓
Evandur, age 24, organizes a funeral at sea for his father Lognan aboard the family ship *The Survivor*. He disembarks via dinghy before ordering the castle's siege ballistae to destroy the vessel, killing his brothers Lucretius, Jaugr, and Malston. Lucretius is blown clear of the explosion and survives — rescued by an as-yet unknown individual. Evandur believes all three brothers dead. *The Shrikes*, the organized crime group operating in Setland, will not yet know Lucretius exists for several years.
↳ **Evandur believes** all rivals eliminated. **Lucretius** begins his years-long infiltration of the Setland underworld. **Shar knows.** **No public knowledge** of Lucretius's survival.
*Cross-ref: Evandur Tallwood (NPC), Luca / Lucretius Tallwood (NPC), The Survivor (Vessel)*

---

**[~1479–1480 DR — approximately 10–11 years before campaign]** — *Lucretius takes over The Shrikes* ⚓
After roughly three years working through the Setland underworld, Lucretius consolidates control of The Shrikes — the same organized crime network that once operated in his family's city. He operates under the alias "Luca," his face heavily scarred from the explosion. He uses a Mirror of Illusion to present a clean disguise when conducting business. The Shrikes in Setland are now under his command, though the rank and file do not know who leads them.
↳ **Lucretius / Luca** is now in position. **Evandur does not know** his brother survived. **Roland Elakian and the Black Arrows** fill the vacuum The Shrikes leave when Luca vanishes into the loop.
*Cross-ref: Luca / Lucretius Tallwood (NPC), The Shrikes (Faction), Black Arrows (Organization)*

---

**[~1479 DR — approximately 11 years before campaign]** — *Quellenna Ilphekiir sent to Charnelhold; Galadiil placed in Aestrum* ⚓
Following Galadiil's substitution into Evandur Tallwood's marriage contract, Quellenna is displaced and sent to Setland as advisor to Duke Malak du Leon. She has never been permitted to leave. Simultaneously, Galadiil is placed inside Aestrum — far enough ahead of the cycle's creation (~Kythorn 1480 DR) to develop a visible identity and local history within the town, but not so long that his presence would invite scrutiny from the outside.

Both placements were deliberate and coordinated. Galadiil needed to be a resident before the lock, not inserted into it.
↳ **Quellenna considers Charnelhold a prison.** This is the origin of the escape tunnel. **Galadiil's cover identity** is established over the following ~year. **Shar's containment architecture** is now fully in place ahead of the loop's creation.
*Cross-ref: Quellenna (NPC), Galadiil (NPC), Charnelhold (Location), Aestrum (Location)*

---

**[~Pre-1488 DR — sometime after Quellenna's arrival]** — *Quellenna and Aidra's relationship begins*
Quellenna meets Aidra Calemthor, a Setland officer she identifies and later advocates to promote. The relationship develops. Quellenna builds the escape tunnel; its exit is in Aidra's house outside the castle walls.
↳ **Shar becomes aware.** Files it without deploying it.
*Cross-ref: Quellenna (NPC), Aidra (NPC), Charnelhold (Location), Ledger*

---

**[~Pre-1489 DR — approximate]** — *Blooddigger clan imprisoned in Dalihad*
A Mikaelvad laborer escaped and returned to Dalihad — the only known survivor long enough to understand the camp's nature. Dunwick hired the Blooddigger dwarven clan to locate and return the individual. After delivery, he had the Blooddiggers seized. They are in the quarries. Some have since been transferred to Mikaelvad.
↳ **Dunwick knows.** **Shar knows.** *(Any Blooddigger-tied party member's awareness of their clan's fate is per-instance — see saved overlay.)*
*Cross-ref: Dunwick (NPC, Ledger)*

## ERA II — THE AESTRUM LOOP
*Shar locks Aestrum in a daily reset. Ten years pass in the outside world.*

---

**[~late Mirtul 1480 DR — approximately two weeks before the lock]** — *Miri Amblecrown arrives in Aestrum* ⚓
Returning from a diplomatic mission to Cormyr — negotiating military backing for Duke Amblecrown's claim on the throne, a two-front deterrent against Setland — Miri stops in Duskwall and takes a room at [Sound Asleep]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/duskwall/sound_asleep.md). **She is not caught at the border.** She is simply present, and settled, when the loop closes a fortnight later; everything after her arrival unfolds as fast as Shar can move the remaining pieces into place.
**Her arrival is what forced the lock's timing.** Aestrum had been a patient piece in Shar's long game; word that a Chosen of Selûne would be passing through turned it into an opportunity with a deadline, and everything after unfolded at whatever speed the remaining pieces could be moved.
↳ **Cormyr never receives confirmation** of the promise she made there. **Duke Amblecrown** believes her missing from this point forward. The inn's register still shows the fortnight she had accumulated — it resets to that figure every morning and has never advanced since.
*Cross-ref: Miri Amblecrown (NPC), Sound Asleep (Location), The Loop (Key Lore)*

---

**[~Kythorn 11, 1480 DR — approximately Day −10 years]** — *Shar creates the Aestrum time loop* ⚓
Evandur Tallwood and all inhabitants present at cycle start are locked in a daily reset. Characters in the loop reset at midnight; they retain no memory of prior cycles unless they entered from outside. Externally, Aestrum goes silent — no taxes, no correspondence, no trade.

Motivation: tactical containment of Evandur; also a petty dig at Selûne, who had not interfered in Nortmunde and did not deserve it. **The timing, however, was not Shar's choosing.** Aestrum had been a patient piece under long consideration; word that a Chosen of Selûne would be travelling through escalated it, and the lock fell as fast as the remaining pieces could be moved — a fortnight after Miri Amblecrown arrived. See [`{{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/shar.md`]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/shar.md), *Why Aestrum — and Why Then*.

Residents experience **1480 DR** perpetually. The outside world is in **1490 DR**.
↳ **Shar knows** (she created it). **Selûne is aware** at some level; has not directly intervened. **No one else in Nortmunde knows the mechanism.** Aestrum simply went quiet.
*Cross-ref: Shar (NPC), Evandur (NPC), Aestrum (Location), The Loop (Key Lore)*

## ERA III — THE LONG ILLNESS
*315 days of slow poison. The King's decline misread as natural aging by everyone close to him.*

---

**[Day −315 — Eleasis 14, 1489 DR]** — *First Greymist administration* ⚓
Councive Drethnal begins adulterating the King's daily respiratory tonic with Greymist: a combination of three individually benign components (gout salve ingredient, respiratory sedative, binding compound). Colorless, tasteless, accumulates over months. The King's chronic respiratory weakness — the reason he takes the tonic — is the delivery vector. High summer. The illness will read as a natural autumn and winter decline.
↳ **Drethnal knows.** **Shar knows** (Drethnal is her embedded cultist). **No one else knows.**
*Cross-ref: Drethnal (NPC), Greymist (Item), The King's Maester (NPC)*

---

**[Day −300 — Eleasis 29, 1489 DR]** — *First noticeable symptoms appear* ⚓
King begins exhibiting fatigue, word-finding difficulty, and accelerating physical decline — approximately two weeks after first Greymist exposure. The King's Maester attributes everything to age and existing conditions.

*The Maester, when pressed by the King:*
> *"Of course you feel lethargic — you're old."*
> *"What about forgetting names and words?"*
> *"You're OLD."*

↳ **The Maester does not know.** He is a lifelong friend treating in good faith. **Drethnal knows.** **Shar knows.**
*Cross-ref: The King's Maester (NPC), Drethnal (NPC)*

---

**[Day −60 — Tarsakh 26, 1490 DR]** — *Veyra Sollin deduces the Greymist compound and sends warning* ⚓
Veyra, an aspiring Maester's apprentice and ingredient supplier, has been studying Drethnal's supply orders for her own professional development. She recognizes the combination as Greymist before the King dies. She sends a warning letter to Tarwick Grale bearing her company seal — specific, credible, with two months of lead time to act.
↳ **Veyra knows.** **Shar's network identifies her as a loose thread; she becomes a target.** **The letter is in transit.**
*Cross-ref: Veyra Sollin (NPC)*

---

**[Day −53 — Mirtul 2, 1490 DR]** — *Veyra's letter arrives at Tarwick's* ⚓
Seven days of transit. Letter sits unopened.
↳ **Tarwick has it but has not read it.**

---

**[Day −51 — Mirtul 4, 1490 DR]** — *Tarwick opens Veyra's letter; sends outsourced muscle* ⚓
Tarwick reads the letter. He calculates: confirmed poisoning requires a criminal investigation, suspended succession, and risks his removal from the Regency — which would expose his tower. Natural causes is tidier. He allows it. He sends contracted agents to silence Veyra the same day he reads it.
↳ **Tarwick holds complicity in the King's death.** This is Ledger Secret 2. **Veyra does not yet know she has been identified.** The King has 51 days left.
*Cross-ref: Tarwick Grale (NPC, Ledger), Veyra Sollin (NPC)*

---

**[Day −50 — Mirtul 5, 1490 DR]** — *Veyra sees men break into her shop; begins running* ⚓
Tarwick's agents arrive the day after he sends them. Veyra witnesses the break-in — she is not there when they arrive, or escapes before they reach her — and has been running under a false name since. She does not know for certain who sent them, though she has inferences. Safety destination: Skaarsdaam / Amblecrown territory.
↳ **Veyra knows she is being hunted.** **Shar's network is also tracking her.** **She does not know Tarwick did nothing about her warning.**
*Cross-ref: Veyra Sollin (NPC)*

Roland Elakian, leader of the Black Arrows gang, is given a notice: a bounty has been placed on **Galadiil Ilphekiir**, who is believed to be somewhere in or around Aestrum. Roland files this and begins considering how to use it — specifically whether to involve any Black Arrows assets attached to the imminent Aestrum expedition.
↳ **Roland knows.** **Galadiil does not know** — she is inside the loop. *(Bounty issuer is [TBD] — see pending.)*

**[Day −14 — Kythorn 11, 1490 DR]** — *Malak dispatches a tax-collection expedition to Aestrum* ⚓
Malak du Leon contracts an adventuring party (specific composition per playthrough; recorded in saved) to travel to Aestrum and retrieve a decade of unpaid taxes and tribute — the **inciting event of Chapter 1: The Murder of the Amber Crown**. The King of Nortmunde is alive at this moment, with 14 days remaining. The party will travel for several days before reaching the Aestrum border.
↳ **Malak's stated purpose** is tax collection. **His unstated purpose** — see next entry. *(What the party knows or does not know about any of this is per-instance — see saved.)*

---

**[Day −14 — Kythorn 11, 1490 DR]** — *Malak's invasion planning begins in secret* ⚓
The same day the expedition departs, Malak begins secret planning for military annexation of Aestrum. The expedition is partly a pretext: if they fail (likely) or succeed and bring tax wealth back (also useful), either outcome legitimizes military follow-up. Roland Elakian, having received the Galadiil bounty the day before, considers attaching a Black Arrows asset to the same expedition where possible.
↳ **Malak and Quellenna know** about the invasion planning. **Roland knows** about the Galadiil bounty and the parallel expedition. *(Whether Roland successfully plants an asset on this specific party is per-instance — see saved.)*
*Cross-ref: Malak du Leon (NPC), Quellenna (NPC), Roland Elakian (NPC)*

---

**[Day −7 — Kythorn 18, 1490 DR]** — *Quellenna learns the King's death is imminent; begins covert planning* ⚓
Quellenna receives intelligence through her network that the King of Nortmunde has days remaining. She moves immediately to Sagrona Teema, one of Malak's lesser advisors — a two-day working session, ostensibly collaborative, to develop a concrete invasion plan for Aestrum. While they work, she learns what angers him.
↳ **Quellenna knows** the King's death is imminent. **Sagrona Teema is being used** without understanding it. **Malak has not been informed** of the planning session. The King has 7 days remaining.
*Cross-ref: Quellenna Ilphekiir (NPC), Sagrona Teema (NPC), Malak du Leon (NPC)*

---

**[Day −5 — Kythorn 20, 1490 DR]** — *Malak's Secret War Council; Aestrum invasion plan decided* ⚓
Malak convenes a closed war council at Charnelhold. Present: Malak du Leon, Quellenna Ilphekiir, Aidra Calemthor, and lesser advisors Lorad Telman and Sagrona Teema.

In the hour before the council convenes, Quellenna provokes Sagrona — deliberately, precisely — and he enters the chamber furious. He informs her he is presenting the Aestrum invasion plan as his own. She protests visibly, for the room.

At the council:
- **Lorad Telman** proposes continued diplomacy — receives a scowl from Malak.
- **Sagrona Teema** presents the Aestrum invasion plan — politically coherent, militarily unsound.
- **Aidra Calemthor** proposes an immediate strike on Dalihad, laying out resources, staging, and supply logistics in full.
- **Quellenna Ilphekiir** hastily proposes a strike on Skaarsdam, finds a hole in her own proposal, withdraws it, then advocates for Sagrona's plan with modifications incorporating Aidra's military framework.

The council decides: Aestrum invasion, executed with Aidra's logistics. Sagrona Teema believes he won. Quellenna achieved the outcome she designed from Day −7 and left no fingerprints on the result.
↳ **All present know** the Aestrum invasion is decided. **Malak does not know** the plan originated with Quellenna. **Sagrona Teema believes** the plan is his. The King has 5 days remaining.
*Cross-ref: Malak du Leon (NPC), Quellenna Ilphekiir (NPC), Aidra Calemthor (NPC)*
