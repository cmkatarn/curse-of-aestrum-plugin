## Overview

A small Setland assassination expedition, trapped inside the cycle for ~10 years. The party should not be assumed to know any of what follows; they have only the observable evidence they uncover in play.

**This is not Setland's army.** Setland's standing force is [The Setland Army]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/setland_military.md) under Aidra Calemthor — a separate body, outside the loop, with ten years of history these operatives do not share. **The two would not recognize each other:** the expedition is frozen at the day it crossed, carrying orders a decade stale, while Malak believes it dead and the army has moved on around it. Do not run them as one organization, and do not let one's knowledge leak into the other.

## The Advance Expedition (trapped — established loop fixture)

A ~20-operative force dispatched ten years ago to assassinate Duke Evandur Tallwood, sent ahead of an army that was supposed to follow but never did. They are disguised as Duskwall guards — standard raven-motif outer gear over tunics bearing the Lion crest of Duke du Leon.

They entered Aestrum on the same day the cycle began. Because they survived until midnight inside Aestrum, they became part of the loop and have reset daily ever since. They carry Malak's original assassination orders; they have had no contact with Malak in a decade and carry nothing more recent than the day they crossed the border.

**Vault contingent.** A small group split off to attempt a "back door" into the castle via the vault maze. Two killed the Aestrum guards at the side entrance, took their uniforms, and have occupied those posts every day since (wearing Lion-crest tunics under the Aestrum guard uniforms). Three entered the maze and died inside before their first midnight — having never been snapshotted by the cycle, they have no reset point and are permanently dead. The Gelatinous Cube in The Drain digested one of them; the cube itself snapshots normally with its contents, so a lion shield persists inside it every cycle.

**Castle assault group.** The remainder are camped in the forest outside the castle. From 11:30 PM each night, Judith Asemyeer opens a Gate; they breach the castle and kill Evandur and Galadiil in the thirty-minute window before midnight. Killers and victims are swept into the reset together. This has been the nightly pattern for ten years.

**Chain of command behind the assault.** Shar → Hati Heldrivver → Judith Asemyeer. Judith does not know she is assisting a Setland military operation — she follows Hati's orders, and Hati follows Shar's.

## Malak's Posture

Malak believes the advance expedition was killed — he assumes Aestrum repelled the assassination attempt and severed communications, and he treats the duchies as in cold war. He has no idea the expedition is alive, trapped, or that it successfully completes its mission every single night.

**Ledger Secret — Malak du Leon Secret 2:** Malak has been planning military conquest of Aestrum for at least a decade. The original assassination plan, the chain of command, the intended mission scope, and the premeditated nature of the conquest all predate any legitimate succession claim. The trapped expedition still physically carries the original assassination orders. If the party makes contact with them, this is explosive.

## DM Notes

**Loop-unawareness of the trapped expedition.** The fuzzball suppresses any impulse to question repetition, missing comrades, or lack of mission progress. They wake each morning, execute their orders, reset, and never connect the pattern. They cannot be reasoned out of this from inside the loop alone.

**Death and the cycle inside Aestrum.** The vault maze is not a special place that defeats the cycle — anyone who dies anywhere in Aestrum is reset as long as the cycle has snapshotted them (i.e., they have survived at least one midnight inside Aestrum). The three permadead vault soldiers died before their first midnight, which is why they have no reset point. The same rule applies to anyone else — survive one midnight inside, and you're in the loop.

**The Void exception.** The only physical location in Aestrum that genuinely sits outside the cycle is The Void, inside the vault maze. The Void is a space within Shar's realm; subjects inside it are not within the cycle bubble and are immune to the reset. The doors of The Void, however, are *not* part of Shar's realm and reset normally. Depending on the locked state of each door at the moment of reset, a subject inside The Void can be trapped there until someone outside the Void opens a door — a particularly terrifying failure mode if the keyholder is not coming back.

**If the party makes contact with the trapped expedition.** Malak's assassination orders are physically in their possession. The mismatch between those orders and any current Setland narrative is enough to break the cold-war framing in any duchy that hears it.
