## Overview

A **centuries-long lineage of Selûne acolytes** keeping the threshold at Aestrum's
eastern boundary — one at a time, in a small hut some two hundred feet from the line.
[Jiasha]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/jiasha.md) is the current holder of the post and, as far
as canon records, its only named member ever.

**A roster of one, trivially complete.** The order is not an organization in the sense
the gangs are: it is a *post*, held by a single person, passed down.

## The Succession

She believes **the position passes** — that when she is done another will come, summoned
by Selûne in whatever quiet way Selûne summoned her. She does not know how she will know
when she is done. The lineage and its succession mechanism are the one piece of her
devotion she has **never doubted**, even through the long silence of Selûne's absence
inside Aestrum; the continuity is older than her own faith and does not require it.

That is worth holding onto when running her: the silence has wounded her faith, and it
has not touched her confidence in the line.

## What the Lineage Carries

Centuries of **observation from outside** — what successive holders of the post could
plausibly see of Aestrum from the boundary, transmitted forward. This is the source of
Jiasha's knowledge and also its hard limit: her understanding of Aestrum's *interior*
workings is sharply bounded, because no one whose memory survived the night was ever
available to tell the line anything.

The DM catch on her sheet is the test to apply: *whose memory of inside Aestrum could
plausibly have transmitted this to her or her lineage?* If the answer is nobody, she
does not know it.

## Open Slots — To Develop in Play

- **[name]** **Her predecessor has never been named**, nor has any earlier holder. A
  centuries-long line with exactly one name in it is a large reserve of unspent
  material — a predecessor's notes, a grave, a successor's arrival.
- **[relation]** **Is the Duskwall shrine part of this order?** The previous Selûne
  priestess there — who disappeared in the weeks before the lock and whose post Hati
  Heldrivver took — is a *second* unnamed Selûne figure, and nothing establishes
  whether she was of this lineage, ordinary town clergy, or unconnected. It matters:
  if she was of the line, then Shar's operation began by removing one of Jiasha's own,
  and Jiasha has no idea.

## DM Notes

Jiasha is **outside** the loop at the boundary and is not a snapshot subject; her
connection to Selûne is her foundation outside Aestrum and is suppressed entirely
within it. See her sheet for the knowledge-boundary discipline, which is unusually
strict — the lineage's centuries of watching is exactly the sort of thing that invites
her to know more than she can.
