## Overview

Setland's standing army — a large force under the command of
[Aidra Calemthor]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/aidra_calemthor.md), stationed in Setland and
ready to mobilize on Duke Malak du Leon's order. It has never entered Aestrum.

This army lives in the **outside world**: ten years of continuous service, ordinary
rotations, accumulated history, and a chain of command that has moved on repeatedly
since 1480.

## Not the Agents of Setland

**These are two different bodies and must not be run as one.** The
[Agents of Setland]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/agents_of_setland.md) are the ~20-operative advance expedition
that crossed into Aestrum the day the cycle began and has been trapped in it ever
since.

| | The Agents (in Aestrum) | The Army (in Setland) |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Inside the loop, reset daily | Setland, outside the loop |
| **When** | Frozen at the day they crossed, ~1480 | Ten years of subsequent history |
| **Orders** | Malak's original assassination orders, carried on their persons | Whatever Malak's current standing orders are |
| **Command** | No contact with Setland in a decade | Aidra Calemthor |

**They would not recognize each other.** The expedition was dispatched a decade ago
and is presumed dead; Malak believes Aestrum repelled the assassination and severed
communications. Anyone serving in the army today likely enlisted after the expedition
left, and the expedition's members know nothing and no one from the intervening ten
years. A meeting between them would be a meeting of strangers who happen to wear the
same crest — and whose crests are ten years apart in pattern.

## If the Army Marches

Default trajectory: reaches Aestrum's border roughly **two days** from launch, and
crosses inside in roughly **five days**, making permanent camp at the base of the road
back toward Setland — visible from Duskwall as a large smoke column. Once camped
inside Aestrum, **the camp becomes the army's loop reset position.**

## If It Crosses While the Cycle Is Active

The sudden influx of an invading army exceeds Shar's capacity to maintain the cycle
cleanly. She refuses to release it, so the strain produces **cascading failures** —
memory failures, teleportation failures, sleep failures. Her control begins to show
hairline fractures. Major destruction events within a given day's loop (the castle
being demolished, for instance) become genuinely difficult to reset and compound the
strain further.

## DM Notes

**Malak's posture.** He treats the duchies as being in a cold war and believes the
advance expedition dead. He has no idea it is alive, trapped, or that it successfully
completes its mission every single night. That belief is why no relief force was ever
sent, and why the army has spent a decade pointed at Aestrum without moving.

**On the commander.** Aidra executes the duchy's military affairs with professional
excellence and presents as wholly Malak's. Her sheet carries author-only state about
where her loyalty actually sits — read it before running her in any scene where the
army's orders are in question.
