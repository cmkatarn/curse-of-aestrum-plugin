## Overview

The company of *The Sapphire Serpent*, a **privateer sailing under letter of marque and
hunting pirates for the bounty**. She took heavy rigging damage running a pirate ship
down and is currently moored in the **Bay of Basteen**, in Setland, for roughly two
tendays of work — three tendays of shore leave for the crew.

That shore leave is how [Zephyra]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/party/zephyra_stormchaser.md) came to be in
Aestrum at all. She spotted a posted bounty outside a hotel catering to non-humans and
took the tax-expedition work opportunistically, with a ship to return to.

## The Clock

**Zephyra is not overdue yet — and that is the interesting part.**

Count it against her own numbers. She left the ship on **Nortmunde Day −14**; the
expedition crossed into Aestrum on **Day −8**; the campaign currently stands at
**Aestrum Day 13 — Nortmunde Day +6**, by the run's own mapping (Aestrum Day 12 =
Nortmunde Day +5 = Kythorn 30). That is **twenty days** away from the Serpent, against:

| | Length | Status at Aestrum Day 13 |
|---|---|---|
| **Repairs** | two tendays — 20 days | **due to finish now** |
| **Her shore leave** | three tendays — 30 days | **ten days left** |

So the Serpent is **still moored in the Bay of Basteen**, work still on her, and
Zephyra is comfortably inside her leave. Nobody aboard is worried, nobody has written
her off, and there is nothing yet to explain.

**What she has is a deadline she cannot meet and cannot send word about.** The hull is
sound as of about now; in ten days her leave expires; and she is inside a boundary that
lets nothing out. The pressure is not grief on the Serpent's deck — it is a date
approaching on hers.

Only once that date passes does the question open up: whether Vosk holds the mooring
past the season for a crew member who has never been late before, whether the ship
sails, or whether Torvald goes looking in the wrong city. **Not settled here** — but it
is not yet live either, and it should not be played as though it were.

*The clock above is `die_a_hero`'s. Another run stands at its own day and should redo
the arithmetic before making the Serpent live.*

## DM Notes

**The crew are not loop subjects.** They are in Setland, outside the boundary, living
through ordinary time while Zephyra repeats a day. However long the campaign's Aestrum
days take, the Serpent's world has moved on by exactly that much — this is the sharpest
outside-clock the party has attached to any of its members.

Per Zephyra's sheet the ship and crew are flagged for **chapter-2 development**; these
three files are the scaffolding for that, not a finished thread.
