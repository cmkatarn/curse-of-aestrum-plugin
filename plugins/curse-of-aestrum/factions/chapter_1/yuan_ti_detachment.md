## Overview

A small Yuan-Ti detachment holding the [Temple of Sathraza]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/nahamkate_temple.md) in the Nahamkate Desert, led by the priestess [Assaneela]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/assaneela.md). They arrived to two purposes that run together: Assaneela's own — recover the Scale of Ssath'razza, a relic taken north from this temple centuries ago by followers of Akadi — and the detachment's work, an ongoing transformation rite in the entry chamber, doctrine dedicated to Sathraza.

**They have no formal name.** Like [Mieke's band]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/mieke_band.md), that is what they are rather than an oversight: a working cohort sent to a site under its priestess, not an identity with a banner. To anyone who met them they would be Assaneela's people, or simply the Yuan-Ti at the temple.

## What They Want

- **The rite.** A transformation ceremony in the entry chamber, named in the temple's liturgical glyphs as a gift of ascension into Sathraza's form. It advances on a timer over five stages; if it completes, the restrained subject's arms become living snakes and he turns feral, then dies. The detachment reveres it as doctrine and will not allow it disturbed. *(They do not know the subject is the same restrained man every day, never completing — see the loop note below.)*
- **The Scale of Ssath'razza.** Assaneela's charge from Zehir: recover the Scale — or any lesser relic of Ssath'razza — taken from this temple long ago and carried north by Akadi's followers. She found the relic chamber stripped on arrival and has been searching the secured spaces since. The gem-room puzzle is the door; the [Mounted Ruby]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/items/chapter_1/misc_items.md) is the key she lacks.

## Reach — Set-Piece, Not a Recurring Faction

This detachment is a **set-piece**, consistent with the temple file's own [Faction Reach]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/nahamkate_temple.md): the temple is an active ritual site, not a settled base, and the Yuan-Ti present are not a recurring faction. **Zehir has no active presence in Aestrum beyond Assaneela**, and Sathraza none beyond this rite. The detachment does not send agents, hold territory, or act anywhere but here. If Assaneela escapes with the Scale or a lesser relic, that is a Chapter-2 hook, not an ongoing incursion.

## DM Notes

**Loop-bound.** Assaneela and the detachment arrived in Aestrum *after* the loop snapshot, reached the temple, took up residence, and were caught by the loop at the first midnight they spent there — that midnight is now their reset state. They do not know the loop exists. Each morning the rite begins again on the same restrained man; to them the work is simply ongoing, weeks in and weeks from done. Only a loop-aware party watching a second time understands what is repeating. See [Assaneela's sheet]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/npcs/chapter_1/assaneela.md) for her reset behavior and the archaeologist cover she runs from the top on every encounter.

**Combat.** So long as the rite is undisturbed and the party keeps Assaneela's arrangement, the detachment does not engage. If combat begins, every Yuan-Ti fights **except the Low Priest, who continues the rite** — and Assaneela first summons the Malison heavy from the gem room before joining herself. Disturbing the ceremony voids the arrangement and starts the fight. Stat the rank and file at their type (Malison, Pit Master; the Low Priest as a lesser priest); Assaneela's block is on her sheet.

**They are Assaneela's, not the temple's.** The bone pile in the entry chamber predates them — it was there when they arrived, and Assaneela cannot explain it. The detachment are not the temple's ancient keepers; they are a recent arrival performing their rite in a structure that long predates them.
