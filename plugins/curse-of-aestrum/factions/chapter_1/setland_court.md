## Overview

The ruling court of Setland, seated at [Charnelhold]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/locations/chapter_1/charnelhold.md):
Duke Malak du Leon, his royal advisor, and his military commander. It is the court
pursuing the Nortmunde throne, and — on the Duke's order — the invasion of Aestrum.

**A court is a maximally public body**, and this table says nothing anyone is hiding.
Which is the point of it: **every real secret at this court is an allegiance held
somewhere other than here**, and none of them is visible in this column.

- **Quellenna** serves [Shar]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/shar.md), which is on that roster and not this one.
  Malak does not know. Her political work and her devotion are the same work.
- **Aidra** is also a member of [the Setland Army]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/setland_military.md) — she commands
  it — and her sheet holds author-only state about where her deeper loyalty sits.
  **Malak does not know that either**; his own sheet names it as the second of his
  blind spots.
- **Quellenna and Aidra are in a long-term relationship**, conducted through a tunnel
  beneath the shared wall from Charnelhold to Aidra's house. That is a *relationship*,
  not a membership, and it stays on their sheets. It is also the one part of
  Quellenna's life she will not weaponize.

So: three members, all publicly known, of whom two are running something the third
cannot see. **Read this table for who sits in the room, never for who is loyal to
whom.**

## Not Members

**Roland Elakian is not part of this court.** The Black Arrows' patriarch is *openly
allied* with Malak, and Quellenna commissioned the Galadiil bounty through a
Charnelhold contact into his gang — but connection is not membership. He holds no
office, sits at no table, and answers to no one here. His roster is
[the Black Arrows]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/black_arrow.md).

The distinction matters because the alliance is public: someone watching Charnelhold
will see Roland come and go and conclude he is one of Malak's men. He is a contractor
with political cover, and he would tell you so himself.

## DM Notes

**Malak's blind spots are the court's structure.** He believes he is a transformative
ruler; the duchy is held together by his advisor. He believes his general is wholly
his. Both beliefs are load-bearing for how he plays, and neither survives contact with
what the party may learn. Do not have him behave like a man who suspects.
