## Overview

Duskwall Castle's guard — the men and women who hold the gates, the wards and the
curtain wall. Standard issue is raven-motif outer gear.

A guard roster is public by nature: they wear the uniform in daylight and stand where
everyone can see them. Only Cael is individually authored; the rest of the watch is
unnamed and can be generated as scenes require.

## Two of Those Posts Are Not Held by Guards

**The side-entrance posts are held by Setland operatives**, not by members of this
institution. Two of the [trapped expedition's]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/agents_of_setland.md) vault contingent
killed the Aestrum guards stationed there, took their uniforms, and have occupied those
posts every day since — Lion-crest tunics worn underneath the raven gear.

**They are not members of the Aestrum Guard and must never be run as though they were.**
They take no orders from Cael or anyone above him, they have no loyalty to Aestrum, and
they carry a decade-old Setland assassination order. Their file is
[agents_of_setland.md]({{PLUGIN_ROOT}}/factions/chapter_1/agents_of_setland.md); this entry exists so that nobody reads a
uniform as a roster.

This is the same trap as the Shrikes' — whose file already warns that the vault-entrance
operatives are not Shrikes either. **Two different factions have now been mistaken for
these same two men**, which is a fair measure of how well the disguise works.

## DM Notes — A Hook Worth Deciding

Cael's whole characterization is that **he knows every face that belongs inside the
curtain wall**, and how people move when they are lost, lying, or simply having a bad
day. He is precisely the man who ought to have noticed two strangers on the side
entrance.

He has not. The question the campaign has never answered is *why*, and the two
candidates play very differently:

- **The impostors took those posts on the day the cycle began** — the same day Cael's
  snapshot was taken. If they were already in place when it fired, they have been
  inside his sense of "belongs" from the first moment, and he cannot notice a wrongness
  that his baseline includes.
- **Or he can still notice**, and simply never has, because nothing has yet made him
  look — in which case a party who gives him a reason is handing him the thread.

The first makes him structurally blind and the impostors safe; the second makes him a
loaded weapon pointed at them. **Not settled here** — it needs a DM ruling, and it
determines whether Cael is an obstacle or the best ally a party could have inside that
castle.
