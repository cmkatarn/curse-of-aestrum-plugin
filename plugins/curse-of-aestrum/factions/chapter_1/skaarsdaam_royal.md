## Overview

The ducal house of Skaarsdaam: Duke Amblecrown and his daughter Miri, Chosen of
Selûne and central figure in the Amber Crown Prophecy. A house of two, and one of
them has been missing for ten years.

## What Each Is Hiding From the Other

Neither secret is a membership, but they rhyme, and they point at the same foreign
power:

- **Amblecrown sent her.** The mission was his instruction — secure Cormyr's military
  backing as a two-front deterrent against Setland — so he knows perfectly well that
  she went to negotiate. What he does not know is **what she committed him to**: an
  *unauthorized conditional promise* made on his behalf, beyond her brief, never
  confirmed back to him. His eastern backing depends on a pact he does not know the
  terms of, or that it exists.
- **Amblecrown has since entered his own quiet negotiations with Cormyr** — trading
  rights, port access, tariff arrangements that are not constitutionally his to
  promise without the throne, plus a requested pledge of military backing. If it
  surfaced it would read as inviting foreign military intervention.

**Neither knows the other has been making promises to Cormyr.** If both surfaced
together it would be devastating, and the shape of it is almost funny: a father who
emptied his treasury searching for a daughter, while both of them separately
mortgaged the duchy to the same kingdom.

## Open Slots — To Develop in Play

- **[relation]** **There is no Duchess.** No wife of the Duke and no mother for Miri
  appears anywhere in canon — not in his entry, not in her sheet, not in the timeline.
  Miri's grief in the overlay is entirely for her father: she "longs to see his face
  when he learns she is alive," and no second parent is ever named. Whether the mother
  is **dead, absent, estranged, or simply never written** is unestablished, and it is
  a conspicuous hole in a house this small — a ten-year disappearance with only one
  parent to grieve it.
- **[relation]** Whether the house has anyone else at all — seneschal, cousins, a line
  of succession if Miri does not return. Nothing establishes one.

## DM Notes

**The Duke is playing weakened and everyone can see it.** Malak's own assessment lists
him as "sentimental, weakened by the missing daughter," and Erethezra's network holds
working intelligence on the treasury he has drained on covert searches. His grief is
public knowledge and is being priced in by his rivals.

**Miri is returning to Skaarsdaam regardless of where a party goes** (chapter 2), which
makes this house the destination of her arc rather than its backdrop. What Shar has
prepared for her there is to be developed.
